The night the checks fought back

This page's first draft said the ship floats (+6.4 t at the declared factor). Three adversarial reviewers were set on the physics before any page quoted it, and they confirmed eleven bugs — a missing divisor in the general-instability law, a shear stiffness built on the wrong leg of a triangle, a brace pitch the drawn fan cannot produce, caps that escaped two of their duties. Fixed, the ship sinks on the harsh basis. Kept fixed, three things came back the honest way:

  • The ring-plane X-webs — a member class no drawing had, without which the two walls cannot act as one deep ring. Now drawn, priced, gated.
  • The spokes — the fallback the record had already licensed for exactly this finding: pretensioned tension cords across the void, a Winkler foundation strongest at precisely the low-order ovalization modes the sandwich pays most for. t of cord doing work that compression iron priced at ten times that.
  • The verdict gates — FLOATS is only printed when the structure also stands; a failing check now fails the sentence, not just a row in a table.

What would have to be true

  • The GI knockdown lands near frame practice. SHIP-2's test campaign; the single biggest lever on the ship — worth roughly a hundred tonnes of verdict.
  • The chord coupons land above the working estimate. The second lever; decides the world inside whichever knockdown holds.
  • The spokes hold their premise: pretension without creep-walk (Dyneema-class cord at near-zero mean load, or a stiffer fibre), terminations at -scale part counts.
  • The drape licenses the two-way film credit — the model prices the film one-way until it does.
  • η holds at % at ship joint counts, and the Ti fitting masses price at their estimates.
