# Stage B cell and joint: next-decision evidence map

Status: 2026-10-10. Method plan only; every prospective observation is UNKNOWN.
This map supplies no new calculation, test result, allowable or qualification.
The existing [float ledger](../../../docs/FLOAT-LEDGER.md) and
[vacuum-cell analysis](../../analysis/vacuum-cell.md) retain their published
failures and assumptions. Private close-out results remain outside this repository.

## Decision order

First establish a complete, consistently defined cell mass account. In parallel
on paper, specify the complete Alternative B split connection and how it could
be installed between fixed nodes. Then identify which structural questions a
beam representation can answer and which require shell, film or joint evidence.
Actual material and connection observations would replace assumptions only
within their measured scope. This order authorizes documentation, not fabrication
or an experiment. A smaller connector alone supplies no demonstrated cell-level
mass closure.

The machine-readable [gap map](EVIDENCE-GAPS.json) records observation identifiers,
quantities, units, methods, invalidation conditions and missing acceptance criteria.
Its null values are missing observations, not zero or an invitation to interpolate.
The [provenance record](PROVENANCE.json) binds the public sources to the starting
revision. The private decision brief and sealed source pins are held in the
worker's private unit evidence; they are not inputs published by this map.

## Complete cell mass account

The [stock-build discussion](../../analysis/vacuum-cell.md#the-stock-build-purchased-pipe-printed-joints)
distinguishes geometry-derived quantities from physical weighing and retains
the published mass shortfall. Keep that disposition; this plan does not replace
it with a more favourable estimate. The prospective E1 account must include
the article's tubes, every node, film, barrier, seams, adhesive, alternative
hardware and allocated neighbour/support structure on one declared boundary.

| Required observation | Prospective method | What would invalidate its use |
|---|---|---|
| Component and complete dry article mass, kg | Record calibrated balance uncertainty, conditioning state, part IDs, bill revision and independently weighed assembly | Unallocated hardware, missing moisture/adhesive, double counting or a different article boundary |
| Enclosed volume, m³ | Declare the sealed boundary and trace its dimensional observation to the same article revision | Sub-cell pitch volume substituted for actual sealed article volume |
| Pressure and leak/outgassing history, Pa and Pa/s | A separately authorized protocol would record temperature, pressure, duration and conditioning; distinguish leak from desorption | Pressure rise alone treated as a separated leak mechanism |

The [E1 plan](../../../docs/VERIFICATION-PLAN.md#e1--what-a-sealed-vacuum-cell-actually-weighs)
names the missing degradation decision. Its proposed allowance is not an adopted
acceptance criterion here. Do not invent a replacement. Component observations
would fill individual mass rows; complete-assembly reconciliation remains a
separate observation. Neither geometrical integration nor a catalogue density
is a physical weighing record.

## Alternative B: complete installation geometry

B is a prospective split connection, not a selected manufacturing design.
Before any installation conclusion, require an identified full node with its
actual stock ends, seats, fasteners, holes, lugs, tolerances and access tools.
Record the initial fixed-node configuration, installation order, moving-part
sweep and final secured configuration. Keep a complete bill tied to that revision;
an isolated primitive is not the complete connection or its allocated mass.

| Required observation | Prospective method | What would invalidate its use |
|---|---|---|
| Signed clearance and engagement, mm | Trace every affected end and assembly pose; include tool, fastener and tolerance envelopes | Testing only convenient representatives, a different stock SKU or an undeclared pose change |
| Solid topology and minimum wall, mm | Inspect the complete toleranced solid and its local minimum sections, with a named resolution limit | Closed mesh flag or median wall used as a minimum-wall certificate |
| Retained assembled state and full bill | Specify retention after tools are withdrawn and identify all load-bearing and locking parts | Friction, perfect contact or omitted hardware silently supplying retention |

This is a requirement map. Clearance, printable-solid conformity and physical
retention are UNKNOWN. A numerical retention example cannot establish full
installation access, manufacturability or bearing/pull-out strength. Preserve
the public analysis's frozen assembly defects and distinguish a future correction
from the original failing geometry.

## Beam, shell and film applicability

A beam idealization requires explicit member identity, section, frame, applied
load line and fixture. A future comparison would identify its reaction and
displacement observations separately from its material law and numerical
completion. Mesh agreement alone does not measure joint fixity or establish an
allowable. Solver failure does not establish physical collapse.

Tube-wall instability, membrane strain and barrier behaviour, contact/bearing,
and printed-node topology need evidence appropriate to those surfaces. Do not
transfer a beam result to shell or film capacity, or turn a prescribed edge load
into an equilibrated skin submodel. Joint-station offsets and true load-line
eccentricity require distinct input contracts. The
[strength-input discussion](../../analysis/vacuum-cell.md#strength-inputs-tension-and-compression-are-different-properties)
retains proxy-property limits; the [load-owner discussion](../../analysis/vacuum-cell.md#which-member-reacts-which-load--and-the-96-that-is-not-a-member-count)
retains the distinction between applied demand and demonstrated capacity.

## Actual material and joint observations

Prospective records must identify stock batch, laminate or print orientation,
conditioning, geometry, apparatus, calibration, loading path, units and
uncertainty. Tensile data are not a measured compressive, bearing or bond-shear
allowable. A material coupon is not an installed joint, and a joint coupon is
not the complete sealed article. Required observations include directional
constitutive response, tube imperfections, connection force/displacement and
moment/rotation, bondline and pull-out behaviour, and time-dependent response.

The [verification plan](../../../docs/VERIFICATION-PLAN.md#the-experiments-specified)
is the existing prospective route. Owner approval, physical execution and an
applicable acceptance decision are separate from this paper. Until those records
exist, all actual material, joint and installation fields remain UNKNOWN.
