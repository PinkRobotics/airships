## Qualifications that change the conclusion

- Coating at 0.20 kg/m² is assumed. AZ-93's nominal thickness and optical data do not supply an installed areal mass or qualify Be adhesion. The historical NASA Z-93 compilation supplies no missing density.
- Retained water is 019's optimistic 0.1 mm scenario. Applying its separate 1 mm case makes every primary account negative even at all four endpoints; the best, R30 ISA actual, is about −594.989 kg.
- The detailed 200°F cure tests are for AF163-2K .060 metal joints, not the target 2U .015 Be/honeycomb assembly. Cure tooling pressure and actual stress-free temperature remain open.
- Substituting rigid-face shear constraints removes the primary floats. Including cure compression in global/crimp demand turns R15 optimistic actual all-four payload into −1.821166 kg. Edge/scarf coincidence removes four of the thirteen primary floats even at all four endpoints.
- The light pad carries only 0.431832 of the 70 kn force. The resized heavy pad and the 30–50 kg sealed-service package remain unqualified hardware bills.
