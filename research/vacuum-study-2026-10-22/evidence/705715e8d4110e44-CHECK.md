## Equilibrium and counts

A great-circle cut gives isotropic membrane force n=pR/2. For the three
bar families at 60 degrees, sum(cos² theta)=3/2, and perpendicular family
spacing is sqrt(3)s/2. Therefore

```
n = (3/2) N / (sqrt(3)s/2)
N = p R s/(2 sqrt(3))
V = 8 pi R²/(sqrt(3)s²) + 2
M = 3V - 6 = 24 pi R²/(sqrt(3)s²)
ends = 2M
```

At s=2.04 m, N=562166.272759906 N,
versus 973700.546721780 N for the superseded
pRs/2 convention. Six-member radial equilibrium and tributary-load arch
balance independently give the same corrected force. The sixfold node's
pressure resultant is 344045.758929 N.
V=350.673464866, M=1046.020394599,
and the mean ends per node are 5.965780131.
These fractional counts are continuum/topological estimates, not a realised
integer mesh with identical sixfold nodes everywhere.

At fixed geometry, yield, Euler, local and Perry SF multiply by sqrt(3);
global and film SF are unchanged. The field named `twoway` in this regression
changes the member force while retaining triangulated counts and stiffness.
It is an old-load regression, not a separately designed square mesh.

## Joint sizing and exact mass closure

For a sleeve or wrap with density rho_j and axial sizing strength sigma_j,
let tau be nominal average interface shear:

```
required axial area = N/sigma_j
bond length l = N/(2 pi r tau)
thin sleeve thickness t_j = N/(2 pi r sigma_j)
m_end = rho_j (N/sigma_j) l = C N²/r
C = rho_j/(2 pi sigma_j tau)
m_joint = 2M m_end
```

With corrected N and radius held, the s² in N² cancels the 1/s² in M:
`m_joint = 4 pi C p² R⁴/(sqrt(3)r)`. It does not become independent of
radius or of a selected joint load factor. In the four spacing checks,
constant-mass residual is below 6e-14 kg.

For the continued ceramic wall, `l=N/(2 pi r tau_b)` and
`m_end=rho_SiC A l`. Its coefficient `rho_SiC/(2 pi tau_b)` is
1.97352129434e-05
**kg/(N·m)**, equivalently s²/m². The owner's `1/N` label is dimensionally
incorrect although the numerical coefficient is correct. With A fixed,
total braze-extension mass scales as 1/s. Re-closing changes A.

Let B=lift-film-systems-0.02V. For percentage joints,
`m_tube=B/1.05`. For sleeves, subtract the independently sized joint mass,
then `A=(B-m_joint)/(rho_SiC M s)`. For the socket, solve the coupled mass
law exactly: `A=B/[rho_SiC M(s+2l)]`. This explains 127.920063164 kg for
the held original wall versus 130.611002768 kg
for the re-closed thicker wall; those are different conditions, not a mismatch.

## Shell scores and searches

```
r_i = sqrt(r²-A/pi); wall = r-r_i; I = pi(r⁴-r_i⁴)/4
stress = N/A
SF_yield = 3900e6/stress
sigma_e = pi² E I/(A s²); SF_euler = sigma_e/stress
SF_local = 0.6 E wall/(r stress)
eta = (s/1000)r/(I/A)
Perry: sigma²-[sigma_y+(1+eta)sigma_e]sigma+sigma_y sigma_e=0
SF_perry = smaller Perry root/stress
SF_film = 2 sigma_film t_film/(p s)
SF_global = k 4 sqrt(3/2) E sqrt(I A)/(s R² p)
```
