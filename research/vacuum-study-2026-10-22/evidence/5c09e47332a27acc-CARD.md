# MAINLINE-044 — Fatigue and life

**ESTIMATE · QUESTIONABLE / PROJECTION.** Unit `vacuum-mainline044-fatigue-and-life-1020`; order `ord-admiral-rr2-mainline044-dispatch-1019`. No installed-hull life is qualified.

**The hull does not yet have a defensible service life in days.** On the declared spectrum, the original mooring pads are the first identified problem: their underlying core fails the minimum-strength static screen. Even if that issue is waived, the corrected metal plates reach a fatigue-design damage index of one in **about 950 days**. The filed 037b cure/scarf fix does not change those pads. Be-sheet and scarf fatigue remain unqualified, so 950 days is not a lower bound or a prediction of complete-hull failure.

For the daily thermal cycle alone, the inherited 037 screen gives **about 3,700 days** on the weaker of the two principal-direction, minimum-strength fatigue projections. The 037b free-edge thermal model gives **about 18,800 days** after its 200°F cure fix. Those estimates use normalized fatigue data from much denser core. Mean stress, manufacturing state and the shear model matter more than arithmetic precision.

One explicit candidate for ten years of the declared pad spectrum uses **six larger pads totaling 29.09 kg**, plus a **60:1 scarf adding 10.98 kg** at the 028b geometry. Relative to the corrected original six plates, the combined addition is **36.66 kg**. It clears the stated pad fatigue allocation only under the identified projections. It neither demonstrates ten-year scarf/Be life nor floats on the unchanged mass budget.

## 2. A one-year spectrum

**These counts and ordinary-weather ranges are declared scenarios, not measured mission statistics.** Ten years is 3,650 days. The reference assumes residence and ground service at 500 m, flights upward from there, the coated surface, and daily vacuum top-up without venting.

| Load | Annual count | Range used |
|---|---:|---|
| Outer-face thermal | 365 | 271.076–301.569 K; 30.493 K range |
| Inner-face thermal | 365, same physical cycle | 271.475–301.170 K; 29.695 K range, with projected night endpoint |
| Through-core gradient | 365, same cycle | −0.3992 to +0.3992 K; night sign/magnitude is an explicit projection |
| Restricted weather | 365 | ±100 Pa, hence 200 Pa range |
| Altitude | 12 | 500→1,000→500 m; about 5.6 kPa pressure range |
| Small / medium / limit gust excursions | 100,000 / 10,000 / 100 | 25 / 100 / 290.634 Pa increments, on 189.214 Pa flight dynamic pressure |
| Mooring force fluctuations | 100,000 / 10,000 / 1,000 | 0–0.1 / 0.1–0.3 / 0.3–0.6 of 48.259 kN total |
| Docking / handling | 730 | 0–48.259 kN total; two excursions per day |
| Ground vacuum top-up | 365 | First day 27.562 Pa; day 365 8.340 Pa; separate daily records retained |
| Full vent and evacuation | 0 scheduled; commissioning half-cycle | Approximately 95.45 kPa range; alternatives are 12 or 365 full cycles/year |

The night inner-face temperature and gradient are not reconstructible from 037's filed scalars, so they are projected rather than described as reproduced. Through-core and common-temperature effects are combined in the same thermal cycle, not counted as two independent fatigue events.

The limit gust and mooring severities follow the inherited FAA/037 screening loads; the [FAA airship criteria](https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/aircraft/air_cert/design_approvals/airships/aceAirshipDesignCriteria.pdf) do not supply this annual occurrence histogram. The 70 kn alternative is evaluated separately.

**The restrictive pressure envelope is consequential.** The original pressure-only seven-mode screen has just **606.947 Pa** of headroom. Adding the reference weather, flight and limit-gust peaks leaves SF **1.00028** before local thermal/cure effects. A +2 kPa weather excursion gives SF **0.98571**; descent to sea level gives **0.94812**; the 70 kn uniform-pressure screen gives **0.99844**. These are first-excursion screening failures, not accumulated fatigue lives.

### Vacuum maintenance is not a daily full-pressure cycle

034 used a different face/core and seam layout. Reconstructing its law for the actual 028b point gives **2,842.13 m** of seams, **4.0411 mbar L/s** of assumed leakage, and **0.96815 kg** of finite conditioned release inventory. At 284.9 K, pressure rises from 10 to **37.562 Pa** on day one and from 10 to **18.340 Pa** late in the year. These are gas-load projections.

Top-up changes Be membrane stress by about **0.086 MPa** on day one; full venting changes it by about **298.4 MPa**. The release inventory decays once and is not restarted daily. A vent/reconditioning sequence may change that inventory and needs separate evidence. Neither 034's gas-retention days nor its pump package proves a year of structural or electrical autonomy; its daily ground-service premise remains.

## 4. Damage and life

Miner's sum `D=sum(n/N)` is used as a transparent screen. `D=1` is a calculation endpoint, not a reliability statement. Static overload takes precedence. Missing curves produce **unknown damage**, never zero damage. Curve transfer, mean correction and spectrum assumptions remain tagged.

### Daily thermal core cycle

037's **263.037 kPa is a range**, not always a peak. At its ambient stress-free reference the W endpoints are −119.250 and +143.788 kPa: amplitude **131.519 kPa**, mean **12.269 kPa**. At the midpoint the range is unchanged and the mean becomes zero. With the cold endpoint stress-free, the peak is the entire 263.037 kPa and exceeds even typical W strength.

| Model and state | Governing principal-direction thermal result | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| 037 rigid screen, ambient stress-free | **3,697 days L; 3,989 days W**, minimum-average strengths | Two-direction dense-core transfer; approximately ten years with very little fatigue margin |
| Same, typical strengths | 24,198 days L; 57,638 days W | Typical strength is not a lower-bound allowable |
| 037 rigid screen, actual 250°F cure reference | No admissible life | Static residual shear already exceeds strength |
| 037b free edge, 250°F cure reference | No admissible minimum-basis thermal life | Cold W shear 206.845 kPa versus 172.369 kPa minimum average |
| 037b free edge, filed 200°F cure/scarf fix | **18,816 days W**, minimum-average basis | Thermal-only curve interpolation after density/mean-stress transfer |
| Same fixed model, ±0.03 curve reading shift | **13,609–26,016 days** | Sensitivity, not a confidence interval |

In the first row, L governs fatigue even though W governs several static shear comparisons. Off-axis joints and cells are not qualified by those two directions. Occurrence rates of 0.1, 1 and 10 thermal cycles/day scale these thermal-only day counts inversely.

037b changes the physical shear model, not merely the reference temperature: its free-edge daily W range is **51.202 kPa**, amplitude **25.601 kPa**. The 200°F cure gives a **134.601 kPa** thermal mean and minimum-strength peak SF **1.07595**.

This card also evaluates a **new coupled pressure/thermal edge sensitivity**. Uniform vacuum contraction can oppose residual core extension; the common-strain force balance is recorded in METHOD.md. At the 037b fix it gives about **50.73 kPa** daily range near a **3.56 kPa** mean. This is not validated pressure relief. It illustrates why a tested pressure/temperature sequence is required.

### All five elements

| Element | Original point / stated assumptions | 037b fix |
|---|---|---|
| Be | Nominal daily thermal amplitude about 2 MPa; full vent amplitude about 149 MPa. S200F proxy bookkeeping `D_year=0.01112`; qualified SR200 life **unknown** | Essentially unchanged qualification gap |

| 30:1 or 30.34:1 scarf | Applicable S-N absent; no fatigue life assigned | Static scarf margin restored only for 037b's own load combination, not all 037 local thermal and flight loads |

The Be/AF proxy damage numbers allocate the same finite test horizon to every smaller counted excursion. Their nominal 32,822-day arithmetic endpoint is **not a predicted or demonstrated hull life**. A local fatigue concentration factor around 1.76 would already move a full-vent Be cycle above the selected 262 MPa proxy threshold. No such factor has been measured.

For the bond, gross-area stress is particularly incomplete. The ideal bare foil-foot contact area is only about **0.593%** of gross area. Dividing the 200°F cold-edge nominal shear by that fraction gives a **27.0 MPa no-fillet area sensitivity**. Remaining below the cited 1,500 psi lap-runout stress would require at least **1.55% effective contact area**, before peel and edge concentrations. Actual fillets may spread load, but their area and stress field are unmeasured; the gross-area AF proxy cannot establish bond life.

The original six-pad core shear reaches **241.317 kPa**. Relative to minimum W strength, SF is **0.7143** before an additional pressure interaction reservation. The corrected plate calculation gives six plates totaling **3.414 kg**, each 6.323 mm thick, with a nominal 276 MPa peak. Their damage is **0.38427/year** on the declared handling/wind spectrum. The source cyclic-yield value is lower than that peak; therefore even the 950-day plate number requires an elastic-screen qualification.

The coupled edge sensitivity also exposes full-vent sensitivity. At a 250°F stress-free reference, its **core-only** indices are about 90,628 days with top-ups, 4,228 days with monthly full venting, and **146 days** with daily full venting. The last case extrapolates outside the tested fatigue domain and includes a static-invalid vented residual state; it is not a valid survival prediction. At the 037b 200°F fix the corresponding numerical indices are 91,977, 84,385 and 24,616 days. These large differences are model sensitivity, not evidence that the hull is safe for those durations.

**Combined static loads still matter.** Enveloping pressure, cure residual and the inherited local thermal peaks gives scarf SF **0.9875** at the original 250°F point and **0.9950** at the 037b fix before the added flight/weather load. With that load the values become **0.9817 / 0.9891**. These conservative combinations are not time-correlated local solutions, but they preclude claiming that the small 037b scarf change already closes all load cases.

These are explicit candidate changes. A demonstrated ten-year **hull** still requires closing the Be, scarf, local buckling, environment and manufactured-panel data gaps.

The pad calculation includes a deliberately conservative scalar pressure reservation in core capacity. Its life restoration depends on extrapolating low-amplitude core cycles beyond the available test range. There are more than a million pad cycles in the proposed decade. Merely assigning every cycle the longest published test duration cannot demonstrate that decade, regardless of further stress reduction.

The denser-core comparison includes the change in modulus and residual shear; it does not hold demand fixed while increasing strength. The 1.6 pcf minimum-strength case is consequently less favorable than a strength-only comparison suggests.

There is also a **cure-sequence issue**: the face scarf is HexBond312, whose cited cure is 120°C. A subsequent whole-panel scarf cure could invalidate an assumed 200°F face/core stress-free temperature. The 037b low-temperature benefit needs an evidenced manufacturing sequence and measured residual state, not just a different oven setting.

### Inspection and repair

A 90-day metal-pad replacement thought experiment consumes approximately **0.0948** of the original plate fatigue index per interval. Forty replacements over a decade consume about **136.56 kg of plate spares**, offboard; installed plate mass stays 3.414 kg. This does **not** repair the static-invalid underlying core or reset face/scarf fatigue.

A one-square-metre replacement sandwich has a bare face/core/two-film inventory of **3.459 kg**, before patch overlaps, closeouts, sealant and coating. Monitoring and qualified repair masses remain unknown. No inspection interval is credited as restoring ten years without a detectable-defect size, crack/debond growth law and demonstrated repair retention. The core experiments report little stiffness change until late in life, so stiffness alone is not a supported early-warning strategy.

## 6. Evidence, verification and disposition

`NFI.json` records the open qualification items with concrete evidence needed to close each; the 13 inherited 037b items remain attached. They do not block this completed estimate. No vendor contact or cross-seat contact was made.

The independent calculation passes **21 numerical checks and five physical/allocation assertions**. It reproduces the inherited geometry, 037 shear range, 037b edge shear and corrected pad mass; checks finite release and unit conversions; and balances nine pressure/temperature membrane states. Source-table values and the plotted curves were visually checked. `PIN-VALIDATION.json` records the separate input/source hash audit.

Machine-readable spectrum, cycle-by-cycle stresses/damage, low-stress extrapolation flags, static envelopes, mass options, source data and the executable calculation accompany this card. The source-data figure is fatigue-evidence.png [source link unavailable in this public projection]; [METHOD.md](1e28d49f84af551f-METHOD.md) states the equations and limitations.

**Disposition:** The estimate is complete. The original point and the small 037b fix do not establish ten years of installed service. Pads and combined-load scarf margins need changes; a conditional +36.66 kg stress-reduction candidate is quantified, with the remaining material, assembly and mass-budget gaps explicit. The brief names no successor: file once and stop.
