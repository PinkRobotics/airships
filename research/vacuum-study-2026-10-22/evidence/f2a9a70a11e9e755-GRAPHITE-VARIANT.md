# Graphite-film variant, reconciled with 071

Unit vacuum-mainline069-design-point-1020. QUESTIONABLE. PROJECTION. No qualified hull safety factor.

069 filed the gamma-held-0.20 graphite cases from 070. No case floats. At R 30 optimistic the face is 2.534 mm, the core 158.3 mm, 969 GPa and 2250 kg/m3, payload −58888 kg. The factor still needed there is 1.659, to 1357 kg/m3. The family witness is 1.693, set by R 20 optimistic. Vc at R 30 is 3.36 and 3.45, outside the CR-1457 support for holding gamma at 0.20.

071 (rpt-ref-9851d55bdb5d2ec1f91b) is the thermal result this variant carries. The filed 069 page is left as pinned. This note and claim-record delta DELTA-069-GRAPHITE-071 reconcile it.

Above Vc 2, CR-1457 governs by shear crimping and sets the knockdown to 1. 070's gamma = 1 branch matches that handbook rule, with a named limit: not this film on CR-III, and not a closed 20 to 30 m sphere. DOI 10.2514/3.5568 and DOI 10.2514/3.1932 do not govern that step.

On the aluminum core every gamma-step case is thermally infeasible. The signed expansion 071 used is the HOPG catalogue value, −1.0e-6 per K, a different product from the film. The interface coefficient becomes 24.6e-6. At 070's geometry the thermal margin is 0.536. Face density cannot release it. A core expansion at or below 12.2e-6 per K would release it at this stiffness. The 25 psi screen would have to rise by 1.866, to 0.322 MPa, which is above the core's own W shear. Cure temperature is not in the interface stress.

At the largest mismatch that is just feasible, R 20 optimistic still needs a face-density factor of 3.467 at concept and 3.137 at TRL 7, to 649 and 717 kg/m3. That core thickness is the search floor. Those factors are worse than 070's gamma-step factors 1.248 and 1.126.

On CR-III the graphite variant is infeasible. Its condition is a core with matched expansion. 072 tests real cores of that kind.
