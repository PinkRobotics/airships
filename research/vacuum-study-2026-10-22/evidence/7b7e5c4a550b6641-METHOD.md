## Membrane load and two-group buckling

Use h=tf+tc, Af=Ef tf/(1−nu_f²), Al=(3sqrt(3)/4)El A_member/a.
The smeared lattice has Q12=Al/3. Total A11=2Af+Al, A12=2nu_f Af+Al/3 and Ebar=A11−A12²/A11.

N=pR/2; epsilon=N/(A11+A12); lattice load share fr=(4Al/3)/(A11+A12). Face membrane stress is Ef epsilon/(1−nu_f), and mechanical member force El epsilon A_member.

The outer face is at z=h/2. The inner group's extensional stiffness is Ai=Af+Al, with centroid
zi=[−Af h/2−Al(h/2+tf/2+zc)]/Ai.
Write ell=h/2−zi.

D_internal=(3sqrt(3)/4)El I/a + Af Al/(Af+Al)(tf/2+zc)².

D_separation=Af Ai/(Af+Ai) ell²; S_core=Gc ell²/tc.

For x=k², Ncr(x)=D_internal x + D_separation x/(1+D_separation x/S_core) + Ebar/(R²x).

The independent solver finds the zero of the derivative by solving the monotonic equation

D_internal x² + D_separation[x/(1+D_separation x/S_core)]² = Ebar/R².

The restricted solution uses x≤(pi/a)². This differs algorithmically from a generic bounded wavelength minimizer and gives the same values within the recorded tolerance.

Without lattice, sigma0=Ef h/[R sqrt(1−nu_f²)], crimp=h²Gc/(2tf tc), Vc=sigma0/crimp and Kc=1−Vc/4 below Vc=2, otherwise 1/Vc. This recovers the nominal sandwich rule. The effective thickness reported by the owner is sqrt(12D_total/Ebar).

Bare-sandwich D_skin=Ef tf h²/[2(1−nu_f²)]. The reproduced adequacy parameter is gamma_s=(D_total/D_skin−1)/(3sqrt(3)/4). This is a composite stiffness measure; it is not the isolated member EI ratio.
