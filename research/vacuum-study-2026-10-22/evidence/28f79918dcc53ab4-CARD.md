The 0.82 wrinkle coefficient yields 1.533305491633405. The two cell sizes tie on the least factor.

The implementation uses h=tc+tf, sigma0=E*h/[R*sqrt(1-nu²)], crimp stress=h²*Gc/(2*tf*tc), Vc=sigma0/crimp, and Kc=1-Vc/4 through Vc=2 or 1/Vc above it. Face stress demand is pR/(4tf). Global capacity is gamma*Kc*sigma0 divided by demand. Honeycomb wrinkling is b*0.33*sqrt(E*Ec*tf/tc) divided by demand, where b³+b=1. Face compression and core crush use sigma/demand and strength/(p/2). These match the inherited calculation.

The aluminium point needs kd=0.324125581628885 for **global buckling alone** to reach SF=1. At fixed geometry wrinkling remains 0.617061966145151 and face compression remains 0.771940448044521. Thus no change in kd alone makes every mode pass. Even using the theoretical wrinkle coefficient leaves face compression below 1.
