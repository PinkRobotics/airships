# Method and reproduction

This is an addendum to CHECK050 under `ord-admiral-rr2-check050-codex-089a-1020`. It consumes the equations in `inputs/GAS-BOUND.md` and `inputs/CONVECTION.md`, supplied by the owner in response to two queued clarification requests. All five attached artifact hashes were verified. Two original non-code input files were copied from the sealed checker's owner-data snapshot after their original source pins were reverified.

`check_conventions.py` is a new independent implementation. It imports the Python standard library and NumPy only. It reads the existing face/core geometries from `MASS-CURVES.json`, computes every one of the 160 selected gas candidates, and chooses the pressure maximum separately for each radius. It also independently calculates the maximum mass to establish whether those selections coincide. The comparison answers in `SUPPLEMENTAL-RESULTS.json` and `SCALAR-REPLAY.json` are consumed only after all independent scalar calculations have been made. No owner executable file was read, copied, imported or executed.

The script separately calculates five convection cases from the supplied ambient inputs. It obtains the water dew point by 60 bisections, evaluates the heater's positive affine deficit analytically, and evaluates its 128-point Gauss-Legendre quadrature. It performs no geometry optimization, weather-map computation or transient integration. Those remain covered by the original CHECK050.

The gas comparison checks all four filed physical quantities and selected masks. Absolute comparison tolerances are 1e-9 in each gas quantity's stated unit, 1e-8 for the convection scalar comparisons, and 1e-10 for the heater comparisons. Actual differences are recorded, including zero differences for all twenty compared gas quantities. No tolerance is used to establish physical qualification.
