# MAINLINE-031 — Altitude as the hot-day margin

ESTIMATE · QUESTIONABLE inputs and transfers · PROJECTION atmosphere, fabrication and named optimisms · Not certification.

**Altitude can replace part of the climate mass allowance, conditional on an explicit altitude range.** For the optimistic 019 weather set and an illustrative 100m centre-altitude floor, P2 needs 55.34kg climate headroom and nominal SF 1.036553, instead of 231.74kg climate headroom at the original 500m floor. The least named set remains gamma, wrinkling and panel size; its complete bookkeeping reserve is 616.43kg (12.607% of nominal lift), versus 021b's 775.73kg. Covering both 019 weather sets at the same 100m floor requires 441.77kg climate headroom, nominal SF 1.022889, and a fourth named optimism: face strength. Its total reserve is 967.96kg (19.797%).

A hull spending all nominal lift has C=0 and floats at 500m on ISA. On the +10K-only day it floats at 130.743m, a 369.257m descent, with 4.352464% more pressure. With the optimistic low pressure and humidity included, the mathematical root is −23.190m; the conservative hot/low/humid root is −1015.877m. Those roots lie below sea-level ground and cannot provide clearance at that assumed site. The rough heat-only estimate was sound; the complete weather requirement changes the conclusion.

The atmospheric model is a hydrostatic 6.5K/km lapse with constant vapour mole fraction e/p, anchored to each 019 condition at 500 geopotential metres. It is a stated PROJECTION. A single-level temperature, pressure and humidity does not determine a float altitude. See [METHOD.md](f7c1f9ffb20d9657-METHOD.md) for equations, objective, alternatives and the inherited structural equations. The 100m floor is an illustrative requirements option, not an approval.

**Requirement to adopt, if this trade is wanted.** One possible requirement is: “A passive R10m hull, with the specified carried inventory and no ballast or thrust, may float at about 617.1m on ISA and no lower than 100m centre altitude on the optimistic 019 weather envelope over sea-level ground.” For both 019 weather sets the analogous ISA equilibrium is about 1464.5m, not 500m. The organisation must choose the weather envelope, terrain-relative floor, ceiling, clearance and altitude-control requirement. A strict 500m ISA free-float condition fixes C=0 and is incompatible with those ground-clearance requirements in the chosen profiles.

The 027 optimistic climate-plus-internal-gas lower bound of 5.739617% is therefore specific to the old 500m-every-day lift requirement. At the illustrative optimistic 100m floor, those two rows total 2.131845%. This does not remove water, contamination, films, joints or systems. The complete least-set reserve remains 12.607%.
