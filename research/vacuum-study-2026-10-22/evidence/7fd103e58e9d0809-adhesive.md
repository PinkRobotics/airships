## AF 163-2U (3M datasheet, Nov 2009, local copy)
- Cure: 250-300 F (121-149 C), 60 min; 225-250 F, 90 min. 3M's 2U flatwise data use 235 F, 90 min, 35 psig. Lowest cure stated: 200 F, up to 6 h (strength still rising from 2 h to 6 h). Pressure 20-80 psi; rise 1-20 F/min; hold pressure below 150 F.
- Gel time at 250 F: 20.5 min.
- Dry Tg 108 C, wet 82 C (DMA, cured 60 min at 250 F). Cure exceeds Tg by 13 K.
- CTE 90e-6 /C (-30 to 50 C). Film modulus 1.6e5 psi at 75 F, 2.3e5 psi at -67 F.
- Flatwise tensile 1408 psi at 75 F, 1858 psi at -67 F; lap shear 6377 psi. Aluminium skins and core only.

## Stress-free temperature
- Adams and da Silva 2006 (abstract only): the cure temperature for a high-temperature adhesive; the Tg for a low-Tg adhesive taken through a higher cure.
- Porto thesis 2005: stress relieves between gel and vitrification, locks in from vitrification. CFRP laminate measured about 236 C against 180 C cure (author: inconclusive).
- JPL 2026 slides: elevated cure sets it; ambient cure minimises mismatch.
- Best rule: cure temperature, or Tg if held above Tg. For AF 163-2U that is 381-394 K, a drop of 79-123 K to 271-302 K (PROJECTION).
- No measured relaxed fraction found.

## Room- and low-temperature cure

- 3M 2216 B/A: 7 days at 23 C, T-peel 25 lb/in. No lap shear or Tg.
- Cure near mid-swing: no source. Crooks 2003 (abstract): thermal residual strain fell 26.5 % (FM 73M) and 7.4 % (EA 9696) with lower cure.
