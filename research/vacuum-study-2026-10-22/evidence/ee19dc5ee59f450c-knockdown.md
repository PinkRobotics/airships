Knockdown sourcing, card MAINLINE-017b (all values QUESTIONABLE; detail in knockdown.json)

SPHERES. Best tested complete sphere: 0.86 of classical (Carlson, Sendelbeck, Hoff 1966; electroformed NICKEL, R/t 1570-2120; best shell diameter variation 0.006 in, thickness about +/-5 percent). Machined aluminium shallow caps reached up to 0.9 (Krenzke 1963, second-hand through Lee 2016; the Krenzke page itself was blocked). SP-8032 (Aug 1969) design lower bound: 0.14 + 3.2/lambda^2, tending to 0.14 for deep shells. Hutchinson 2016: the 20 percent rule is "still the governing design rule"; imperfection plateau 15-20 percent. Lee 2016 elastomer near-perfect hemispheres: 0.69 +/- 0.06. Numerical 2024 (Ubamanyu): clamped near-perfect hemisphere 0.8; the authors note a scarcity of any data above 0.9. No new test ratio above 0.86 found; Wagner/Huhne 2018-21 domes read only as abstracts ("much higher KDFs", no numbers).

SANDWICH DOMES. CR-1457 (1969) gamma_d = 0.20 hemispheres, 0.35 ellipsoids, from only 2 hemisphere and 6 ellipsoid S-II scale-bulkhead points (Gonzalez and Patton 1964); the report calls the basis "meager". SP-8032 gives no sandwich-dome rule. Newer sandwich tests are cylinders only: CTA8.1 (2018) test/perfect 0.87 against SP-8007 0.61; CTA8.2B 0.721 against 0.562.

CYLINDERS. SP-8007 formula 1 - 0.901(1 - e^-phi) is unchanged in Rev 2 (Dec 2020); Rev 2 says 70-90 percent is "not uncommon" in modern tests. The design rule has not moved in 50 years; test results and test-validated analysis (loads predicted within 5-10 percent) have.

WRINKLING. Classical 0.82 (CR-1457 Eq. 2.2-8, K_delta = 0; 0.86 Bartelds-Mayers). Design 0.33 (Eq. 2.2-11, 2.2-14, "knock-down factor of approximately 0.4") is a lower bound to Forest Products Laboratory data. The waviness it assumes is not stated. Measured: Norris-Boller-Voss honeycomb struts 0.82; Jenkinson-Kuenzi 0.125; Harris-Crisman 0.14-0.38; Bansemir-Pfeifer 0.33-0.42 (CR-1999-208994 Table 3).

SHAPE. 1966: 0.006 in on a roughly 8.5 in sphere. 2006: 16-17 um RMS on 12 m ALMA reflectors. 2018: +/-0.1 in on an 8 ft sandwich cylinder. No like-for-like trend found.

LATTICE. Kato and Niho 2001: alpha_0 = 0.65 (xi at least 4.2), no initial imperfection. Wright 1965 and JGJ 7-2010 were NOT readable; the 0.4 and 0.2 are unconfirmed.

STRAIGHTNESS. Steel L/1000 (AISC Code 2005). CFRP tubes 1 mm/m on retail sheets (6-22 mm OD). SiC and EN 1090 values not found.

Trend over 30 years: the printed design numbers (0.14, 0.20, 0.33, 0.65) did not move; best tests moved little; only cylinder tests and analysis moved.
