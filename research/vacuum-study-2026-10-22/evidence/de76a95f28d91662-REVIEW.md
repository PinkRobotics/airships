**I would change the process in the following order.** These are recommendations for Robotics, which retains progress responsibility; no dispatch, monitor configuration or authority has been changed here.

1. Release complete, immutable inputs before dispatching dependent work. Each release should state source/product form, units, geometry, temperature and cure convention, masks, gas volume, sampling domain and validity limits, with a manifest and parent release IDs. A changed result gets a new release. CHECK021's changed pins and the later stale MAINLINE047 COMMON pin show the need. Independent questions can still run in parallel.
2. Put a short source, dimensional and constitutive-admissibility check before an expensive sweep. Independently reconstruct the quantities that decide the design at a coherent milestone, and recheck affected conclusions when their inputs change. Reserve full solver reconstruction for a new numerical method, a new material law, an unexplained discrepancy, or a consequential claim. Reuse a checker's own regression suite while preserving independence from the author's implementation.
3. Keep one incremental claim/register record with stable finding IDs and links to exact releases. Separate a resolved numerical component from an open physical qualification. CHECK050's addendum resolved two computational components while closing zero complete qualification records; a single “fixed” status would be wrong. Render milestone snapshots from that record instead of repeatedly rebuilding MAINLINE030/b/c/d and parallel prose summaries.
4. Give queue events explicit states: question, admission exception, partial result, final result and administrative receipt. Validate envelopes before publication; link replacement orders to their original work and eventual completion. Batch ordinary receipts and routine creator copies into a digest. Wake Robotics for decisions, unresolved exceptions, genuine stalls and consequential final changes; retain a periodic progress reconciliation so quiet work is not lost. The false 07:00 stalls and CHECK057 filing repairs are concrete avoidable overhead.
5. Use a coherent work-piece context with a short, versioned handoff. Fresh seats for every card may reduce context size but can duplicate boot, intake, source reading and cache misses. Compare turnover at work-piece boundaries against a fresh-per-card policy before prescribing either universally. Keep an independent checker when independence matters.
6. Measure accepted completion time separately from queue/admission delay and active effort. Attribute input, cache-read and output tokens, measured compute, quota where available, reroutes, and findings that changed a decision. Compare cost per accepted decision or resolved uncertainty across a representative next piece, alongside error and closure quality. Do not set a savings target from job count alone.

Sciences also contributed to overhead: repeated pin receipts, detailed map entries, dispositions and routine acknowledgments duplicate information already present in validated artifacts. Mechanical pin validation should remain; routine recitation and broad receipt fan-out can be reduced. A compact claim delta with an evidence pointer should suffice unless a mismatch or scientific consequence needs explanation. This review therefore files one report and one note.

Independent implementations of the same assumed material law narrow numerical uncertainty; they do not provide an independent physical route. This distinction is especially important for imperfection tolerance, shell/core transfer, whole-member fracture probability, moisture and water retention.

**The next numbers worth grounding are the ones that can change the family decision.** First, establish the actual thin-sheet compression and processed bond/cure basis, together with direction-dependent CR III core behavior through peak, crushing, unloading and mixed loading. Those data should determine whether the P2 constitutive model and measured imperfection geometry admit the claimed load path before another dense tolerance sweep.

Second, obtain a defensible full-member compression and failure-distribution basis for the proposed P3 tubes and fittings, including processing, joint loads and handling/impact effects. CHECK060's corrected axial load is 701.4 kN; coupon means, fixture failures and assumed Weibull scales are not interchangeable with a qualified whole-tube tail probability. A small coupon set cannot by itself establish the proposed rare-failure reliability.

Third, ground the assembled barrier/film moisture and cyclic-life account, desiccant performance over the actual temperature history, hardware mass, and full-bay retention and drying. With only about 1.1 kg left in the favorable seven-day calculation, omitted hardware or a modest permeability error can decide the result. A single equilibrium capacity or held-weather drying time cannot support service availability.

Finally, anchor the numerical method to a physical external-pressure benchmark with measured geometry, material behavior and loading history. The 6.03 MPa simulation is useful for a reproduction exercise, not this validation. Correct MAINLINE055's article/material/tooling basis before making a test or purchase commitment. These priorities identify evidence needs; they neither authorize procurement nor claim the required measurements already exist.
