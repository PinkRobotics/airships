**MAINLINE-036 — Reproduction and calculation method**

The input artifacts are copied from hash-pinned 027 and 028b reports and the dispatched brief. `intake/PINS.json` records original paths, hashes, byte counts and adopted copies. No predecessor executable is imported or run. `prepare_inputs.py` freezes numerical data; `calculate.py` implements mass and mode equations independently. All results describe an analytical screen with unresolved material and installed-hull qualification.

The shell has equal faces of finished thickness tf and a core of thickness tc. Radius is R, area A=4πR², face density ρf=1850 kg/m³, core density ρc, and core mass fraction f. Structural mass S gives

`tf=(1−f)S/(2Aρf)` and `tc=fS/(Aρc)`.

Each face carries `N=pR/4` in each tangent direction, giving `stress=pR/(4tf)`. R10 uses p=95,460.83791390515 Pa; R20 uses 1.01 times that pressure, as explicitly required by 027's optimistic envelope. No internal-pressure load credit is taken.

For R10, the budget is

`S + max(0.05S, Q) + 411.2989440716474 + 100 = 4889.442150186961 kg`.

The fixed reserve contains the inherited water, contamination, weather and internal-gas provisions. There is no new five-percent flotation reserve. A low physical joining mass does not release the retained joint allowance.

For the 30:1 scarf, overlap is `30tf` and coincident seam length is

`L=A[1/(0.6096−30tf)+1/(1.8288−30tf)]`.

For the ideal weld, omit overlap. Physical joining mass is

`Q=2A(μbond+μprimer)+L[0.25tc+0.002+2tf√901×0.070]`

with the final term present only for a scarf. Film/primer quantities apply to both interfaces; the 0.002 kg/m seal allowance already covers both skins. Catalog AF015 is 0.07323641454574575 kg/m² per interface; the laboratory film assumption is 0.008 kg/m². A monotonic 48-step bisection closes mass to substantially better precision than material inputs justify.

R20 uses 027's coupled optimistic mass law. With `λ=1/0.6096+1/1.8288`, splice width w=0.06 m, film μ=0.1464728290914915 kg/m², gap g=0.001 m, seal area Ab=1e−5 m² and splice/paste density 1100 kg/m³:

`Q=Aλ[2ρf tf w+2μw+1100g tc+1100Ab]+2Aμ=u(f)S+v`.

The available mass after the 100 kg system floor and other reserves is H. If `Q(H/1.05)≤0.05H/1.05`, then `S=H/1.05`; otherwise `S=(H−v)/(1+u)`. Other reserves are recomputed from 027's p/T/humidity/internal gas/water/contamination equations, yielding 2,767.736727998812 kg at R20. Lift is 39,115.53720149569 kg. The joined-section screen is `0.9Fcy/stress`; all other properties and joining mass are kept independent of Fcy.

The six continuum screens and one joint screen are:

`h=tf+tc`

`σ0=E h/[R√(1−ν²)]`

`σcrimp=h²Gc/(2tf tc)`

`Vc=σ0/σcrimp`

`Kc=1−Vc/4` for `Vc≤2`, otherwise `1/Vc`

`SFglobal=γKcσ0/stress`

`SFface=Fcy/stress`

`SFcrimp=σcrimp/stress`

`SFcrush=core_crush/(p/2)`

`SFwrinkle=b k√(E Ec tf/tc)/stress`, where `b³+b=1`, `b=0.6823278038280194`

`SFdimple=E/(1−ν²) [source link unavailable in this public projection]²/stress`

`SFscarf=[10 MPa × 901/30]/stress`; or `SFjoint=ηFcy/stress` for the ideal weld / R20 splice.

The scarf capacity is an inherited owner assumption, not a tested material property. Weld η=1 is also an assumption. The biaxial corrections already present in 028b/027 wrinkling and dimpling are retained; they do not establish a biaxial material yield criterion.

Three mechanical cases are kept distinct:

- **Held 028b coefficients:** E=290 GPa, ν=0.0752, the filed route-specific constant γ and k. This reproduces the original required geometry; strength is then varied and geometry re-optimized without raising those coefficients.
- **Full projected envelope:** γ=0.2+0.4min(Vc,2), k=0.82. R10 has separate E=290 and 293.6 GPa calculations. R20 reproduces E=293.6 GPa, ν=0.33 and separately evaluates ν=0.0752, plus handbook E=293.02718496 GPa, ν=0.10.
- **Today screen:** k=0.33, E=290 GPa, γ=0.2 below Vc=2 and 1 at/above the inherited branch. Even arbitrarily high strength does not make this envelope float.

Every Fcy is an assumed **effective equibiaxial** strength for these calculations. A uniaxial source does not automatically provide that property. S-200-F and HIP-preform numbers are sensitivities with all other sheet-model inputs held; no product-form equivalence is asserted.

For each Fcy, the maximum minimum-mode SF is searched over f and all 47 inherited R10 core rows or 37 inherited R20 aluminium rows. A 1,601-point grid finds every resolved local peak, refined with bounded scalar minimization. The Vc=2 event is explicitly included for the discontinuous “today” model. All returned optimum points include masses, tf/tc, individual modes, γ, Vc and Kc.

The minimum required Fcy is computed by a different optimization: first find the feasible set of all strength-independent modes, then minimize `stress/η` on that set. Individual mode crossings are root-solved from a 4,001-point grid. This retains isolated feasible points where held global and wrinkling screens both meet one, which a simple feasible-grid search could miss. The scarf screen is independent of Fcy and remains a constraint. For R20, η=0.9 moves the joint condition into required strength. Values 0.01% above/below each threshold are checked with the max-min optimizer.

Five primary thresholds receive separate 20,001-point all-core scans. The checks also reproduce the held predecessor geometries, verify global membrane force equilibrium, mass residuals, and the zero-core R10 bound:

`Smax=(lift−fixed reserve−100)/1.05`, `tfmax=Smax/(2Aρf)`.

That deliberately favorable bound gives SF255=0.9582172955. It cannot certify an unknown material; it only excludes the specified 255 MPa sensitivity.

For the biaxial diagnostic only, NASA-TM-4397 Table 8 uses

`F1σ1+F2σ2+F11σ1²+F22σ2²+F12σ1σ2=1`

with no extra factor of two on the printed F12 term. Setting σ1=σ2=−s gives

`(3.521e−4+2.889e−4−2.726e−4)s² + (2.394e−3+7.800e−4)s = 1`

in ksi units. The positive root is 47.9702669 ksi = 330.7433475 MPa. Axis intercepts reproduce the table's 50 and 57.5 ksi compression entries to its rounding precision. This is **unqualified extrapolation**: the fitted “yield” data include substituted biaxial bending/failure data, lack target-gauge statistics, and do not qualify the compression quadrant.

Thickness scenarios use `stress_new=stress_old tf/(tf−loss)`. Catalog minus tolerances and additional post-processing removals are separate cases; they are never summed by default. Existing etched stock does not automatically require another etch. These rows isolate membrane consequences and do not revalidate all shell modes after a dimensional change.

Source handling: maker PDFs and public NASA/DOE reports were downloaded with content hashes; archived primary handbooks were obtained from mirrors and visually checked. Retrieval failures, missing original Fenn tests and uninspected current handbook editions are explicit in `SEARCH-LOG.json`. `EVIDENCE.json` distinguishes reported minima, research values, ultimate failure, wrong-form data and missing fields.

Reproduce from this output directory using Python with NumPy and SciPy: `python3 prepare_inputs.py`, then `python3 calculate.py`, then `python3 verify.py`, then `python3 evidence.py`, then `python3 plot.py`. `fetch_sources.py` and `fetch_additional.py` record public source retrievals. No source retrieval or numerical result authorizes another seat's wake or out-of-band contact.
