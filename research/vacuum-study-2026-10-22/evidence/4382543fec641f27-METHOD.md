# CHECK-033 — independent method

This check reads the filed card, equations and data. It neither reads nor runs
the owner's executable files. All calculations below are implemented in this
check's own Python files. Material inputs remain QUESTIONABLE; geometric,
maintenance, environmental and performance transfers remain PROJECTION.

## Direct arithmetic

For each reported geometry, compute its area, face and core masses directly
from thickness and density. Derive the applied face stress from membrane force,
`sigma = p R/(4 tf)`, and independently form each critical stress. Divide
capacity by that applied stress to recover all six shell modes and the separate
scarf screen. No reported safety factor is fed into another calculation.

Use `h=tf+tc`, `sigma0=E h/[R sqrt(1-nu²)]`,
`sigma_cr=Gc h²/(2 tf tc)`, and `Vc=sigma0/sigma_cr`.
The declared branch law is retained: baseline gamma is .20 below Vc=2 and 1
at/above it. Full gamma is `.20+.40 min(Vc,2)`. The literal handbook case is
separately held at gamma=.20. The wrinkling coefficient uses the independently
solved positive root of `beta³+beta=1`.

Every reported geometry's properties, improvement factors, safety factors,
joining takeoff, reserve rows and total mass closure are recomputed. Gamma/Q
slack removal is also recomputed from the stress required to reach SF=1;
the reported reduced factors are not used as inputs.

At the 8,977 distinct reported per-core mass splits, the independently sized
minimum thickness gives a separate payload calculation. The catalogue's
numeric fields are compared against the frozen 014b data, and all 47 row
names resolve. This checks transfer of those data, not their physical allowables.

## Independent search variable and thickness bounds

Set `y=tc/tf` and `lambda=tf/R`. At a specified core mass fraction f,

    y = 2 rho_face f/[rho_core (1-f)].

Direct stress formulas give bounds on lambda:

    compression: p/(4 strength)
    crimp:       p y/[2 Gc (1+y)²]
    wrinkling:   p/[4 beta Q sqrt(E Ec/y)]
    scarf:       p/[4 tau_scarf (1+r²)/r]       when constrained
    dimpling:    {p cell² (1-nu²)/(4 E R²)}^(1/3).

For the global bound,

    Vc = lambda 2 E y/[Gc (1+y) sqrt(1-nu²)]
    SFglobal = lambda² 4 E (1+y) gamma(Vc) Kc(Vc)/
               [p sqrt(1-nu²)].

Solve this monotone thickness condition with 54 bisection steps, preserving
the upward branch switch. A core whose crush score is below one is infeasible
regardless of thickness. The greatest of the independent thickness bounds
sets the least structural mass at that shape. Dimpling is the only included
shell constraint that breaks this radius similarity at fixed materials,
pressure and improvement usage.

The search scans every one of the 47 supplied cores. It includes a uniform
interior fraction grid and logarithmic tails down to 1e-8 from each endpoint,
then refines every observed local optimum. Endpoint checks use 4,097 interior
fractions. Usage and radius evaluations use 2,049; each solved usage minimum
and radius witness is checked again with 8,193. Ideal bounds use 8,193.
Grid size alone is not a proof of global optimality; the complete search
scope and residuals are retained.

All feasible subset usages are independently found by a zero-payload root
over u in [0,1], reoptimizing geometry and core at each trial. Set selection
first minimizes the count of named improvements, then usage. At the selected
geometry, recompute the necessary gamma and Q separately; they need not use
the same fraction of their available range when scarf shear is active.

Every published radius sample is recomputed. The first negative-to-positive
optimized payload bracket is solved anew; the reported crossing radius is
not used as a root seed. “No radius” claims based on an ideal envelope are
checked by independently maximizing that envelope over the same catalogue.
These are numerical checks of the declared model, not interval certificates.

## Reserve and joining reconstruction

At R=10 m, independently reproduce 019's original optimistic loss rows.
Apply 027's volume law to temperature/pressure, humidity and internal gas,
and its area law to water and contamination. The 100 kg systems floor remains
fixed. Joining is replaced by the specified 028 nominal construction; the
old 020 strap geometry and joint-efficiency scalar are not carried across.

For each new geometry, fixed stock blanks lose `r tf` from each coverage
dimension. Unique seam length is

    4 pi R² [1/(a-r tf) + 1/(b-r tf)].

Both face/core films, both scarf films, core splice, sealing and any specified
primer are charged. Positive coverage is required. Joining is recomputed
at every geometry; its complete mass is not scaled as one frozen areal number.
The 028 nominal reference geometry is independently reproduced.

Compare both declared accounts:

    explicit:       Y=L-100-B-S-J
    retained floor: Y=L-100-B-S-max(J,.05 S).

The second retains unspent joining allowance without charging it twice.
These different accounts explain the two R=30 payload pairs and the two
Q-plus-strength crossing radii.

## Precision and output scope

Direct comparisons use absolute tolerance 1e-6 plus relative tolerance 2e-9,
except required thickness (1e-10 m), required maximum usage (3e-8) and
per-core sized payload (2e-5 kg). Every raw difference is saved.

Independent optimum comparisons use 2e-7 of reference lift plus 1e-5 kg;
individual core comparisons additionally allow 2e-8 relative error.
Usage roots use a 2e-6 comparison tolerance and radius roots 2e-5 m.
These tolerances cover numerical optimization precision, not material
uncertainty. They are stated before reviewing the search results.

CSV fields are checked against independent direct arithmetic. Rounded card
tables use half the last displayed unit plus 1e-8. The radius table is checked
against the independent search. Measured timestamps, library versions and
source identifiers are metadata, not scientific quantities to re-derive.

All frozen upstream files retain their original bytes. In particular, the
021b reference may contain its original unused aramid `Infinity` token;
new results are serialized as strict JSON.

## Follow-up of differences

The broad search deliberately includes smaller fractions than the first
.00025 point of a 3,999-interior-point uniform search. Repeat differing
nonwinning core cases on [.00025,.99975] to distinguish domain effects from
arithmetic errors. This is a diagnostic of the numerical search range, not a
new fabrication constraint. Tiny-core counterexamples remain projections.

Eight extended-domain witnesses rounded Vc to 1.9999999999999998 when checked
directly, despite the shape solver selecting the upper branch at Vc=2.
`audit_differences.py` increases both thicknesses by 4e-12 relatively in those
witnesses. This exposes strictly upper-sided, feasible points with effectively
the same negative payload. The guard and raw/guarded masses are recorded.

For the extreme radius samples, independently perturb both thicknesses by
3e-13 and recompute joining. The remaining short-side coverage is only
60.96 nm. This sensitivity tests why apparently tiny sizing differences
strongly change those enormous negative margins. It is explicitly an inference
about conditioning; no owner's code is inspected to attribute a cause.

At each finite crossing, remove the outer geometry optimizer by solving the
observed active stress equations directly, then solve its mass equation for
radius. Compression/global or scarf/global fixes tf/R and tc/tf; the
wrinkle/global or wrinkle/crimp pairs fix both ratios through their equality.
Check both sides of the resulting shape at offsets 1e-7, 1e-6 and 1e-5 in f.
This exposes last-digit optimizer convergence effects, including two radii
outside the predeclared 2e-5 m comparison tolerance. Four nonwinning
low-density handbook rows also retain a small same-domain precision residual.
None is waived by widening tolerances.

Run the follow-up with `python3 audit_differences.py`. DIFFERENCES.json preserves
the first comparisons and all follow-up evidence; FINAL_FINDINGS.json and
DIFFERENCES.csv present the resulting classifications.
