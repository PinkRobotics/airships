   written and re-read (its `cgroup` line, then `GUARD OK`), the guard exits 97 and nothing runs.
2. **A missing GUARD OK is non-zero even when the payload returned 0:** GUARD_FAIL exits with the payload rc if it is

3. **Closure is complete only when** the unit is collected, GUARD OK is present, home and tmp are both removed, and
   memory.peak is recorded.

   - Failed removal gives INCOMPLETE_CLEANUP 31. A missing memory.peak gives INCOMPLETE_EVIDENCE 33. A receipt that is
     not written gives 32.
4. **Progression stops on incomplete closure.** Every later invocation requires the previous receipt to show
   closure_complete true, as well as its rc rule (refuse 16).
