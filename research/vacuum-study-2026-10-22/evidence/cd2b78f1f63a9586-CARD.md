# MAINLINE-067: which real materials could float P2

Unit vacuum-mainline067-materials-pathway-1020. QUESTIONABLE. PROJECTION. No qualified hull safety factor. No simjob. The work is published product data and one script, rank.py. Nothing is bought.

## The bar

The only admissible clear test is DELTA-C at gamma 0.20 and Q 0.33. The face keeps the programme SR-200 values, 1850 kg/m3, 290 GPa and 345 MPa, and the density has to be divided by the factor that brings concept payload to zero.

R 20 ISA needs 1.779, a target of 1040 kg/m3. Concept binds on face wrinkling. R 20 optimistic needs 1.863, a target of 993 kg/m3. Concept binds on global buckling. R 30 ISA needs 1.743, a target of 1061 kg/m3. Concept binds on face wrinkling. R 30 optimistic needs 1.825, a target of 1014 kg/m3. Concept binds on global buckling.

The strictest target is 993 kg/m3. The favorable-ramp factors of 1.06 to 1.12 are not admissible, and they are not used.

Two faces, both counted at the outer radius, are 10053 m2 at R 20 and 22619 m2 at R 30. The inner face is smaller, so this area is slightly high.

## Faces

SR-200, the programme tuple. Density 1850 kg/m3, modulus 290 GPa, strength 345 MPa. Every index below is a ratio to this row. Materion states sheet from 0.020 to 0.250 inch, at most 24 by 72 inch, 1.115 m2 a sheet. That is 9018 sheets at R 20 and 20289 sheets at R 30 if every sheet is the maximum rectangle and is used whole. Readiness is production, made to order. Admissible with a named limit: this is the hull model's face, not a new compression test on CR-III. USGS reports about 230 t of beryllium apparent consumption. That figure is not a sheet-area rate.

SAE SR200 minimum. Modulus 293 GPa and minimum tensile yield 206.8 MPa, with density taken as 1850. Specific modulus is 1.01 times the programme face. The wrinkling index is 1.003. The strength index is 0.60, worse, because the specification floor sits below 345 MPa. Same gauge. Admissible with a named limit: the yield is a tensile minimum from a partial extract, and that extract did not print the density.

I-220-H. Density 1844, modulus 303 GPa, minimum yield 345 MPa. Specific modulus 1.05, wrinkling 1.02, strength 1.00. It is block, rod or bar, not sheet. Not admissible as a face. Production instrument blanks.

AlBeMet AM162 rolled sheet. Density 2071, modulus 193 GPa, minimum yield 276 MPa. Specific modulus 0.59, wrinkling 0.78, strength 0.71. All three are worse than SR-200. Gauge was not on the pages read. Admissible with a named limit: rolled-sheet tensile minimums, not a bonded compression face.

CVD diamond windows. Density 3515, modulus 1050 GPa, tensile strength 750 MPa at 500 µm on the nucleation side. Specific modulus is 1.91 times SR-200, which clears that index on every handbook row. The wrinkling index is 0.81, which clears none. The strength index is 1.14, which clears none. Gauge is 10 to 2000 µm, diameter up to 100 mm. The R 20 faces would take about 1.28 million such disks. Not admissible as a hull face.

Boron/epoxy quasi-isotropic, a textbook typical. Modulus 80.3 GPa. The printed specific modulus 3820 is read as 3820 thousand metres, which gives density 2144. Specific modulus 0.24, wrinkling 0.56. Strength was not given. Not admissible. Not a lot certificate.

Carbon/epoxy quasi-isotropic, the same textbook, ordinary fiber. Modulus 54.8 GPa and density 1583 on that unit reading. Specific modulus 0.22, wrinkling 0.67. This is not a high-modulus lot. Not admissible.

Toray M60J epoxy, unidirectional, 60 percent fiber by volume. Modulus 360 GPa, unidirectional compression 790 MPa, density 1638. The density uses an assumed resin density of 1200 kg/m3, which is not on the Toray sheet. Specific modulus 1.40, wrinkling 1.21, strength 2.59. The strength clear is real only for that unidirectional compression coupon. Neither modulus index reaches 1.74. A quasi-isotropic sheet would be lower still. Not admissible as the membrane.

Miralon CNT yarn in a cyanate unidirectional laminate. One reported row is 329 GPa at 1380 kg/m3. Specific modulus 1.52, wrinkling 1.40. The paper's best specific-modulus claim is 1.63 times SR-200. That is still short of 1.74, and it is unidirectional. Coupons are about 0.19 mm. Lab. Not admissible. Tensile strength was not used as compression.

Miralon CNT nonwoven sheet. Density 650 kg/m3. A secondary table's modulus figure was ambiguous, so modulus is not used. Not admissible.

Aluminium 6061-T6 from the common brief. Density 2700, modulus 68.9 GPa, yield 276 MPa. Specific modulus 0.16, wrinkling 0.42, strength 0.55. It is the floor of the ranking. Admissible with a named limit as the pinned comparison metal.

## How the faces rank

Specific modulus, higher better: diamond, CNT yarn, M60J, I-220-H, the SAE minimum, programme SR-200, AlBeMet, boron quasi-isotropic, carbon quasi-isotropic, 6061.

Wrinkling improvement, higher better: CNT yarn, M60J, I-220-H, the SAE minimum, programme SR-200, diamond, AlBeMet, carbon quasi-isotropic, boron quasi-isotropic, 6061.

Strength improvement, only where a strength was entered: M60J, diamond, I-220-H, programme SR-200, AlBeMet, the SAE minimum, 6061.

The membrane rank drops the unidirectional coupons, the block, the diamond disks and the sheet with no modulus. On that rank, programme SR-200 leads both specific modulus and wrinkling. AlBeMet, both textbook laminates and 6061 follow, and all of them are worse. The SAE minimum is not a lighter product. It is a lower strength floor on the same sheet.

## Cores

Hexcel CR-III 5056, 3/8 inch cell, 1.0 pcf. Density 16.02 kg/m3. Through-thickness modulus 103.4 MPa. W shear modulus 46.9 MPa. This is the baseline wrinkling support. Admissible with a named limit: datasheet typicals for flat honeycomb, not this sphere. The CR-III 5056 table that was read has no grade below 1.0 pcf.

Hexcel HRH-10, 6.4 mm cell, 24 kg/m3. This is the lightest hexagonal grade in the EU table that was read. Stabilized compression modulus 41 MPa. W shear modulus 9 MPa. Wrinkling support is 0.42 of the baseline, at a higher density. Heavier and softer. It does not meet the screen. Admissible with a named limit as a flat aramid-honeycomb typical.

Ultracor UCF-51, 3/8 inch cell, 2.0 pcf. Density 32 kg/m3. Ribbon shear modulus 331 MPa. Compressive strength 1.41 MPa. The through-thickness modulus was not on the sheet, so wrinkling support is not ranked. Heavier than the baseline. Not admissible as this core. Satellite panel stock, about 36 by 24 inch.

## Bonds

3M AF 163-2U at 0.030 lb/ft2 weighs 0.1465 kg/m2. That is the programme baseline film. Flatwise tensile is 5.52 MPa at 75 F, on aluminium skins and 5052 honeycomb, not on beryllium. Production. Admissible with that limit.

3M AF 163-2U at 0.015 lb/ft2 weighs 0.0732 kg/m2, half the baseline. The weight matches the programme's favorable film. Flatwise strength at 0.015 was not in the rows read. Production. The weight is admissible. The strength is not. Half a film does not clear the face density factors.

## The answer

Nothing clears today. No product keeps SR-200's modulus and strength and reaches 993 to 1061 kg/m3. On the wrinkling index, which binds the concept payload at both ISA rows, SR-200 leads every sheet and quasi-isotropic row. Diamond clears only specific modulus, and only as small disks. The unidirectional coupons improve the indices and still miss 1.74, and the sphere cannot use a unidirectional membrane.

The projection, labeled as one, is that no metal trend in these sheets reaches 290 GPa below 1061 kg/m3. The best measured specific modulus here is still short of the factor, and it is unidirectional. Diamond's specific modulus is already enough. Its size is not a hull-sheet trend.

What each candidate would have to demonstrate is a biaxial compressive modulus and strength, at a gauge that can be made, over an area that can tile the two faces, bonded to the core that would fly. T3 and T7 should stay on SR-200 bonded to CR-III. Gamma and Q are knockdowns of that sandwich. A different face does not raise them. A diamond or CNT coupon would not be that test.
