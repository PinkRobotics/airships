## 1. Admissibility and the inputs that decide the answer

`ADMISSIBILITY.md` gives each source anchor and its limit.

- **Core:** CR III 5056, 3/8-inch, 1.0 pcf has catalog E3 ≈ 103.42 MPa and
  L/W shear moduli ≈ 103.42/46.88 MPa, plus separate compression, crush and shear
  strength scalars. These are coupon anchors. The required in-plane tensor,
  peak-to-densification curves, unload/reload and mixed-load law are not supplied
  by those numbers. 047's in-plane 1/0.5 MPa moduli remain regularization.
  056's foam benchmark supplies directional-law requirements, not CR III data.
- **Faces:** the 345 MPa SR-200 minimum is tensile yield. 036 does not establish
  the required finished thin-sheet biaxial compressive allowable. Forming,
  gauge loss, orientation, cure and lot scatter remain decisive. A source
  clarification: the current **SR-200 sheet datasheet does list 290 GPa**.
  That resolves the blanket “unsourced scalar E” wording, without qualifying
  an orthotropic sheet tensor or compressive/plastic law.
- **Bond:** light AF 163-2U **0.015 lb/ft² is a real listed product**. Its identity
  does not qualify the exact proposed Be/CR III process. The explicit 200 °F
  strength table is for 2K 0.060 on aluminum; the 2U results use another cure.
  The room-temperature Be scarf and measured cure residual state remain open.

These distinctions use [Hexcel Table II](https://www.hexcel.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/HexWeb_CRIII_DataSheet.pdf),
[Materion's SR-200 sheet data](https://hkvxni.files.cmp.optimizely.com/download/c3370ecef75e11eeb4768ed6ed17dca6)
and the [3M AF 163-2 guide](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/282041O/3m-scotch-weld-structural-adhesive-film-af-163-2-af-163-3.pdf),
alongside the pinned 036, 037b and 056 evidence.

CHECK047's 0.01–10 MPa core-regularization sensitivity moves tolerance by at most
0.004 mm. That supports prioritizing **processed face compression and gauge,
residual state, seams and measured defect geometry**, rather than another dense
regularization sweep. It does not make the unsourced core law physically valid.

`FE-NEXT.md` specifies the future admissible calculation: qualify material and

## 2. Closed-form size transfer

With one-face thickness `tf`, face-centre separation `d = tc + tf` and a local
Gaussian amplitude `a`, use the explicitly calibrated hypothesis

```text
sigma_m = SF*p*R/(4*tf)
sigma_peak ≈ sigma_m*(1 + C*a/d) + sigma_res
a_allow ≈ d/C * [(Fy - sigma_res)/sigma_m - 1]
```

For homothetic geometry at fixed material and pressure, `a_allow` scales with
**R**. The requested rows instead recover gauges from 057's mask-15,
actual-joining ISA bills. Holding `C` across those slightly different ratios
and shear compliance is an unvalidated transfer.

Calibrate separately to CHECK047's 1.2179 mm at SF 1 and 0.7469 mm at SF 1.05,
using 047's held R10 point and its **345 MPa tensile proxy**. The reference
geometry differs from the R10 row above. Retain 037b/047's residual-compression
estimate as a separate scaled scalar debit, not as thermal geometric stiffness.
At SF 1 the resulting cure-debited limits are **1.250, 2.535 and 3.847 mm**.

`METHOD.md` gives equations, calibration, geometry selection, Gaussian spans,
homothetic controls and the full precision values. The Gaussian e-fold radial
spans are 0.325, 0.645 and 0.967 m. These estimates cover that specified defect
family; they are not bounds for arbitrary dents, wrinkles or seam interactions.

law, exact processed-bond/seam behavior, measured cure/release state, and a
physically validated full-field defect model. A released curved joint and
unsupported closed-shell process article must then demonstrate retained shape.
An attractive RMS, numerical convergence or a positive no-growth payload cannot
replace those results.
