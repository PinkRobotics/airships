Measure T3 first. The equibiaxial factor is what moved the concept face off the filed 993 and 1146 kg/m3 targets, and it is the cheaper of the two earlier ranges. If the measured factor is 1, wrinkling on 069's filed geometry returns from 0.7937005438272905 to 1, the filed +5.031 kg point stands again as a paper result, and the TRL 7 knockdown the shell must show moves back toward 0.5533974659060663. If the measured factor stays 0.7937005259840998, today's 1850 kg/m3 face does not float at concept at γ 0.20, and only T7 can still ask whether γ 0.6407145989713096 floats that face at TRL 7, on a different geometry from the concept closure. Building the shell before the coupon prices the larger test against an allowable the coupon may retire. Neither purchase is authorized.

## T7 — knockdown γ — closes 038 route G1

G1 is the imperfect sandwich-sphere route. Its physical test in the record is T7. Physical gamma is still unvalidated.

### The question

T7 measures the hemisphere knockdown γ in p_collapse / (4 Kc σ0 tf / R), with Kc = 1 − Vc/4. That Kc is CR-1457 (4.2-4). It applies because every closure below has Vc under 2. Shear crimping is a different mode and its knockdown is 1. Do not report a shear-crimp failure as γ.

Today's admissible value is γ = 0.20.

The value the design needs, for TRL 7 at R 30 m on today's 1850 kg/m3 face, both weathers, is realized γ = 0.6407145989713096, factor 3.2035729948565477. UNREVIEWED. The two weathers are separate geometries. Optimistic: tf 0.0025609994447783123 m, tc 0.06053783162116337 m, Vc 1.028813150418843, payload residual +0.004303968004933267 kg. ISA at the same factor: tf 0.0025080725073963655 m, tc 0.059854777880926524 m, payload +3963.2344252914827 kg. That ISA figure is not a same-hull result. ISA alone would close at factor 2.9225964350158016, realized γ 0.5845192870031604, tf 0.0025763020572882886 m, tc 0.06468573203533268 m. The filed pre-2.2-15 factor is 2.7669873295303313, realized γ 0.5533974659060663.

### The article

Three closed sandwich shells, production SR-200 on CR-III 5056 3/8-1.0, processed bond, cell size not scaled.

The shell that answers the TRL 7 question uses the optimistic closure: each face 2.560999 mm, core 60.537832 mm, radius 30 m. Then R/tc = 495.557888 and tf/tc = 0.042304. Vc on that closure is 1.028813150418843.

A concept-gauge shell is a different article, not a substitute: each face 3.668148 mm, core 167.033469 mm, radius 30 m, R/tc = 179.604723, tf/tc = 0.021961, Vc 1.5029143292089922. Quote it only as a second family.

Count for the quote: three shells of the TRL 7 gauge pair, one as-built, one with a controlled localized dimple, one with a selected low-mode imperfection. The cheapest article that still returns a number is the single as-built shell. One shell does not measure scatter and does not calibrate the dimple. A 0.5 m foil or aluminum shell is the conditional model experiment in the 038 plan. It does not transfer to P2 at R 20–30 m, and this specification does not accept it as γ.

### Loading and measurement

External pressure, increased to collapse or to a declared overload past the pressure the γ formula would give at 0.6407145989713096. Survey the finished shell before any pressure: full surface, both face thicknesses, core thickness, core ribbon direction and both shear moduli, bond, seams, and mass. Instrument with full-field displacement, strain, pressure, and the pressure-volume trace. Record the first mode.

State the power used for σ0. The model uses the square root of 1−ν². Handbook (4.2-3) uses the one-quarter power. A result that does not say which power it used is not admissible. A result is admissible for the record when the mode is global buckling of this production sandwich at the stated gauges, the imperfection map is filed, Vc is reported and is under 2, and γ is reported with the formula above. It is inadmissible when the mode is wrinkling, yield, debond, seam, or shear crimping; when the article is a foil or aluminum surrogate; when the cell was scaled; or when there is no pre-load survey. A finite-element comparison may be filed beside the measurement. It is not the measurement.

### Cost basis

The earlier range is 30–90 thousand US dollars for three custom shells. PROJECTION, from the 038 plan, not a quote. The drivers in that plan are forming, core and bonding (10–30), metrology (5–15), and the pressure facility and reduction (15–45), in the same thousands. That range bought a thin surrogate. It does not buy a design-gauge shell at R/tc 495.6 with the production cell. The cheapest article that still answers the question is one as-built design-gauge shell, surveyed before load. Its price is outside the earlier range. No new price is invented here.

### The article

Production SR-200 on CR-III 5056 3/8-1.0, processed bond, both core ribbon directions identified. Primary gauges are the R30 optimistic concept row: faces 3.668148 mm, core 167.033469 mm. The R20 optimistic row is the second gauge, not a substitute. The panel, or the dome, must be wide enough that the measured wrinkle is short beside R 20 m and beside R 30 m. A 300 mm panel is only a starting size. If the central patch is edge-dominated, enlarge it. A pressurized dome of this same sandwich may replace the panel. A uniaxial strip may be cut from the same lot as a witness. It does not close G5.

Count for the quote: three equibiaxial articles, covering both ribbon directions and one repeat, plus one uniaxial witness. The cheapest pair that still answers is one equibiaxial article at the R30 optimistic gauges and one uniaxial witness from that lot. One article does not measure scatter.

### Loading and measurement

Equibiaxial compression, or external pressure on the dome, with both principal membrane stresses compressive. Report the stress ratio. Survey waviness, both thicknesses, cell, bond and mass before load. Instrument both faces with full-field displacement and strain. Separate onset from face yield, intracell dimpling, end crush and debond. Report the wavelength at onset.

Report Q in the same convention as the 0.33 lower bound, and report the equibiaxial factor as the onset stress divided by the uniaxial onset of the witness. A result is admissible when the product form is this sandwich, the stress ratio is measured, the mode is face wrinkling, and the wavelength is reported against R 20 m and R 30 m. It is inadmissible when the load is uniaxial only, when yield or debond censors the wrinkle, when the wavelength is long beside R and that is not stated, or when the article is not this face, core and bond. The flat-panel bound is not a sphere test. This measurement is what would make the equibiaxial factor a measured allowable.

### Cost basis

The earlier biaxial range is 18–45 thousand US dollars, plus material. PROJECTION, from the 038 plan, not a quote. The drivers are the equal-biaxial fixture, the displacement survey, and a design-gauge panel. The uniaxial screen in that plan, 6–18 thousand, does not answer the factor. The cheapest article that still answers is one design-gauge equibiaxial panel, or one pressurized dome, plus the uniaxial witness. If the core thickness of 167 mm makes a 300 mm panel edge-dominated, the article grows and the earlier range may not hold. No new price is invented here.
