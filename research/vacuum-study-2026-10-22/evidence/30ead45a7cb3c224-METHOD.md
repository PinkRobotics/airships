# Independent CHECK-052 method

The only foreign scientific data used as computational inputs are frozen JSON/CSV/NPZ records. No foreign Python, shell script, archive of executable material, or owner module is read or imported. NPZ files are loaded with `allow_pickle=False`. The checker code and its development snapshots are original to this seat. The previous CHECK-050 pad equation was consulted as this seat's own derivation; no code from that unit is imported.

The independence boundary is the pinned CHECK041 static forces, coordinates and perfect-frame eigenvalue. This check recomputes the complete algebraic resizing, joint sizing, mass, film, gas, subset selection and hazard calculation from that boundary. It does not independently assemble another FE model. Physical qualifications from all predecessor cards remain open.

For each checker edge, L=2R asin(chord/(2R)). The same edge numbering is used by the segment-to-member array. For each triangular cell, planar Heron area divided by semiperimeter gives the inradius. The maximum cell chord defines the inherited cap radius. Cap volume divided by circular footprint area gives mean sag, weighted by the independently calculated spherical triangle area. This yields the 73.100733115 kg reference sag debit.

Let k=R/10 and a be reference-scale area. A=a k², g=g0 k, I=a g0² k⁴, L=L0 k; outer/inner radius squared is 2g²±A/(2π). With pressure ratio q to the original force solution, each segment's amplified compression is qN/A + q(M+NL/1000)ro/[I(1−q/λE)], where λE=π²EI/(L²N). Positive extreme tension is checked separately. The reference-scale computation is equivalent under N∝k² and M∝k³.

A bracketed scalar area root enforces the 0.2 global eigenvalue screen, Euler load, chosen compression ceiling, inherited tension screen and the specified local shell condition. The primary local capacity is compared with peak amplified compression; the alternate axial-only case compares it with axial compression. Local capacity is [.099+.901 exp(−sqrt(ro/t)/16)]×.6E t/ro. These are declared projections, not empirical validation for curved SiC.

For all 1500 member ends, an independent vector bisection solves sleeve area from N/Aj+M rj/Ij=1470 MPa, where rj²=ro²+Aj/π and Ij=Aj(rj²+ro²)/4. Overlap is sqrt[(N+2M/ro)²+(T/ro)²]/(2πro×28.9 MPa). Sleeve volume uses that end's area and overlap. Bond mass uses the complete bond area, fixed 0.2 mm gap and 1400 kg/m³ density, with a 5k² kg installation floor. Tube and sleeve mass scale with k³; fixed-gap adhesive mass scales with k².

The axes are the declared 1→3.9 GPa compression ceiling, 3.48→5.8 GPa effective ply strength, .96^3.65→.995^10 retention, and sleeve allowance max(actual,.05 tube)→actual. Active axes interpolate logarithmically at common u. Disabled axes remain at the lower target. Every subset endpoint is evaluated, the smallest feasible number of active axes selected, and each tied subset's zero-payload u solved independently by Brent bracketing. An empty-subset endpoint retains u=1 as bookkeeping; its physics is identical to u=0.

Total PBO gauge is p rc/(Fply×retention×φT−σthermal), with equal directional plies. φT and σthermal are inherited 046 hot/restraint screens. Gauge times full-sphere area times 1560 gives film mass. Protection comprises the PET, aluminium, attachment, white layer and arc-scaled seam allowance exactly once. The complete bill includes actual/floored sleeve allowance, adhesive, nodes, systems100, additional vacuum service, gas, exterior water, contamination, load introduction, sag and weather headroom.

The mooring allowance independently uses F=.47×(.5ρv²)πR² at exactly 70×1852/3600 m/s; pad area is F/241316.505261 Pa. With a=sqrt(area/π), thickness squared is 3(1+.33)F ln(a/.025)/(2π×276 MPa). Its mass is area×thickness×2700. The logarithm means this allowance is not purely cubic in radius. It is excluded from the immutable cubic lower bound used to demonstrate adverse-weather failure.

The three weather pressure/headroom pairs are the pinned 031/040 conventions, crosschecked against the P2 data. Applied frame/film pressure is P0 max(1.01,qweather)+.5ρISA v70². Sag buoyancy uses reference lift minus the weather headroom. The source field `weather.T_K` does not change the inherited vacuum bulk temperature: the reproduced water inventory convention is Tbulk=284.9 K. This distinction is made explicit here.

Free volume removes cap sag, SiC solid volume and film volume. Tube bores remain vented. Dry species fractions are normalized. Film molar permeation is the .5 cc O2/m²/day datum divided by 22414 cc/mol, multiplied by area, the declared species permeability ratio and species driving pressure/101325. Leakage of .001 mbar L/s per arc metre and node plus .01 ports is converted with 0.1 Pa m³/s per mbar L/s; leaked water fraction is pw/pambient. WVTR contributes an additional .001 kg/m²/day.

Finite water-proxy material inventory B is 10% of the summed PBO/CFRP/PET/adhesive/SiC-surface/attachment inventories. Release is B(1−exp(−t)) for t in days. Water mass is qwater t+B(1−exp(−t)); initial gas is dry. Capacity is .05 psat(Tcold) V Mwater/(Rgas×284.9 K). Modified Buck over water supplies saturation, including the stated supercooled-water convention for the h=2 case. Both humidity crossings are independently bracketed; DOP853 integration checks the first-root inventory at crossing.

Total-pressure screening uses gas heated to 320 K. Peak inlet-throughput demands use the initial release rate and 320 K, divided by pressure headroom above 3 Pa. Integer pump counts satisfy total-pressure, water-partial-pressure and .7 kg/h-per-pump limits with 27.5×.75 m³/h each. Battery mass is (1000n+5)×24/(150×.8×.9). Additional flying equipment is 45+44n kg. Ground hardware is 50 kg inclusive of its monitoring allowance; its separately reported energy diagnostic is not charged again. Ground intervals are null for onboard service rows.

Each member's peak over its 24 segments is used for Hwhole=Σ(σ/3.9 GPa)^10 and Hcoupon=Σ(A L/10⁻⁶ m³)(σ/3.9 GPa)^10. Pf=−expm1(−H); the 10⁻³ target scale follows directly from H/−log(1−10⁻³). These hypotheses omit common causes, load redistribution, joints, film and damage. Neither gives a calibrated hull Pf.

P2 calculations reconstruct selection and mass-ledger arithmetic on the already filed 040/048 endpoint sets. They do not perform a new P2 section optimization. Each upstream endpoint payload is checked by subtracting its complete bill from its lift. The maximum over all subsets is then reduced by 50 kg service. All 48 comparison rows and 312 rows across the three published CSVs are compared with independently reconstructed values.

Numerical-field acceptance is |difference|≤10⁻⁸+3×10⁻⁸|owner|, except payload and usage fields use a 2×10⁻⁶ absolute term. Every difference is retained, including values that pass. The root residual is also checked independently. Printed cells are checked at half their last shown decimal place, with a 2×10⁻⁷ numerical allowance. Near-one probability strings are checked through their explicitly calculated survival probabilities. Prose precision is reported separately.

No runtime observation is a physical tolerance or qualification. Measured start/end times are recorded without a task budget. Source code, all comparisons, exact discrepancies and released simjob receipts are included.
