## The groups

The knockdown uses five groups. The formulas are the model's, and they reproduce the filed Vc on both R 30 closures.

- Vc = σ0 / (G h² / (2 tf tc)), with h = tf + tc and σ0 = E h / (R sqrt(1−ν²)). E is 293.6 GPa and ν is 0.0768. G is the CR-III W shear, 46.884350 MPa. CR-1457 (4.2-4) then gives Kc = 1 − Vc/4 while Vc is under 2. Both articles stay under 2.
- R/tc. Chapter 6 of CR-1457 requires this ratio to be large. The articles keep the design values, 179.604723 and 495.557888.
- tf/tc.
- Imperfection amplitude over the sandwich thickness. Figure 2.2-6 is a waviness amplitude over the core, and the hemisphere knockdown in (6.2-13) is empirical. No amplitude for this sandwich is printed. DOI 10.2514/3.5568 is the monocoque-cylinder imperfection knockdown. It does not give this sandwich ratio. DOI 10.2514/3.1932 does not state these groups.
- Cell count across a buckle. Section 1.2 separates the cell, the wrinkle, and general instability. The count is not a handbook number. On a smaller shell the 3/8 in cell is a larger fraction of any buckle that scales with the shell.

The model σ0 uses the square root of 1−ν². Handbook (4.2-3) uses the one-quarter power. A reduction that omits the power is not admissible.
