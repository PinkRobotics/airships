# MAINLINE-070 graphite-film faces

Unit vacuum-mainline070-graphite-face-1020. QUESTIONABLE. PROJECTION. No qualified hull safety factor. The hull is 063's model as 066 carried it, with BETA set to 1. Two simjobs. No finite-element run.

## Admissibility

The in-plane modulus 969 GPa and the density 2250 kg/m3 are admissible with a named limit. The limit is uniaxial tension of the mirror-like graphite film in DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-62227-6, at process gauges of about 2 to 145 µm, not a compressed face of 1.6 to 2.5 mm.

The film paper does not state a thermal expansion in the text opened here. The in-plane value used is the HOPG catalogue magnitude 1.0e-6 per K, which is a different product. That substitution is admissible with that named limit. The negative sign is not credited. The version-7 interface, cure and core-wall terms stay on 12.3e-6. They do not see the face expansion.

Compressive strength is not a sourced compression test. The sweep carries the tensile mean 1.29 GPa and the programme full allowable 379.2 MPa as a range. The two payloads match, so the range does not bind.

A stack needs a binder. Ten percent of the thickness as adhesive with no modulus and a density of 1200 kg/m3 is a projection: 872.1 GPa and 2145 kg/m3. It is not a product.

Gamma of 0.20 is the card's value. The CR-1457 support for 0.20 is the named limit below Vc of 2. These optima sit at Vc 3.02 to 3.45, so 0.20 is outside that support. Version 7's own step sets gamma to 1 above Vc 2. Both are reported. Q stays 0.33.

## Gamma held at 0.20

No case floats. Concept payloads are -16149.9, -17974.2, -52721.1 and -58887.8 kg. TRL 7 payloads are -11268.6, -12971.3, -36447.5 and -42202.5 kg. The order is R 20 ISA, R 20 optimistic, R 30 ISA, R 30 optimistic.

Global buckling and scarf shear co-bind at 1. Wrinkling sits near 1.376. Face compression sits near 4.25 at 1.29 GPa and does not move when strength is 379.2 MPa. Thermal interface sits near 1.071. Core-wall yield sits near 2.40.

The face-density factor that just floats each case is 1.606, 1.693, 1.574 and 1.659 at concept, and 1.405, 1.478, 1.382 and 1.453 at TRL 7. The densities are 1401, 1329, 1429 and 1357 kg/m3 at concept, and 1601, 1522, 1629 and 1549 kg/m3 at TRL 7. R 20 optimistic sets both families. Face gauges are 1.645 to 2.534 mm, so a 145 µm film is about 11 to 17 plies.

## Version 7's gamma step

With the copied step, Vc is 3.05 to 3.13 and gamma becomes 1. Payloads are still negative: concept -6939.8, -8521.1, -21491.3 and -26831.2 kg, and TRL 7 -2894.3, -4376.0, -8055.7 and -13059.4 kg. Scarf shear and core shear crimping co-bind. The core thins to about 17 to 27 mm. The density factors fall to 1.199, 1.248, 1.180 and 1.227 at concept, and 1.082, 1.126, 1.067 and 1.110 at TRL 7. Still no float. Leaving the beryllium expansion of 11.3e-6 in the self-stress terms makes the concept R 20 ISA payload -7248 kg instead of -6940 kg. It does not change the bind.

## Binder and the answer

The ten-percent binder projection at gamma 0.20 is less negative, -14140 kg at concept R 20 ISA, and it does not float.

A graphite-film P2 floats in no case. At the card's gamma the improvement still needed is a lighter face, factor 1.693 at concept and 1.478 at TRL 7, to 1329 and 1522 kg/m3, without giving up the 969 GPa. Thermal interface does not bind, and the copied term cannot show a graphite mismatch. Strength inside the carried range does not bind.
