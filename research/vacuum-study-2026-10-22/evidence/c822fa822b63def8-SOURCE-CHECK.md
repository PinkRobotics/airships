**CHECK-051 source audit — QUESTIONABLE / PROJECTION**

The current primary records support the principal material numbers used by MAINLINE-051. They do not establish complete-member compression distributions or qualified frame safety factors. Source observations are distinguished below from the checker's inference about transfer to this design. All local source copies used for this check are pinned in INPUTS.json. No supplier was contacted.

Saint-Gobain's Hexoloy-SA sheet lists 3.10 g/cm³, E=430 GPa, ν=.14 and typical room-temperature compression 3900 MPa. Its listed Weibull modulus 10 is not accompanied by a fitted compression population or compression specimen size. The 3×4×45 mm footnote attaches to flexure. Consequently the owner's whole-member scale=3.9 GPa/m=10 remains a declared hypothesis. The 041 reference's E=410 GPa is a separate inherited model input. [Manufacturer sheet, page 4](https://www.ceramicsrefractories.saint-gobain.com/sites/hps-mac3-cma-pcr/files/assetbank_media/hexoloy-sa-sic-en-1013-tds-215398.pdf).

CoorsTek's 2024 table supports PureSiC-HR E=462 GPa, density 3.21 g/cm³ and ν=.21, with compression blank. UltraSiC direct-sintered has E=410 GPa, density 3.15 g/cm³, ν=.21 and typical compression 3500 MPa; LP is a different grade. The initial web open returned 502; the indexed primary PDF returned the complete table. No secondary reseller values were used. [CoorsTek table](https://www.coorstek.com/media/4249/silicon-carbides.pdf).

The current M55J sheet reports composite axial tensile modulus 338 GPa, compression 890 MPa, transverse tension 37 MPa and in-plane shear 68 MPa, at 60% fibre volume with #2500 epoxy. Fibre E=540 GPa and density 1.91 g/cm³ are separate entries. These are reference lot averages. The owner's use of 338 GPa and 890 MPa is faithful; its transverse elastic constants and wrapped tube construction remain projections. [Toray M55J, 2025 revision](https://www.toraycma.com/wp-content/uploads/M55J-Data-Sheet.pdf).

The current T700S sheet reports composite E=134 GPa, compression 1450 MPa, transverse tension 81 MPa and in-plane shear 136 MPa, normalized to 60% fibre volume. Fibre density is 1.80 g/cm³. This confirms the owner's 1450 MPa rather than a transferred older 1470 MPa. [Toray T700S, November 2025 revision](https://www.toraycma.com/wp-content/uploads/T700S-Data-Sheet.pdf).

The DIALEAD brochure's image table on PDF page 7 gives K13D2U fibre E=935 GPa, tensile strength 3700 MPa and density 2.20 g/cm³. The pinned page image was inspected independently. The source does not provide the modeled tube's compression distribution. The 60% volume mixture with assumed epoxy E=3 GPa/density 1200, other lamina constants, and 0/+45/−45/90 wall fractions remain explicit design hypotheses. [Mitsubishi brochure](https://www.m-chemical.co.jp/carbon-fiber/pdf/dialead/composites.pdf).

Specialty Materials' metric table confirms SCS-6/SCS-Ultra fibre E=380/415 GPa, tensile strength 3900/5900 MPa and density 3.08/2.89 g/cm³. The page's descriptive diameter and imperial entries conflict with parts of its metric table, as the owner discloses. It also cautions that the surface is not optimized for resin interfaces. None of these values supplies axial-compression strength for an epoxy tube. [Manufacturer page](https://www.specmaterials.com/silicon-carbide-fiber-1).

Kaiser lists 6061-T6 typical tensile yield 276 MPa, E=68.3 GPa averaged over tension/compression, and nominal density 2700 kg/m³. A symmetric compression-yield extension is a constitutive assumption; a brittle Weibull fracture distribution is appropriately left unused. The density itself has a direct citation on page 2, although the owner's model note calls it a nominal assumption. [Kaiser tube sheet](https://online.kaiseraluminum.com/depot/PublicProductInformation/Document/1071/Kaiser_Aluminum_6061_Pipe_Tube.pdf).

The inherited HNS filament/bundle and Tyranno bar/tube evidence was retained with its original population and loading mode. The pinned source text contains the HNS 8.42/3062 MPa filament and 9/1730 MPa bundle fits, and Tyranno's flexural m=10.2 with mean 597 MPa. Reusing those fits for compression or impact would require new evidence; the owner excludes them from compression and labels its hail transfers uncalibrated. The GE HNS-CMC compression statement concerns hot component averages, not the projected room-temperature tube. [HNS experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7452681/), [Tyranno experiment](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/matertrans/44/9/44_9_1797/_pdf).

The orthotropic shell calculation is an idealized continuum screen. The NASA formulation supports studying orthotropic cylindrical-shell buckling, but does not qualify the inherited isotropic knockdown, peak-stress substitution, homogenized wall, or a manufactured tube. Those transfers remain qualification NFIs. [NASA/TP-2009-215778](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20090029956).
