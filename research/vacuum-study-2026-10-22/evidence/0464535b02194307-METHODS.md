# MAINLINE-026b methods — QUESTIONABLE

This is a parameter extension of the seat's independent frozen
MAINLINE-026 model. No peer calculation program is imported or executed.
The verified 022/022b JSON rows supply section and mass data.
The 030 CONTINUUM row is compared independently. Peer Python files in
intake are provenance and readable equations only.

The radius-10 m icosahedral frequency-f mesh has 10f²+2 joints,
30f² curved members and 20f² triangular cells. Members follow
great-circle arcs; each is represented by m straight, stress-free
Euler–Bernoulli frame subelements. Rigid joints share translations and
rotations; ball joints share translations only. Internal subelement
connections are rigid. Six global motions are removed by orthogonality
constraints rather than physical supports.

The exact annular section is A = π(ro²−ri²), I = π(ro⁴−ri⁴)/4,
EA = E A, EI = E I and GJ = E I/(1+ν), with E = 410 GPa and ν = 0.14.
`beam.configure(frequency)` selects CASES.json before symbol imports;
each invocation uses a fresh process, and all frames in that invocation
use the same section. E = 410 GPa is the dispatched comparison input,
not a claim that all Hexoloy data sheets specify that value.

For each triangular cell, its curved boundary nodes define a centroid
c equal to their arithmetic mean. With oriented boundary b_i,

    V = Σ_cells c · Σ_i(b_i × b_(i+1)) / 6
    external potential = λ p V
    pressure force = −λ p ∇V.

The centroid's coordinate dependence is differentiated. Film material
stiffness is omitted. Equal triangular cells give tributary width
s/(2√3), hence N approximately pRs/(2√3). The fan is a conservative
load-transfer convention, not an equilibrium membrane calculation.
Its enclosed volume is not used as validated displaced-air volume.

The unit-pressure elastic state solves K u = −p ∇V with the six
orthogonality constraints. Compression N on each subelement supplies
the standard initial-stress matrix for transverse cubic Hermite
interpolation. In coordinates [v_i, θ_i, v_j, θ_j] its positive
destabilizing block is

    N/(30L) [ 36,    3L,  −36,    3L
               3L, 4L²,  −3L,  −L²
              −36, −3L,   36,   −3L
               3L, −L²,  −3L,  4L² ].

Both bending planes are included with consistent rotation signs.
The buckling problem is

    K v = λ (G_N − p Hessian(V)) v.

The largest generalized eigenvalues of the destabilizing matrix against
K give the smallest positive λ by reciprocal. This is a different
selection problem from the nonlinear nearest-zero ambiguity.
Static and eigen residuals and twelve representative modes are saved.
The formulation omits initial-moment couplings and local wall effects.
The inherited method and its primary references are retained in
the pinned 026 methods [source link unavailable in this public projection].
The standard initial-stress formulation is also described in
[Bleyer's beam buckling example](https://comet-fenics.readthedocs.io/en/latest/demo/beam_buckling/beam_buckling.html).

The source continuum comparison is

    λ_cont = 4 sqrt(1.5) E sqrt(A I) / (s R² p).

It is evaluated with k = 1 at each source's nominal s. A separate
sensitivity replaces s with the actual mean great-circle arc.
The register's smeared half-wave is recovered as

    ℓ_half = π sqrt(sqrt(1.125) sqrt(I/A) R).

These inherited formulas are comparison hypotheses. Their successful
arithmetic reproduction is not validation of homogenization at coarse
bays. Realization changes member lengths and counts as well as the
representability of a continuum buckle.

The optional nonlinear calculation retains the objective corotational
beam energy from 026, differentiated by CPU float64 automatic
differentiation. Displacement-controlled equilibrium includes the
conservative pressure Hessian. The exact mixed virtual-centroid
factorization equals the condensed pressure matrix; new zero-state
checks verify this for each frequency. Rotation/strain conventions
and finite-state validation are in the pinned 026 methods.

**Nonlinear result qualification:** the reused solver asks for three
eigenvalues nearest zero (`eigsh`, `sigma=0`, `which="LM"`).
That does not establish the smallest algebraic tangent eigenvalue.
The distinction follows directly from
[SciPy's eigsh selection definitions](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.sparse.linalg.eigsh.html).
The frequency-4 path exposes an ambiguous change of the selected
near-zero family. Raw `elastic_instability_bracketed` status and
`critical_lambda_estimate` fields therefore describe the solver's
reported crossing, not an accepted first loss. Top-level RESULTS
sets `verified_first_nonlinear_instability` to null for both new cases.
No nonlinear imperfection or material-collapse claim is made.
A fresh authorized check must use smallest-algebraic selection or
indefinite-matrix inertia across the precritical path, then refine
the earliest sign change. No such run was started after refusal.

For pinned counts, the endpoint-distance rigidity matrix has one row
per member and 3V columns. Its computed rank is 3V−6 for f = 2, 4, 6.
Thus only the six global motions remain among endpoint positions.
Each curved member can independently rotate rigidly about its endpoint
chord, adding one internal mechanism. Representative 0.05 rad finite
rolls have elastic energies below 1.4e−21 J and infinitesimal stiffness
residuals below 3e−18. No pinned positive-pressure eigenvalue is reported.

For the rise supplement, a member chord C and original sagitta h0
define h = 8h0 and a circular arc of radius C²/(8h)+h/2 through the
unchanged endpoints, bowed outward. The reference is stress-free.
One load case moves the pressure fan with this reference; another
holds the original spherical force vector. Static force statistics
use subelement-length-weighted axial member means and endpoint force
projections onto the endpoint chord. Bending stress is
ro sqrt(My²+Mz²)/I at each subelement end. At excessive elastic
stresses this is a diagnostic extrapolation, not an allowable.
The legacy metadata `arc_length_m` always describes endpoint
great-circle separation; `arc_total_length_m` and mass use the actual
raised circular lengths in the rise-8 case.

Mass uses true circular lengths rather than the shorter subelement
chords. Film, joints and systems remain source projections; the
022b sag debit is carried separately. Neither elastic eigenvalues
nor 1.5 GPa projection film validate fabrication or lift.

All simulations and numerical self-checks ran through simjob, one CPU
core each, with numerical library threads fixed to one. The 17th
request set includes one memory-admission refusal and 16 successful,
released admissions. A refusal ended further scientific launches.
`collect.py` only reads JSON and generates presentation SVGs;
rasterization and package validation launch no simulation or solver.
