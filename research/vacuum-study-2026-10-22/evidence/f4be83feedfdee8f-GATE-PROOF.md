## Domain and representation

Take n=0, lambda=0, the frozen 120-cell space (40 cells on each of the
three panels), full U,V in H1_0 and W in H2_0. Retain both physical axial
parities and the axisymmetric tangential V field. Divide both form and norm
by their common 2*pi*R factor. Use exactly

    G = m (||U'||^2 + ||V'||^2) + D ||W''||^2.

The admitted endpoint formula gives y=y_x=N=Ny=0 at this particular load;
its load derivatives have NOT been set to zero. The imperfection slope is
s=(eta/10) sin(x) on the central panel, x=gamma*X/L, and zero outside.
eta=sqrt(sqrt(273)/2500), eta<9/100. Thus S=9/1000 bounds |s|.

On central cell i=1,...,40, x_i=(2i-41)*pi/40, d=pi/40 and
xi=(x-x_i)/d belongs to [-1,1]. Use the degree-five Taylor polynomial

    t_i(xi) = sum(j=0..5) a_ij xi^j,
    a_ij = (eta/10) d^j/j! sin(x_i+j*pi/2).

This formula specifies the representation; no list of its coefficients
has been evaluated. Rational coefficient selection is defined below. The
physical reference-cell map is exact, including its symbolic geometry;
rationality means rational coefficients in xi, not a replacement of pi or
the physical mesh by rational endpoints.

Choose coefficients on i=1,...,20 by the fixed rule below and define the
other 20 cells by exact odd reflection, shat(X)=-shat(-X). Outside the
central panel shat=0. This preserves the reflection symmetry of the form.
No continuity of shat at joins is required for this bounded form error;
piecewise integration must retain the exact joins. In particular no
derivative across a join is used. Its value error applies also at the
endpoints by either one-sided bound.

## Finite rational selection, with an a priori error

This is a finite constructive specification, not a coefficient evaluation.
Obtain a rational enclosure of pi by the 256-term Machin series specified
in admitted B, and an enclosure of eta by two 256-step endpoint square-root
bisections, first sqrt(273), then sqrt(sqrt(273)/2500). For the inner root,
the starting bracket [0,273] has width 273*2^-256 afterwards. Division by
2500 and sqrt's Lipschitz constant at most 25/4 (sqrt argument > (2/25)^2)
add less than 2^-256 to the outer width; its two endpoint bisections each
have width 2^-256. Thus the eta enclosure width is less than 3*2^-256.
The Machin enclosure width is at most
16/(513*5^513)+4/(513*239^513), less than 2^-1000.

For either enclosed constant, take the interval midpoint and round down
to the lattice 2^-80 Z. Call the selected rationals p and e. Consequently
|p-pi| and |e-eta| are less than 2^-79. This is floor of a rational midpoint,
so it does not require deciding the exact floor of an irrational number.

Evaluate sin/cos at z=(2i-41)*p/40 using their Taylor polynomials through
degree 80 about zero, exactly rationally. Here |z|<4, and the Lagrange
remainder for either polynomial is at most 4^81/81! < 2^-160. Select sin,
cos, -sin or -cos according to j modulo four, multiply by
(e/10)*(p/40)^j/j!, and round down to 2^-80 Z. This defines shat's six
rational coefficients. There is no adaptive precision, retry or unknown
termination step.

For clarity a deliberately loose forward error bound suffices. Both exact
and selected eta are below 1/10, both d values below 1/10, the polynomial
trig values have absolute value below 2, and the angle perturbation is
below 2^-79. A trig factor's total error is below 2^-78. For 1<=j<=5,
|d^j-dhat^j| <= j*(1/10)^(j-1)*|d-dhat| <= 2^-79;
for j=0 it is zero. Expanding the three-factor product gives coefficient
error less than 2^-77 before rounding, and less than 2^-76 afterwards
(division by j! only improves it). Summing six coefficients on |xi|<=1
gives less than 6*2^-76 < 2^-70. Exact reflection preserves that error.

The spatial Taylor remainder is bounded on the complete cell by

    delta_T <= (9/1000)*(11/140)^6/720,

because d=pi/40<11/140 and every derivative of sin has magnitude at most
one. Therefore the complete, constructive, outward coefficient error is

    ||s-shat||_infinity < delta_T + 2^-70 < delta = 1/10^11.

This includes spatial truncation and rational coefficient selection. No
parameter interval is being represented at this singleton-load gate.
N and Ny are identically zero here by the admitted formula, not by a
claim that adverse prestress has zero symmetric error on a load box.

## Actual G-operator error, full U/V/W

Write A=Et/(1-nu^2), nu=3/10, R/t=500. At n0 the form can be written

    q_s = A||U'+s W'+nu W/R||^2 + m||V'||^2
          +D||W''||^2 + (Et/R^2)||W||^2.

Replace s by shat only. U remains uncondensed: the fixed-end reaction has
not been removed. The difference is the integral of

    A(s-shat) W' [2U'+(s+shat)W'+2nu W/R].

Dirichlet inequalities already used in admitted C give
||U'||<=sqrt(G/m), ||W'||<=L*sqrt(G/D)/2 and
||W||<=L^2*sqrt(G/D)/4. Hence, without any T-to-G substitution,

    |q_s-q_shat| <= epsilon_raw G,
    epsilon_raw = delta*[ A L/sqrt(mD)
                         + A(2S+delta)L^2/(4D)
                         + A nu L^3/(4RD) ].

Use the outward physical constants L/t<400,
A*t/sqrt(mD)<6 and A*t^2/D=12. Then

    epsilon_raw < delta*(126240+480000*delta)
                < epsilon = 1/500000.

For a bounded symmetric form, its absolute diagonal bound is its
self-adjoint operator norm bound in the indicated Hilbert norm. Thus
||Q-Qhat||_G <= epsilon. This holds on the entire constrained space and
therefore on the 120-cell finite space, its G-complement, and either
physical parity. No 480-cell mesh-dependent estimate is used.

The exact screen is passed:

    epsilon+4epsilon^2 = 125001/62500000000 < 1,
    so epsilon < (sqrt(17)-1)/8.

The larger intermediate constants are deliberately conservative. This
proof changes the local representation from linear to degree five; it
does not recycle the known unusable linear-slope/global-error bound.
