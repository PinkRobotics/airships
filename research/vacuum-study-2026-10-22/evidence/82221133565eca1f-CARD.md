- **Calibration:** the retained mass per area is calibrated so this seat's Furmidge size on a measured vertical acrylic plate (advancing 78, receding 48 deg) gives its measured 54 g/m2 after condensation.

- **Hydrophilic finishes** also keep a draining film: Jeffreys' after rain, Nusselt's under dew.
- **Near the top** every finish is capped at the 0.8 mm a horizontal leaf holds.

- **Convection bracket:** the low reading is 040's own sphere correlation; the high is a wind flat-plate h = 5.7 + 3.8 U.
- **Check:** a grass-albedo surface at FAO-56's hourly example reaches 0.72 kg/m2/h at its sun-facing element against FAO's 0.63 mm/h.
- **Mirror finishes** absorb only 9-20% of the sun, so they dry slowly.
