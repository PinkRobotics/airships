Source assessment for CHECK046. QUESTIONABLE / PROJECTION.

The original source files are frozen with hashes in INPUT-SOURCE-PROVENANCE.json and OWNER-MANIFEST-CHECK.json. The graphs and patent exponents were inspected as rendered pages, separately from extracted text. Fresh retrieval of the Toyobo manual exactly matches its frozen SHA-256. FRESH-SOURCE-PINS.json records the two successful direct retrievals. Browser attempts at the publisher and PMC were blocked; the frozen primary published PDF supplied that evidence.

Toyobo's manufacturer manual supports approximately .71 HM retention at half a month, .35 at six months, and visible-light vulnerability. Its long humidity and warehouse curves concern AS yarn. The graph readings are approximate; the outdoor continuation misses two intermediate points. The humidity tail, warehouse exponential and argon repeated-loss continuation are chosen scenario forms. Neither a service climate nor a finished-laminate acceleration law is measured. The creep statement at 19 years is an extrapolation, while <.03% residual strain concerns only 100 hours at half breaking load. Hot-strength graph transfer is approximate. See [Toyobo technical manual](https://www.toyobo-global.com/seihin/kc/pbo/zylon-p/bussei-p/technical.pdf), pages 5, 9, 10 and 12.

All fourteen day/strength pairs were independently extracted from Table 1. The final 3.08/4.82 ratio is .639004. The two successive climates are not one isotherm. Sealed glass/argon at 55°C and subsequent ultra-dry storage do not establish polymer-laminate protection. See [NISTIR 7373](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=860679), PDF pages 6, 10–11, 23 and 26; PRIMARY-TABLE-CHECK.json contains the extraction.

The 400-hour coating results .214, .53 and optimized .59 are short 340 nm, 50°C exposures. They establish partial improvement, not ten-year conversion or a construction mass. See [coating study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-45199-8), frozen published PDF.

The phase-II .58 combined and .80 temperature/humidity retention values are supported. The conditioning protocol explicitly lacks an exact field-life equivalence. Folding's .15/.40 changes concern strain to failure, and the owner correctly does not relabel them tensile-strength losses. See [NISTIR 7627](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/IR/nistir7627.pdf) and [NIST folding study](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=902527).

The fatigue source uses 50 mm HM single filaments, R=0, 50 Hz. Its two median endpoints are 514800 and 21060 cycles at .6/.7 of measured 5.03 GPa strength; sample sizes are 7 and 30. The fitted exponent 20.735575 and all cycle indices reproduce. These are medians, not laminate allowables or high-R gust-life evidence. See [Davies et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10853-010-4721-z), Tables 2–3, [author-hosted paper](https://archimer.ifremer.fr/doc/00016/12688/9625.pdf).

The DuPont guide distinguishes conditioned Kevlar49 yarn strength 3.0 GPa from resin-impregnated strand strength. Density 1440 kg/m³ is supported; ten-year .95 retention remains a requirement. See [DuPont technical guide, distributor-hosted original](https://www.r-g.de/wiki/images/e/ec/Td_en_Kevlar_guide.pdf), table page 5.

Forster et al. give an approximate 140 kJ/mol oxidative shift energy and a conditional 36-year prediction to .70 retention at 43°C. The experiment includes unconstrained yarn and temperatures crossing physical relaxation; this does not identify SK99 or DM20 kinetics. The owner correctly marks the exponential endpoint and nonisothermal transfer as chemical scenarios. See [Forster et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.polymdegradstab.2015.01.028), [NIST original](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=916161).

The current [Dyneema portfolio](https://www.dyneema.com/design-with-dyneema/dyneema-product-portfolio) supports SK99 42.5 cN/dtex, giving 4.1225 GPa at the assumed 970 kg/m³. The patent explicitly gives SK99 7e-6 and DM20 1.3e-6 percent per second at 900 MPa and 30°C; the exponents were visually checked. DM20 tenacity is 32 cN/dtex. These manufacturer example claims establish neither a safe stress nor rupture duration. See [US20180112336A1](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/6e/89/bd/f4165a0c295be7/US20180112336A1.pdf), PDF page 12, paragraphs 0099/0106/0107.

The frozen DuPont Teijin packaging sheet supports a 12 μm OD3 metallized Mylar coupon with WVTR <1 g/m²/day at 38°C/90% RH and oxygen transmission .5 cc/m²/day at 25°C/45% RH/100% oxygen. The owner's 1 g limit is an upper envelope at source conditions, not a climate-adjusted installed rate. Foil gauge, white-coat optical performance, seam mass and flex life are assumptions. Original: sources/barrier-flex-pack-films-2001.pdf, inherited from the pinned CHECK034 source copy.

The water saturation coefficients match the manufacturer's formulation with enhancement factor set to one. Exterior moist-air enhancement and below-freezing ice convention are separate choices; the owner declares supercooled-water use. See [Buck CR-2 manual](https://www.hygrometers.com/wp-content/uploads/CR-2-users-manual-2009-12.pdf), appendix 1, printed page 20. Bulk water pressure does not identify fibre water activity.
