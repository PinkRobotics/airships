## Method and checks

This seat's own code, all pinned:
- **model49.py:** 040's model.py with the face, core, bond, seam, core conductivity, finish, primer and material water as inputs, and CHECK-040's cure-in-global switch. 048's water and pressure terms are ported verbatim.
- **thermal49.py:** 040's thermal.py with the core conductivity, the finish optics and 048's day-or-night convention as inputs.
- **laminate.py**, **acct49.py**, **src49.py**, **run49.py** (040's coating), **sens49.py** and **extra49.py** (040's coating), **fold49.py** (048's finish and water, and CHECK-040's rows) and **card49.py**.

040's model and data were copied into base040/ with their sha256 pins and not changed. Every sourced value is drawn from the sourcing files by property and product, and asserted.

**Checks:**
- **040:** all 12 heavy-pad, vented rows at R 15-30, ISA and optimistic, actual and floor. Least set and usage within 2e-8, endpoint within 1e-3 kg.
- **048:** 8 beryllium rows bit for bit. They cover both finishes, all three drying routes, both joining conventions, both weathers, dry exterior and R 10-15. 048's beryllium finish screen is reproduced to 1e-9.
- **CHECK-040:** all 26 cure-in-global and edge/scarf rows, and both 1 mm rows (-594.989 and -820.787 kg), within 0.0006 kg.
- **Thermal tables:** 048's multilayer and classical tables at R 15, and 040's table, reproduced exactly.
- **The laminate code:** returns an isotropic lamina unchanged, and a T300-like quasi-isotropic modulus of 54.7 GPa.

**How the runs were made.** The standing simjob order and COMMON.md's two new lines ask for heavy runs to go through simjob, retrying a refusal unchanged. Removing exactly those two lines from COMMON.md gives back its dispatched sha256.
- **Before they were read:** run49 and a first fold run had gone bare (05:33 to 05:45, 13 threads, about 1 GB).
- **The pinned results:** every one comes from runs through simjob, retried at least a minute apart after memory-pressure refusals. The scope records are in RESULTS.json.
- **Bare and simjob runs agree:** they give identical numbers.

Stop and file. The robotics seat sends the next job. NFI.json carries the open rows. A checker runs behind this card.
