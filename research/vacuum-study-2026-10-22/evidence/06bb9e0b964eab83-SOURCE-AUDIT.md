# CHECK-059 primary-source audit

Primary downloads are pinned in SOURCE-PINS.json. Owner sourcing JSON/Markdown is preserved as attributed evidence, not silently promoted to independent verification.

**Acrylic calibration.** Leonard and Vandewalle report an 80×80 mm acrylic plate, contact angles 78/48 degrees and retained mass 0.35±0.08 g, summarized as about 54 g/m². The experimental substrate reaches roughly 45°C under forced warm-air condensation. Independent unit conversion gives 54.6875±12.5 g/m². Calibrating the stated Furmidge model to the rounded 54 reproduces its multiplier. A single population mean does not validate transfer to outdoor rain or other finishes. [Primary paper, pp. 2 and 7–8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.12736).

**Silicone angles and Furmidge.** The silicone paper distinguishes static contact angle, contact-angle hysteresis and sliding angle. Its favorable coating has 105±1°, 4±2° and 5±2°, respectively. The owner's assumed 5° hysteresis is within that reported interval, but sliding angle and hysteresis are distinct observables. Supplement A supplies the pinning-force relation and contact-diameter geometry; it supports the equation form, not a universal areal-water law. [Primary paper and supplement](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.11750).

**PTFE/FEP transfer.** The PTFE paper supports an advancing angle near 115° and discusses hysteresis; it does not make a particular FEP coating's receding angle a measured PTFE value. The 115/105 pair remains a cross-material scenario. ITO 30/10 and paint 40/15 advancing/receding pairs remain assumptions; the cited ITO measurement is static. [PTFE primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.03876).

**Two-layer coating.** The manufacturer's first application step specifies 13–25 μm dry bottom coat. Its next step applies the top coat using the same guidelines. The coverage statement applies to top and bottom products. Counting a single layer does not represent the specified system. Under the owner's assumed dry density, two layers give R10 mass 32.67–94.25 kg. Actual cured mass and optics still need measurement. [Manufacturer application guide, p. 1](https://spillcontainment.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Ultra-Ever-Dry-Instructions.pdf).

**Coating price.** The seller's retrieved page lists USD 7,685.90 and labels the product 50 gallons; its descriptive text mentions a 55-gallon drum. We retain the owner's 50-gallon assumption for the arithmetic and flag the inconsistent listing. Applying that price to both products is still an assumption: the two-layer estimate becomes USD 16,861.93 at R10 and USD 151,757.35 at R30, excluding labor and installation. No purchase or supplier contact occurred. [Seller's own listing](https://spill911.com/products/ultratech-4008).

**Heater mass.** Minco's guide gives a typical polyimide heater areal mass of 0.04 g/cm². Conversion to 0.4 kg/m² yields 502.654825 kg at R10 and 4523.893421 kg at R30. This checks the film-only mass, not the complete power/control system. Film alone exceeds the R10 payload margin; it does not exceed either R30 ISA endpoint margin. [Manufacturer design guide, p. 6 / PDF page 7](https://www.minco.com/wp-content/uploads/Minco-Thermal-Solutions-Design-Guide.pdf).

**Evaporation comparison.** FAO-56 Example 19 gives 0.63 mm/h for reference grass at 38°C, 52% RH, 3.3 m/s and 2.450 MJ/m²/h solar radiation. It includes a reference surface resistance and ground heat flux. The owner's approximately 0.72 kg/m²/h sun-facing wet-element result is a different model, so proximity is a plausibility check, not validation. [FAO-56 Example 19](https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e08.htm).

**Inherited evidence and remaining gaps.** The 0.08 mm PVC rain scenario, 0.8 mm leaf cap, field dew yields, drainage law transfer from laboratory liquids, cover fabrics, blower power, coating lifetime and historical hangar figures remain attributed to the owner's pinned source rows. No new installed-hull claim is based on them. The independent web request for the cited inflatable-hangar listing returned an error; its RUB 3,848,256 figure is inherited, not independently price-verified. Floor scaling to RUB 4,925,767.68 at R10 is arithmetic only and does not solve the stated 7.5 m supplier height versus a 20 m hull diameter. The full-size retained-water and drying experiments remain necessary.
