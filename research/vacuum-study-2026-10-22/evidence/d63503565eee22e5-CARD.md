# MAINLINE054 — P3 dry cavity

**QUESTIONABLE / PROJECTION. No installed hull, barrier service interval, adsorbent system or material life is qualified.**

A better barrier is the first useful change. At the all-favourable052 R10 ISA endpoint, a one-day flight can use the metallised12um product envelope with only0.15kg additional barrier mass. Seven days with the colder humidity screen can use HX plus a projected3A cartridge system, but only1.12kg payload remains. Thirty days with that same commercial-barrier/desiccant approach is55.43kg overweight at R10. A larger radius or a substantially better verified thin barrier is needed.

All numbers below retain052's favourable frame and film assumptions. The original least-root account has zero payload; it does not inherit the30.86kg headroom used in the endpoint comparisons.

## 3. Regenerable3A capture

The GSTA source at333K/17.497Pa gives8.623wt%equilibrium uptake. After1wt%initial water and20%utilisation reserve,6.099wt%remains. Use**6wt%net working capacity**, plus10%extra water-load margin. Bed temperature must stay≤333K. Headspace vapour is not credited in bed sizing. [Lin et al., measured3A isotherms](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1311254)

The system includes dry beads, housing max(1kg,25%of bead mass),2kg monitoring,3kg distribution/mounts and0.5W monitoring battery at108Wh/kg usable. These are explicit installation allowances. External water remains aboard; it uses the existing gas reserve before any incremental mass is added. Material outgassing is not counted twice.

**Transport is an open requirement.** The R10 seven-day SiOx case needs about277m3/h effective water conductance at a7.21Pa driving difference, and16.54W initial peak adsorption heat rejection. An ideal emissivity0.8/view-factor1 radiator to321.94K would need0.235m2; real coupling, flow resistance and cartridge dust filters are unverified. These functions must fit within the charged distribution/housing allowance. A bulk isotherm does not prove local PBO dryness.

Swap and regenerate cartridges on the ground, outside PBO. The source laboratory used230C/8h/<0.5Pa; manufacturer guidance supports300–350C vacuum removal of residual water. Our350C estimate includes heating bed/hardware plus4.4MJ/kgwater desorption, then doubles the ideal energy for losses. Ground pump power/cooldown are not measured. Onboard heating alone would put the water back into the cavity. [Merck regeneration guidance](https://www.merckmillipore.com/deepweb/assets/sigmaaldrich/product/documents/106/819/br3537en-mk.pdf)
