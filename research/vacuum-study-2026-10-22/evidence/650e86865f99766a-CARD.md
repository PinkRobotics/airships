# MAINLINE-025 — SiC arcs: strength, reliability and manufacture

Unit `vacuum-mainline025-sic-arc-reality-1020`. All inherited and sourced material/capability values are **QUESTIONABLE** for this structure. Unvalidated transfers and proposed structures are **PROJECTION**. This is a completed estimate card with open qualification issues in `NFI.json`; nothing here is a certification.

**Decision.** The amended equilateral equilibrium gives **0.824658 GPa mean compression**, versus **1.428350 GPa** in the inherited two-way force formula. Both are scored below. A probability-qualified compression allowable for the proposed tube has **not been established**. The 3.9 GPa value is a typical compression property, not a demonstrated allowable for roughly 1,046 thin, two-metre arcs. With a separate local-wall knockdown and re-optimization, that unqualified input gives conditional pressure multiplier **1.20571 equilateral / 1.07575 inherited**. A 380 MPa compression proxy gives **0.45120 / 0.26050**. Flexural strength does not supply the missing compression distribution; none of these multipliers is a measured probability of survival.

**Reproduction and mass.** The pinned 011b SiC point is reproduced independently: spacing 2.04 m, OD 247.3767 mm, wall 0.880299 mm, 1,046.0204 continuum members, and SF **1.225527946881**. Geometry cannot remove an arbitrarily low compressive strength. Since total tube length is \(8\pi\sqrt3R^2/s\), inherited mean compression is \(4\pi\sqrt3\rho pR^3/M_\mathrm{tube}\); equilateral demand divides it by \(\sqrt3\). Strength-only SF cannot exceed \(F_c\) divided by that bound; bending and instability reduce it.

**Equilibrium amendment.** The 011b triangular stiffness was combined with \(N=pRs/2\). For a uniform equilateral network, each of three member families has line density \(2/(\sqrt3s)\), and the direction factors sum to 3/2. Therefore membrane resultant is \(N_x=\sqrt3N/s\); sphere equilibrium \(N_x=pR/2\) gives **\(N=pRs/(2\sqrt3)\)**. This independently derived correction agrees with the pinned amendment `ord-admiral-rr2-mainline025-demand-082gpa-1020`. Both force rules are now scored throughout the principal comparisons. Global stiffness and film formulas remain inherited; MAINLINE-022b owns the complete discrete topology and load-collection reconciliation. Arbitrary extra arch rise does not reduce the equilibrium force and can introduce bending about the thrust line.

**Straightness and curved shape.** An actual Silit SK catalogue gives ≤5 mm centerline deflection at lengths ≤2 m and ≤7 mm at ≤3.5 m for different, smaller, thicker straight products. It does not verify the assumed 2.04 mm deviation from this intended arc. The specified R10 m arc has roughly **52 mm intentional sag**, depending on whether s denotes arc or chord. Cold elastically bending a finished straight target tube would require about **1.237% outer-fiber strain**, or 5.07 GPa in the inherited linear SiC model—13.35× the typical 380 MPa flexure. This diagnoses a forming assumption, not residual stress in a stress-free manufactured arc. Net-shape curved formation, deposition on a shaped removable form, or a reanalyzed segmented design is required. [Scoped dimensional/deflection table, p.23](https://www.saint-gobain.co.jp/jp/download-documents/kataroku/ceramics-systems-kilns-furnaces-en-a4-web.pdf).

**Film and float coupling.** All optima retain the inherited lift ceiling.
