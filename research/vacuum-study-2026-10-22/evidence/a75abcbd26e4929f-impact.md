## Low-velocity impact (BVID)
- NASA/TP-2017-219635: about 10.8 J gave BVID, 32-ply [45/0/-45/90]4S IM7/epoxy, 12.7 mm tup (p20/40)
- Garcia-Moreno 2019: 20 J chosen for BVID, 16-ply 4 mm M21E/IMA [0/90/+-45]2s
- Liu 2024: critical energy 45 J (failure mode change), 5.7 mm T700 woven laminate

## CAI
- Barile 2019, 5 mm laminate, 50 J, 16 mm tup: damaged -202 +-15 MPa vs undamaged -385 +-20 MPa (only row with both)
- NASA TP 2017: CAI 269-452 MPa at about 10.8 J across seven resins (Table 4); OHC 324-346 MPa
- Garcia-Moreno 2019: 214.47 MPa non-aged at 20 J (4 mm)
- Liu 2024: 264.37 MPa at 25 J falling to 165.62 MPa at 125 J (37 % lower than the 25 J value, not vs undamaged)

## Tubes
- Mack and Tan, ASC 2023: UT3 (ID 76.2 mm) and UT4 (ID 101.6 mm) unidirectional CFRP tubes, 300 mm long, 16 mm tup; both punctured at 20 J; UT3 10 J high-mass case lost about 20 kN peak axial load; UT4 15 J no significant peak-force loss. Wall thickness only in Figure 1.
- Xiao 2020 (Materials 13, 4143): filament-wound T800 tube (ID 70 mm, about 3.0 mm wall, 100 mm long), 15 J, 2 kg, 12.5 mm tup: RADIAL residual compression ratio 88.1 % (T800), 90.7 % (T700); sharp force drop near 1250 N. Radial, not axial.
