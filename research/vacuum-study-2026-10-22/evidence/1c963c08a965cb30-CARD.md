## The answer in four lines

- **P2 floats on paper on two optimisms.** These are a gamma between 2.3 and 3.0 times CR-1457's 0.20, and a wrinkling
  coefficient between 1.1 and 1.8 times 0.33. With none of the named optimisms the hull is at 0.34 to 0.43.
- **P3's best filed number is out of model.** 022b gives 1.375, but at s 6.15 m on a 40-node frame, where the smeared
  global formula does not apply. Inside the band where it does apply, P3 is about 1.13, and only on a film material that
  does not exist.
- **The cheapest retirements are computational.** One sandwich-gamma finite-element run and one discrete finite-element
  run of the P3 frame, both CPU only.
- **The most mass sits in the P2 seams and bonds.** Seams and bonds run from 214 to 968 kg against a 216 kg allowance.

## 4. Ground first

These are ranked by optimism retired per unit of effort.

1. **Sandwich gamma near Vc 1, by finite element.** One imperfect, geometrically nonlinear sandwich-sphere run at the
   021 point (Vc 1.06), and one at 021b (Vc 0.89), on CPU only. 018b sized such a run at 12.6 GiB for R/tc 40, and the
   brief's 029 is that FE; it has not filed. It retires O1, the factor of 2.3 to 3.0 that carries all of P2.
2. **The P3 frame as a discrete structure.** One geometrically nonlinear buckling run, with ccx and gmsh, at s 2.2 m
   and 6.15 m. These tools are installed, and 026 is already solving member forces with them. It settles R055 and R057:
   whether 022b's 1.375 and the two-way 0.986 survive.
3. **020's weighed, curved, sealed two-skin joint and core panel, with measured load transfer.** This retires seam and
   bond mass (214 to 968 kg) and joint efficiency (0.6 to 0.9).
4. **An SR-200 thin-sheet specification from the supplier.** It needs the largest sheet at 0.7 to 0.85 mm, a gauge map
   and post-form compression. It retires O3 (255 to 379 MPa) and O4 (0.914 against 3.13 m). This is the least work, but
   it means contacting a supplier, which is outward-facing and needs the Admiral's authorization.
5. **A wrinkling coupon on Be over CR-III 5056 3/8-1.0.** It retires O2 (0.37 to 0.61 needed against 0.33).

P3's film is a material gap, not a test: no sourced film reaches a specific strength 13 times Kapton's.
