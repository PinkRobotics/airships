The 27 supplied non-code artifact pins all matched. Five executable paths were deliberately excluded.

The [3M AF 163-2 datasheet](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/282041O/3m-scotch-weld-structural-adhesive-film-af-163-2-af-163-3.pdf), November 2009, supports the family's alternative 200 °F cure. Its detailed 2/4/6-hour results are **AF 163-2K, 0.060 lb/ft², metal-to-metal**, at 50 psi; the hull uses 2U at 0.015 lb/ft² on honeycomb. The prose singles out six hours for useful low-temperature performance. Treat the 2U process as a candidate requiring transfer evidence. The creep entry is 0.0002 inch at 180 °F, 800 psi, 192 hours; this is a displacement result, not proof of zero service creep or a measured stress-relaxation fraction. See PDF pages 11, 15 and 19.

The [HexBond 679 datasheet](https://www.hexcel.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/HexBond679TDSv12.pdf), page 1, distinguishes 250 g/m² resin from a 30 g/m² support. The product is supported film. Counting resin alone understates two full interfaces by 75.3982236862 kg at R=10 m. Pages 1–4 give the 70/80 °C schedules and typical bonding data. No Be assembly qualification follows from these data.

The [Hexcel CR-III datasheet](https://www.hexcel.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/HexWeb_CRIII_DataSheet.pdf), Table II, gives the 5056 3/8–1.0 row: L shear 55/45 psi typical/minimum and 15 ksi modulus; W 35/25 psi and 6.8 ksi. Its stabilized compression is 60/35 psi, relevant to transferring the 50 psi low-cure test cycle: typical/minimum factors would be 1.2/0.7 if the core directly carries that pressure. Tooling and load path therefore matter. The temperature card's 25 psi W value is a minimum average from the supplier table, not a statistical design allowable for the cured hull.

The [Hexcel design guide](https://www.hexcel.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Honeycomb_Sandwich_Design_Technology.pdf), printed page 20, assigns the panel's in-plane forces mainly to the faces. The supplied NASA CR-1457, Introduction §1.1, says the same. [NASA TM-4585](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19940029198/downloads/19940029198.pdf), after equation 7, neglects core thermal resultants in its analysis. These support face-dominated modeling; none proves the proposed local bond survives its cure, or supplies the new edge equation.

[NASA/TM-2018-219753](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20180001568/downloads/20180001568.pdf), printed page 4 / PDF page 8, Figure 2, was inspected as a page image. It gives 8.2 Msi and 33 ksi for 5052-H39 foil. The card uses rounded SI values and transfers the yield to 5056. Its 67 GPa / 2700 kg/m³ pair came from a secondary 5056-H14 bulk register, not a primary 5056-H39 foil test verified here. Those assumptions remain explicit.

The [Hexcel attributes guide](https://www.hexcel.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/HexWebHoneycombAttributesandProperties.pdf), printed page 18, labels the quoted expansion coefficients as **thickness-direction** values. The card's use of those numbers in-plane remains a projection. The [HFT](https://www.hexcel.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/HFT_us.pdf), [HRP](https://www.hexcel.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/HRP_us.pdf) and [HRH-49](https://www.hexcel.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/HRH_49_us.pdf) tables support the selected densities and room-temperature mechanical input rows. SUPPLEMENT.json converts their printed units independently; it does not establish the same properties at the extremely thin failed-solve geometries.

The historical metallic densities are documented in [NASA's titanium panel report](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19770011276/downloads/19770011276.pdf), [1959 stainless-core data](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19980228047/downloads/19980228047.pdf), and [the beryllium honeycomb development report](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19730016169/downloads/19730016169.pdf). They establish historical specimens, not present procurement or matched-core strength allowables. The [TIMET 3-2.5 sheet](https://www.timet.com/documents/datasheets/alpha-and-beta-alloys/timetal-3-2.5.pdf) and [17-7 PH supplier sheet](https://www.upmet.com/sites/default/files/products/datasheet/17-7-ph-datasheet.pdf) support the stated bulk-alloy expansion coefficients, over their specified temperature intervals.

[Henkel EA 9394](https://datasheets.tdx.henkel.com/LOCTITE-EA-9394-AERO-en_US.pdf) supports the 25 °C cure class, mixed density 1.36 g/ml and 4,200 psi aluminum lap-shear example. It does not supply the honeycomb fillet mass assumed equal to AF film here.

[Dokania and Kruit's RTV 566 study](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_b204370a-5022-454b-8345-76186627a2c2), PDF page 3, reports 0.5/0.64/0.635 MPa for 95/142/190 µm layers. Applying its modulus to 25 µm or millimetre-scale sandwich layers is a transfer. The [Momentive datasheet, manufacturer PDF hosted by Curbell](https://www.curbellplastics.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/RTV566-Data-Sheet.pdf), supports the 1.49 base-compound specific gravity and 465 psi primed-Al lap-shear figure. It does not establish a Be/core interlayer.
