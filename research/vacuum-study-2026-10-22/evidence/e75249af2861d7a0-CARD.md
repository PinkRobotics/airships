## Plain reading

The cheapest work is not a stronger material. On every P2 leader the two modes that meet are shear crimping and wrinkling, and the single lever that lifts both after re-optimisation is a lighter face at the same stiffness and strength, by about 1.46 to 1.65. That face does not exist today. The one lever that exists today, in theoretical form, is the wrinkling coefficient on the CNT point. Gc, the property the governing mode names, is a trap alone: past Vc 2 the handbook hands the point back to kd 0.20. P3 is closer than P2 in ordinary materials, needing only about 1.31 in tube strength or density, and its ceramic leader is already past 1 on the modes scored so far.
