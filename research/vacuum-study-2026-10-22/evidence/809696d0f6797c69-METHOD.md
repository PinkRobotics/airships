# MAINLINE-050 method and reproduction

All material/source values are QUESTIONABLE; all transfers, weather profiles and computed performance are PROJECTION. Radius means radius, not diameter. Qualified installed-hull SF and qualified operational availability remain null.

The implementation imports no predecessor module. It reproduces 048's chosen multilayer CCAg/ITO, bake70, retained joining floor and the 037b 200°F cure/scarf fix. The original files are copied and hash checked. `model50.py` uses their declared equations and material inputs. `REGRESSION50.json` reproduces R10 ISA payload to 0.00014 kg; the small difference is mainly the rounded 0.514444 m/s knot conversion. The inherited thermal values agree. The `es_water` docstring's 1981 attribution is corrected here to the 1996 Buck update, as verified in [NCAR's equation reference](https://www.eol.ucar.edu/data-software/conventions-and-standards/water-vapor-pressure-formulations).

The surface is sea level. At centre altitude z, T=288.15−0.0065z+ΔT and p_surface=101325+100Δp_hPa. Local e=RH es_water(T). A constant vapor mole fraction through the column gives p=p_surface(T/T_surface)^[g(1−0.378 e/p)/(Rd L)]; twelve fixed-point updates solve its implicit p. Density=(p−0.378e)/(Rd T). RH is defined over liquid water, including below freezing. The altitudes are treated as geopotential; the handbook's geometric altitudes differ slightly. Surface elevation, vertical humidity structure and real-gas/enhancement corrections are assumptions, not sourced soundings.

The actual displaced-air mass is rho V. No additional fixed weather debit is charged: doing both would count the lost lift twice. The pressure design load is max(1.01 p_500ISA,p_local)+max(q70,q35+gust), with the inherited reference-density wind pressures. The 70-knot branch governs. This holds the mechanical 048 account constant while changing ambient pressure; it does not validate a weather-dependent mooring system.

The complete bill retains two Be faces, core, max(actual joining, 5% of faces+core), coating, structural primer, legacy 040 pad, 1% of original ISA lift as gas reserve, 100 kg systems, exterior water 0.1 kg/m², contamination and post-bake extra absorbed water. Scarf, splice, film and seal are counted separately before the joining maximum. Internal released inventory changes gas pressure; it is not added again as new vehicle mass. The bake projection does not remove exterior water.

Each mask selects endpoints for gamma, Q, coupled face compression/E, and film (bits 1,2,4,8). Mask 11 is the 048 primary endpoint; all sixteen subsets are computed to identify the fewest optimistic axes. An integer bitset stores all masks tied for that minimum cardinality: bit m means endpoint mask m is feasible. It is not itself a material mask. Common logarithmic usage is solved separately for the least ISA subsets. A full-grid unique usage tie-break is not claimed. `LEAST-SET-TRANSITIONS.json` supplies intervals; each of the sixteen zero surfaces is also a set-feasibility transition surface.

For a given core thickness, forty-seven face-thickness bisections find the lightest passing skin. A 640-point global log core scan identifies separate local minima; the best three are refined. Selected comparisons use 1500 or 3000 core points. Pressure curves have 65 knots for the fixed-thermal comparison. The coupled extension has 17 pressure knots and approximately 2 K air-temperature spacing, extra points around the cold interface boundary, and the exact reference temperature. Feasible designs include every inherited screen, including interface stress and scarf stress. Infinite mass in NPZ means infeasible or an unresolved cold-front interpolation interval, not a physical mass prediction.

The first map freezes the 048 thermal state at 284.9 K. The second recomputes the same dry-skin energy balance with air temperature and the two sky temperatures shifted together: day sky=Tair−9.9 K, night sky=Tair−24.9 K. Solar is 970 W/m², wind 2 m/s, absorptance .09 and outer emittance .8. The two skins exchange heat through the projected core conduction and internal radiation; Legendre modes 2–6 retain the inherited thermal-curvature screen. The 1.002 guard is retained. This supplies a conditional thermal weather extension, not independent cloud/solar/wind probabilities.

The primary maps are design families: each weather point may select a different geometry. `FIXED-HULLS.json` also supplies one unchanging hull per radius, designed at the grid's maximum pressure load (103953.1978 Pa) and the first fully feasible cold temperature. This conservative pressure/temperature combination covers the declared rectangular range. Its full bill is fixed and its pressure/thermal screens are evaluated throughout the grid. Original ISA-built geometries are also checked in `ENVELOPE-SUMMARY.json`; their pressure capacity cannot be silently increased when they descend.

The [MIL-HDBK-310 design data](https://chet-aero.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/mil-hdbk-310.pdf) are treated as separate climatic markers. `DESIGN-DAYS.json` distinguishes sourced quantities from assumed pressure, lapse, humidity profile and coincident temperature. Hot and low-pressure markers outside the requested grid are calculated explicitly, not clipped. The high-absolute-humidity marginal gets conditional temperature brackets; the Abadan hot-humid pair has its own joint definition. No independent marginal probabilities are multiplied or reinterpreted as local annual availability.

The nocturnal extension follows the coupled energy and mass-balance approach in [Dong et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.05757), equations 1–3. Exterior sky/ground view factors are (1+mu)/2 and (1−mu)/2. Convection supplies sensible heat and vapor; mass flux is max(e_air−es_surface,0) h (Rd/Rv) Le^(−2/3)/(cp_air p). Le=.87. The chosen large-sphere convection correlation is inherited, not experimentally validated on this hull.

The transient has separate inner and outer heat capacities. Outer enthalpy accounts for deposited water, ice and fusion at 273.15 K; vapor brings its enthalpy into the surface balance. Thus condensation warms the surface and frost is determined by the coupled state, not the dry-surface minimum. Both skins begin at ambient with no deposited water. Internal radiative redistribution integrates to zero. All deposition is retained. No prior-film evaporation, rain/fog impaction, shedding or moving weather front is included. Base integration is 5 s / 64 zones for 12 h, checked at 2.5 s / 128 zones. Room-temperature heat capacities are rounded planning approximations; [NIST Be](https://janaf.nist.gov/tables/Be-002.html) and [NIST Al](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C7429905&Mask=42) show the real temperature dependence.

Total exterior-water capacity is 0.1+(rho V−M_with_0.1water)/A. Thus a dry-start night replaces the reserved film, while a physically preexisting film must be added to the new deposition. The latter comparison is arithmetic, not a re-solved wet-start transient. `DEW-ACCOUNT.csv` uses each night's actual density. The familiar 048 ISA thresholds are also retained for comparison. Cold-night buoyancy credit never overrides a failed thermal screen.

Heating costs are the positive spatial heat deficit needed to maintain a uniform dewpoint-plus-margin setpoint, also above freezing, plus any startup heating. Battery mass uses the inherited 150 Wh/kg × .8 usable × .9 conversion =108 Wh/kg. Heater/cable/support mass is unknown. Low-e=.05 and 10–50 g/m² cover cases are technology/mass sensitivities with no demonstrated installed mechanism. Solar-only morning dry-out uses .09×970/4 W/m² over the whole sphere; losses and air heat exchange mean it is not an elapsed-time prediction.

The source data, exact scripts, grids and receipts are all in the manifest. From this directory, with the same available Python/NumPy/Matplotlib environment, the numerical sequence is `regress50.py`, `envelope50.py`, `dew50.py`, `coupled50.py`, `analyze50.py`, `fixed50.py`, `verify_plot50.py`. Every numerical job was launched through `unpublished-local-ref:6b66f99ebd630954 run`; the exact resource requests and exits are in the logs. Only `coupled50.py` uses four processes (4 CPUs/6 GiB); the others use 1 CPU/2–3 GiB. The 7200-second guard is the host's per-job rule, not a task time budget. One initial exit-75 refusal was preserved, followed by explicit queue retry authority and an admitted unchanged retry. No seat was messaged, woken or restored out of band.
