## Geometry and line loading

Frequency 6 is nearest s=2.04 m: 362 joints, 1,080 curved members, 720 cells,
mean arc 2.001344 m (range
1.627468–2.170540 m).
Exact inherited SiC section: outer radius 123.688362 mm, wall 0.880299 mm,
E=410 GPa. Primary-sheet nu=0.14 sets torsion; its newer E=430 GPa is not substituted.

Each equilateral film cell transfers one third of its resultant to each edge.
Two adjoining cells give **w = ps/(2sqrt(3))**, versus 011b's local-member ps/2.
The actual unequal curved boundaries use a closed centroid-fan pressure potential
+lambda pV at p=95460.8379139 Pa. Its gradient supplies the member line loads and its
Hessian the pressure follower stiffness. Film material stiffness is omitted.

## Pinned joints

The 3-segment ball-pin model has 20,526 physical DOF and 1,080
independent member-roll coordinates in addition to six body motions. Rotating
one curved member about its endpoint chord preserves its elastic shape.
Sample finite rolls of 0.05 rad have at most
9.39e-22 J of computed elastic energy;
all member roll pressure-volume curvatures are negative. Several also have
nonzero initial pressure torque. Thus zero-mode entries describe mechanisms,
not a solved positive buckling reserve. Attached-film or anti-roll joint stiffness
could change this result. See mechanism [source link unavailable in this public projection].

## Nonlinear elastic instability

Each row uses a separate stress-free reference geometry. Node offsets are seeded
radial errors; member crookedness is sinusoidal in seeded transverse directions;
the eigenmode case uses the same primary m6 eigenvector field on both meshes.
Newton equilibrium is checked, followed by a sign-changing tangent-stiffness
bracket. Soft-mode displacement control resolves local-mode limits.
