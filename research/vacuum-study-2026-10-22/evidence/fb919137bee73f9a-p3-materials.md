# 017b P3 materials: history and limits (sourced 2026-10-20)

Every sourced value is QUESTIONABLE. The 0/90 Zylon film is a PROJECTION. Rows, quotes and URLs are in p3-materials.json (75 rows, 12 limits).

## Sintered SiC (Hexoloy SA class)
- Flexural strength has not moved. 1988: 331-402 MPa. 1995: 395. 1997 NIST evaluation: 359. Saint-Gobain 2004, 2012, 2024, 2026: 380. Enhanced SA reached 428 MPa (m = 12) by 2004.
- Modulus is flat at 415-430 GPa. The 410 versus 430 split is inside one vendor: web page 410, 2012 sheet and 2024 brochure 430.
- Compressive 3900 MPa is a vendor figure unchanged since 2004. Sandia 2005 (hot-pressed SiC-N) measured about 3800, one specimen 3988. The 2024 brochure prints 80 ksi in that row, a typesetting error.
- Weibull modulus is 8-12 on vendor sheets, 9 in 1995 flexure but 5 in tension. Hot-pressed grades reach 17-26.
- Gains exist only in other processes: hot-pressed 580-750 MPa (1994-2003), CVD 415 MPa (2019).
- No design compressive allowable found; NASA CARES/Life leaves compression to "other methods".

## Limits
Ideal strength of beta-SiC is 50.8 GPa (calculation); bulk SiC is at 0.75 percent of it. SiC fibre is 3.5 GPa (2019), up from 3.0-3.3 in 1998. The gap is flaws, not physics.

## Films
- Kapton H: 179-211 MPa (1975 NASA test), 231 (2017, 2019). Mylar: 196 (1999), 190 (2022).
- Upilex-S 25 um: 520 MPa at 1.47 g/cm3 (2022), 354 kN*m/kg against Kapton's 163. Strongest real film found.
- Fibres: Zylon 5.8 GPa (3718 kN*m/kg), Dyneema SK99 4.1 GPa (4250). UHMWPE tape 3.6 GPa (2012 patent example). No PBO or UHMWPE film found.
- CNT sheet 175 kN*m/kg (2005); Miralon yarn 911 (2020). Graphene paper 1.1 GPa (2020). GO film 417 MPa (2025).
- Limits: PE chain about 20 GPa (fibre at 15-20 percent); CNT bundle 80 GPa (lab); graphene 130 GPa (disputed).

## End fittings
- Ti-6Al-4V: minimum yield 827 MPa annealed, 1068 aged, 4.42 g/cm3 (TIMET 2000). Announced 1954.
- Carbon composite compression: 1470 MPa (T300, 2002 and 2026), 1490 (T800S), 1870 (T1100G). Fibre tensile doubled; compression rose 27 percent.

## Gaps
No sintered-SiC datasheet at 1996 or 2016 (Wayback returned HTTP 429). No 1990s CVD values. No theoretical PBO strength. No primary introduction year for Upilex-S, Teonex or Zylon.
