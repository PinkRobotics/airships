# MAINLINE-049: AlBeMet and beryllium supply (sourced values, 87 rows in albemet.json; all tagged QUESTIONABLE)
- AM162 = Al-62 wt% Be (spec 60-64 wt% Be) [Materion datasheet MAAB-017-1015; AM162H spec Rev B 2018].
- Rolled sheet minimum: yield 276 MPa (40 ksi), UTS 379 MPa (55 ksi), elongation 5 % [datasheet]; AMS 7913 minimum UTS 386 MPa (56 ksi) [Materion Guide to Lightweight Materials, Table 4.3, p.13].
- Rolled sheet typical (593 C/24 h): yield 314 MPa, UTS 413 MPa, 7 % [datasheet]; the Guide prints 324 / 427 MPa / 7 % [Guide Table 4.3]. Orientation not stated.
- Elastic modulus: sheet 193 GPa, extrusion 202, HIP 197 (typical) [datasheet]. NASA tensile tests on extrusion gave 172-180 GPa L [NASA/TM-2002-212049 Table 1].
- Density 2.071 g/cm3 (form table) / 2.10 (comparison table); Poisson 0.17; CTE 13.9 ppm/C at 25 C; k 210 W/m K [datasheet].
- Sheet thickness "supplied" 0.254-6.35 mm [Guide p.12]; maximum sheet width/length not found.
- Compressive yield: not printed anywhere opened. Fatigue: extruded only, ~207 MPa transverse rotating beam R=-1 [Materion Avionics brochure].
- SR-200 Be sheet (reference): min yield 345 MPa, UTS 483 MPa, 10 % elong.; E 290 GPa, density 1.85, CTE 11.3 ppm/C (25-100 C), k 216; typical in-plane 414/538 MPa, 20 % [SR-200 data sheet 2024; Guide Table 4.5]. Sheet 0.059-0.250 in, max 24 x 72 in [SR-200 sheet].
- World Be mine production (t Be content): 433 (2024), 430 (2025e); US 230 both years [USGS MCS2026]. MCS2025 had 360 for 2024e: the figure was revised.
- US apparent consumption 2025e 230 t, valued about $360 M [USGS MCS2026]. Be-Cu master alloy unit value 1,600 USD/kg contained Be (2025e), 1,500 (2024), 1,400 (2023), 660 (2022), 680 (2021) [USGS MCS2026]; this is not a metal or sheet price.
- Materion Utah: 391,000 lb Be produced in 2025 (= 177 t), 61 % of mill capacity (implies ~291 t/yr by my arithmetic) [Materion 10-K FY2025]; ore reserves 40.88 million lb Be (~18,500 t) [10-K].
- Materion says it increased Utah capacity; no tonnage given [Materion news item 1/1/2024].
