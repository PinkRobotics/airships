Unit vacuum-mainline022-p3-optimistic-floating-point-1020. Every row is QUESTIONABLE or PROJECTION. Nothing here is a certification.

This seat is the integrator. MAINLINE-023 owns the nodes and the joint mass, MAINLINE-024 owns the film, and MAINLINE-025 owns the SiC allowable and whether the tube can be made. None of those three had filed a report when this run started. The carried values are 011b's: joints at 5 percent (PENDING-023), the 1.5 GPa film at 970 kg/m3 (PENDING-024), and the 3900 MPa crush as the tube allowable (PENDING-025). A ceramic-arc frame should take k 0.2. Wright's 0.4 is a modified factor from a 1965 steel-lattice table, and a brittle arc has no plasticity to redistribute. Scoring k 0.4 is an optimism of factor 2. Both values are QUESTIONABLE. This knockdown judgment is this seat's. It is not pending on 023, 024 or 025.
