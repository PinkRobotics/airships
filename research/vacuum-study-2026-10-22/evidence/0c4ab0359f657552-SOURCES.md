The legacy Toray sheet lists 1,470 MPa compression for its 250°F epoxy composite normalized to 60% fiber volume. Its fiber density is 1.80 g/cm³, which is a different quantity from composite joint density. The value used in the card is traceable; a density qualification is still missing. [Legacy Toray T700S sheet](https://www.rockwestcomposites.com/downloads/T700SDataSheet.pdf).

The current Toray sheet, revision November 24, 2025, instead lists 1,450 MPa with a different test designation, still normalized to 60% fiber volume. It identifies the values as lot averages. This is an edition/test-condition distinction, not an arithmetic error in using the explicitly inherited legacy value. [Current Toray T700S sheet](https://www.toraycma.com/wp-content/uploads/T700S-Data-Sheet.pdf).

Henkel's July 2015 bulletin reports 28.9 MPa lap shear at 25°C, tested after a five-day room-temperature cure on phosphoric-acid-anodized 2024-T3 aluminum. That evidence does not determine adhesion, peel or fatigue performance on the proposed SiC joint. [Henkel EA 9394 bulletin, p.3](https://dm.henkel-dam.com/is/content/henkel/EA-9394-Aero).

NASA's isotropic axial-cylinder equations give coefficient 1/√[3(1−ν²)] and the cited empirical γ. The guide's simplified coefficient is about 0.605 for ν=0.3. The check preserves the owner's explicit 0.6/outer-radius convention and records a separate ν=0.14/mid-radius sensitivity, with no new material qualification. [NASA SP-8007 Rev.2, equations 7–10](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20205011530/downloads/20205011530%20Rev%202FINALa%201-2023.pdf?attachment=true).
