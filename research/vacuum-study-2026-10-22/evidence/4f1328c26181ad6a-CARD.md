# MAINLINE-058: widen the band

Unit vacuum-mainline058-flyable-band-1020. Order ord-admiral-rr2-mainline058-dispatch-1019. Author: ref-55cff924be25afe6f9dd (claude-opus-5-5, max).

Every value is QUESTIONABLE or a PROJECTION; nothing here is a certification. I rebuilt 050's P2 account in my own code, and it reproduces 050's five fixed hulls exactly (mass to 0.0001 kg, every zero-payload offset). P3 comes from 052's frozen rows. No owner code was run.

**Conventions.**
- **Band:** temperatures are ISA offsets at the 100 m floor.
- **Worst corner:** 050's: −30 hPa sea-pressure anomaly with 100% RH.
- **P2 reference:** 048's primary endpoint, gamma/Q/film (mask 11).
- **P3 reference:** 052's full endpoint, S/F/L/J.
- **Days:** a day counts at the site only if all 24 hours fit the band.

## The answer

**Two levers widen the band; the rest are minor.**
- **Relief (both leads):** size the hull for the low-pressure day, then relieve pressure above it by climbing or by admitting air. The hot edge rises by 11.9–12.0 K for P2 and 6.4–6.6 K for P3.
- **Finish (P2 only):** a low-emittance finish lowers P2's cold edge (the bond interface on clear nights) by 11 K.

**P2 widens from 4.0 to 25.7 K at R10, and from 13.8 to 34.6 K at R30.** This is at the worst corner, inside the whole bill.

**At London Heathrow (2014–2023) the bands are wider than the worst corner suggests.** On real days pressure and humidity rarely coincide at their worst.

| Hull | Heathrow 24-hour days covered |
|---|---|
| 050's R10 P2 hull, as built | 34% |
| P2 R10 with relief | 64% |
| P2 R10 with relief and the finish | 84% |
| P2 R12 with relief and the finish | 96% |
| P3 R10, sized at standard pressure and climbing | 78% |
| P3 R12, the same | 97% |

**No lever reaches the MIL-HDBK-310 hot days.** On the 10% hot day at R30, P3 is 0.9 t short and P2 is 3.0–3.9 t short.

**All of this assumes flight-proven masses.** With 057's concept-stage growth allowance, nothing floats (N058-11).

## 1. The levers

Each row gives the band the lever adds (in K and hPa), its mass, energy and time, and its cost in the whole bill. Values are R10 and R30; the other radii are in RESULTS.json.

**Trim by admitting air and pumping it out.**
- **What it does:** size the hull for the −30 hPa day (98.0 kPa load instead of 103.95). Admit air whenever the load would exceed that, and on cold days to shed excess lift.
- **Band:** P2's hot edge goes from −6.5 to +5.4 K (R10) and from +3.3 to +15.4 K (R30). Above the design pressure the edge stays flat, because the admitted air caps lift there.
- **Pressure range:** there is no upper hPa limit. The lowest anomaly at which the hull floats at ISA falls from −5.4 to −50 hPa (R10).
- **Mass:** the hull is 4.5–4.7% lighter (−225 kg at R10, −6,175 kg at R30), plus a 5 kg valve.
- **Pumping out in flight is not possible.** With 034's onboard pumps a typical day's trim takes 11–17 days (E2M28) or one to one and a half years (KNF N920) to remove.
- **So pumping out is a ground job.** Heathrow flight days need a median of 7.4 kPa of air at R10 and 11.1 kPa at R30. Removing it takes 17.6–18.7 h at 052's ground speed, against 24.5 h for a full evacuation. The energy is 31 kWh (R10) to 1,120 kWh (R30) per day as an ideal minimum. Real pump sets draw several times that.
- **After hail:** 045's pre-hail vent (2.6 t of air at R10) needs 22.7 h of ground pumping.
- **P3:** the gain is smaller, −5.2 to +1.3 K (R10), because its tubes scale only as p^0.445. Admitting air also caps lift on every normal day, so at Heathrow trim alone lowers P3's coverage (62% to 57% at R10). P3's admitted air must also be dried.

**Operating altitude and climb.**
- **What it does:** the same hull climbs to where the load equals its design value, instead of admitting air.
- **Band:** P2's hot edge rises further on high-pressure days: +5.4/+7.1/+8.6 K at −30/0/+30 hPa (R10), against trim's +5.4/+5.7/+5.7.
- **Cost:** no energy and no pumping, but it needs airspace. At Heathrow it climbs 268 m on a typical day, 426 m at the 99th percentile and 510 m at most.
- **Cold-day penalty:** the air aloft is colder, so climbing costs P2 cold margin. Climb alone covers 53% of R10 days, trim alone 57%. Choosing by the hour (climb, unless that is too cold) covers 64%.
- **P3:** has no cold edge, so climbing always wins. Sized at standard pressure, P3 climbs only 61 m typically and 270 m at most.
- **Floor height is a weak lever:** at R10, 50 m adds 0.1 K; 200 m costs 0.7 K and 300 m costs 3.1 K.

**Ballast, dropped or recovered.**
- **Band:** ballast adds no kelvin at zero payload, because both edges are set by mass and temperature. What it does is hold the floor on cold days at fixed payload, without admitting air.
- **Why that matters:** without any lift dump the hull floats up 0.7–1.7 km into colder air. Then P2's Heathrow coverage falls from 64% to 31% (R10).
- **Capacity:** the water needed is 193 kg typical and 387 kg at the 99th percentile at R10, and 10–16 t at R30.
- **Mass:** about 2% of capacity for the bladder (8 kg at R10, 310 kg at R30). Ground pillow tanks are 0.35–0.93%. Drop or recovery takes no energy; it is refilled at the mooring.
- **With ballast doing the dump,** P2 needs relief air on only 27% of Heathrow days.

**The hull's temperature: finish, shade and flying by night.**
- **Low-emittance finish:** it holds the night skin near the air temperature. Moving the clear-night edge 11 K lower lets P2 fly down to 265.8 K air.
- **The finish options** (cold-edge shift / hot-edge cost, at R10):
  - Al with an acrylic overcoat (Sheldahl, α 0.14, ε 0.05): −11.1 K / −0.5 K, for +10 kg (+667 kg at R30, from the hotter day skin of 319 K).
  - A switchable night state (050's ε 0.05 projection): −11.1 K / ~0 K.
  - TARC-class (ε 0.20, α 0.25): −7.0 K / −0.9 K.
  - SiOx/Al: −5.9 K. Ag/FEP: −3.8 K. CCAg: −1.2 K.
- **Overcast nights:** these already give a 265.3 K edge. Counting observed cloud adds about 5 points of Heathrow coverage for MLCCAg.
- **Flying only by day:** this moves the edge to 269.7 K with no sun credit (266.5 K with 050's overhead sun), but the hull needs cover or heat on the ground at night.
- **Night heating in flight:** holding the skin 1, 2, 5 or 10 K below the edge needs 0.4, 1.5, 8.4 or 29 kW at R10. That is 43, 161, 931 or 3,248 kg of battery for 12 h. Only 1–2 K are practical in flight; beyond that it needs ground power on the mooring.
- **Shade:** buys nothing. A vacuum hull's lift ignores skin temperature, and the day thermal stresses are under 1% of face stress.
- **Flying by night:** this avoids the afternoon peak but meets the clear-night edge. Night-only missions cover 75% of R10 days with MLCCAg and 97% with the low-ε finish.
- **P3:** its account has no cold screen, so these levers do not move its band (N058-09).

**Dynamic lift from thrust or shape.**
- **Thrust:** 1 K is 18.8 kg of lift at R10. With rotor disks totalling 4% of the frontal area that takes 0.8 kW. Net of motor and battery it gives +0.6 K for 1 h, +0.2 K for 2 h and nothing past 2.6 h. At R30 1 K needs 37 kW, and the gain is gone after 1.4 h. Thrust is for transients only.
- **Shape:** a sphere has no lift. A tethered kite sail gives about 1.3 kg/m² at 5 m/s and 5.7 kg/m² at 10 m/s. At Heathrow, the relieved R10 hull with the low-ε finish runs short of lift in 4.8% of hours. Those hours have a median wind at 100 m of 5.7 m/s, and a sail of a quarter of the frontal area (79 m², about 16 kg) would cover 75% of them. This works for tethered hulls only.

## 3. What it means in days

**The site.** London Heathrow (NOAA ISD 03772099999, 51.48 N, 25 m) is a temperate oceanic site. The record is 2014–2023, with 87,354 complete hours.

**How each hour was mapped.** Each hour was taken to the 100 m floor with 050's lapse. Its observed dew point and sea-level pressure were used. The cold test assumes 050's clear sky every night.

| Lead and option | R10 | R12 | R15 | R20 | R30 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| P2 050 full-range build | 34% (70% h) | 54% | 67% | 72% | 75% (92% h) |
| P2 relief + ballast, MLCCAg | 64% (89% h) | 75% | 77% | 77% | 77% |
| P2 relief + ballast + Al+AOC | 84% (95% h) | 96% | 98% | 99.3% | 99.6% |
| P3 full-range build | 62% (83% h) | 91% | 98% | 99.6% | 99.9% |
| P3 sized at 0 hPa, climb + ballast | 78% (93% h) | 97% | 99.6% | 100% | 100% |

Each cell is the fraction of 24-hour days covered; "h" is the fraction of hours.

**What limits each lead.**
- **P2 with MLCCAg** stops near 77% because of cold clear winter nights.
- **The low-ε finish** removes that limit. The remaining failures are hot summer afternoons.
- **Shorter missions do better:** daylight-only flights cover 86% (R10) to 99.5% (R30) for MLCCAg with relief.
- **The design pressure** barely matters for P2 (±1 point), but for P3 standard pressure beats −30 hPa (78% against 66% at R10).

## 4. The answer: the design-day options

**A. Keep 050's full-range build.**
- **Mass:** no added mass.
- **Band:** a 4.0–13.8 K band at the worst corner.
- **Heathrow coverage:** P2 34–75% of days; P3 62–99.9%.

**B. Size for the −30 hPa day and relieve above it.**
- **Mass:** the hull is 4.5–4.7% lighter for P2 (2.5–2.6% for P3).
- **What it costs instead:** climb room of up to 510 m, plus water ballast. If relief is by air, add 17–19 h of ground pumping per flight day.
- **Band:** the hot edge rises about 12 K (P2) or 6.5 K (P3).
- **Heathrow coverage:** P2 64–77%; P3 66–100%.

**C. Size for the site's standard pressure and climb above it.**
- **Mass:** 1.3% heavier than B for P3.
- **Heathrow coverage:** the best for P3, 78–100%. For P2 it makes no difference.

**D. Add a low-emittance finish to P2.**
- **Band:** the cold edge goes 11 K lower.
- **Mass:** Al+AOC costs 0.2–0.5% of the bill and 0.5–1.3 K of hot edge. A switchable finish costs about nothing but is unproven.
- **Heathrow coverage:** 84–99.6%.

**E. Fly 24 hours on a chosen share of Heathrow days.** The table gives the least optimism, with the best levers.

| Target | P2 | P3 |
|---|---|---|
| 75% | R10 gamma/Q/film; R15 gamma/Q | R10 S/F/J; R12 S/J; R30 S |
| 90% | R12 gamma/Q/film; R15 gamma/Q (R10 tops at 89%) | R12 S/F/J; R15 S/J (R10 tops at 78%) |
| 95% | R12 gamma/Q/film (96%); R15 gamma/Q (96%) | R12 S/F/J (96%); R15 S/J (97%) |
| 99% | R20 gamma/Q (99.4%); not below R20 | R15 S/F/J (99.4%); R20 S/J |

**F. Design for a MIL-HDBK-310 hot day.** Not reachable at R ≤ 30.
- **10% hot day:** at R30 the relieved P3 is 886 kg short (0.7% of its mass), and the relieved P2 is 2,956–3,853 kg short.
- **1% hot day:** P3 is 2,435 kg short.
- **Absolute-humidity 10% day (29 °C saturated):** the relieved MLCCAg P2 and P3 float at R20 and R30.

**Behind every option:** all of these need flight-proven masses (057, N058-11).
