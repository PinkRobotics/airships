## Method

I built my own axisymmetric model from the formulation the card states, in check029/axi029.py.

**Model.**
- 8-node quadrilaterals with 3 × 3 Gauss points, total-Lagrangian St. Venant–Kirchhoff.
- Follower pressure, with its load stiffness.
- The honeycomb core is orthotropic in the reference tangent/normal frame.
- The same stress-free Gaussian offset is applied to every layer.

**Solvers.**
- Linear buckling, with and without the pressure load stiffness.
- Arc-length continuation.
- Pole displacement control, a bordered Newton that passes limit points.
- Load control that copies the card's increments.

**Inputs.** Meshes, materials, widths and increments come from the owner's declared MODEL.json records.

**Not used.** I did not read or run axisymmetric.py, three_dimensional.py, the other owner scripts or any deck.

**Validation** on a homogeneous complete sphere:
- Membrane displacement is within 0.15% at R/t 100 and 0.014% at R/t 1000.
- The eigenpressure over the classical value is 0.970 and 0.998. The gap at R/t 100 is the thick-shell effect.

**Runs.** 122 run records. Heavy runs went through simjob; the sub-second validation and radius-reading runs ran directly. Under the standing order ord-admiral-rr2-check029-simjob-retry-standing-1020, 7 simjob refusals (exit 75, memory PSI) were each retried unchanged after 60 s (check029/simjob_retry.sh). Two earlier refusals, before the order, were retried after 30 s. One last scan, the far-shift 021b L at 105 kPa, was stopped unfinished to free capacity; its 30, 60 and 90 kPa states are used.

## Objections (NFI.json)

**C029-01. The EUCASS elastic endpoints are St. Venant–Kirchhoff foam collapse (demonstrated).**
- With ν 0, StVK foam carries at most E/(3√3) = 7.70 MPa in uniaxial compression, at stretch 0.577.
- At each maximum, the foam under the dimple is near that stress and past that stretch:
  - flat, 12 core elements: −7.61 MPa at stretch 0.23;
  - dimple, at the card's mesh: −7.48 MPa at 0.35.
- Under displacement control at the card's mesh, a flat foam point is crushed to stretch 0.0096 at 13.78 MPa, the arc-length critical point. The path then keeps rising to 14.219 MPa through such states.
- Meridional refinement is converged. Core refinement is not.
- The lead models are not affected by this mechanism. Their honeycomb normal strain stays below 1% at the limits, while StVK collapse needs 42%. The lead core's own instability is a different, shear mechanism (C029-10).
- Fix card, if the benchmark is reused: replace the foam law with a compressible hyperelastic or crushable law.

**C029-02. EUCASS linear gap: candidates computed, cause not established.**
- Fig. 4(a) of the paper draws the 42.80 mm arrow to a point inside the wall.
  - Read as the mid-surface radius, it gives 28.076 / 28.061 MPa (+2.0% / +1.8%).
  - Read at the foam/outer-face interface, it gives 29.738 / 29.715 MPa.
- An E_steel of about 187 GPa closes the gap, but Table 1 states 210 GPa.
- The paper's own discretization (about 2 foam solids through 3.1 mm, continuum-shell faces, ties) was not tested.

**C029-03. The nonlinear gap is constitutive (demonstrated in the elastic model).**

At the paper's 6.03 MPa, the imperfect flat model gives:
- **Foam:** −3.15 MPa principal stress. It passes the Fig. 3 plateau (read at about 1.4 MPa) at 2.75 MPa.
- **Outer face:** 1,015 MPa von Mises. It passes 750 MPa at 4.56 MPa. Fig. 5 as printed starts near 7,500 MPa, which is impossible for 201 steel; if its axis is off by a factor of 10, it starts near 750 MPa.

At 12.45 MPa, the perfect model gives:
- **Outer face:** 1,014 MPa at the clamped edge. It reaches 750 MPa at 9.29 MPa.
- **Foam:** only −1.11 MPa.

This matches the paper's report of bottom-edge buckling in its perfect model. It supports the card's NFI-029-FOAM, NFI-029-FACE-PLASTICITY and NFI-029-STEEL-CURVE.

**C029-04. 021b face wrinkling (demonstrated in the linear analysis).**
- 021b's lowest mode is symmetric face wrinkling: the faces move in opposite directions, and the core shear modulus does not enter.
- On graded meshes it falls with refinement: 230.39 (512), 209.17 (1024), 200.79 (2048), 198.22 (4096) and 197.59 kPa (8192). The first three are L and the last two are W; W and L agree to 0.001 kPa at 2048. It converges near 197.4 kPa.
- That is 2.8% below W and 15.8% below L.
- The owner's uniform eigen meshes (61 and 31 mm elements) cannot resolve it. Uniform 4096 (7.7 mm) only partly resolves it: L gives 210.19, and W shows only its global 203.244.
- The card's "512 to 1,024 changes at most 0.0103%" therefore tests only the global modes.
- A Winkler estimate gives 677.7 MPa = 187.5 kPa for 021b (wavelength 36.7 mm). For 021 it gives 913 MPa = 308 kPa, which is not governing; the graded 2048 mesh gives only the global modes, 191.476 and 160.132.
- The static endpoints are unchanged on graded 2048 meshes: 78.631, 90.736 and 105.077 kPa.
- **It depends on the core's in-plane stiffness.** With E_in raised from the card's 0.01 MPa to 1 MPa, the graded 4096 lowest mode is the global 203.251. The card's value is closer to reality: a Gibson–Ashby estimate for 1.0 pcf 5056 honeycomb gives about 0.02 MPa (QUESTIONABLE).
- **On the static paths, refinement does not isolate wrinkling.** Meshes fine enough to resolve it also resolve the core's loss of stability (C029-10).
- Proposed: add the wrinkling eigenpressure, about 197 kPa (about 690 MPa face stress), to the 021b margin table, conditional on a sourced in-plane core stiffness.

**C029-05. The lead endpoints are limit points (demonstrated).**
- 021b W peaks at 90.736 kPa at a pole displacement of −80.4 mm, then falls to 76.53 kPa at −140.4 mm.
- The card's load-control endpoints are within 0.032% of the maxima. This settles the card's NFI-029-LOAD-CONTROL for the axisymmetric model.
- Nonaxisymmetric modes remain open (NFI-029-AXISYMMETRY).

**C029-10. The card's core loses ellipticity under transverse shear (demonstrated).** This extends the card's NFI-029-HOMOGENIZATION.

- **The test.** Refined lead paths become unstable on the dimple flank (r 340–570 mm) below the endpoints. The lowest tangent eigenvalues use a shift of −1000, so they are the true lowest:
  - graded 4096, about 3.5 mm elements: 021b W −570 at 60 kPa and about −1,000 at 80–90.7 kPa; 021b L −55.5 at 60 kPa and −1,077 at 90 kPa;
  - 4096 with grading 2.0, about 1.35 mm elements: −0.595 already at 30 kPa, an exact eigenpair carried by the core;
  - graded 2048, about 7 mm elements: no instability was seen, though that scan's window was narrow.
- **The mechanism.** The modes sit in the core. The onset load falls with refinement, and the unstable waves are a few millimetres long, below the 9.5 mm cell size. That is the signature of a material, not structural, instability.
- **The arithmetic.** For the card's core law, C = diag(E_in, E_n, G) with zero Poisson coupling, the acoustic tensor under transverse shear τ loses positive definiteness when τ exceeds 0.088 MPa at the card's E_in of 0.01 MPa (W and L alike). The threshold is 0.17 MPa at 0.024 MPa and about 2.6 MPa at 1 MPa. The card's paths reach 0.088 MPa at 6.9–11.3 kPa.
- **Raising the regularization removes it.** With in-plane E 1 MPa and G 0.5 MPa (100 times, the card's own sensitivity), the same refined paths are stable up to their limits. The four lowest eigenvalues are positive at every state, for W up to 90.7 kPa and for L up to 105.0 kPa. The only mode going to zero is the limit mode: 0.017 and 0.024 at the limits. The 1.35 mm state at 30 kPa becomes 0.0197.
- **The negative modes are mesh-scale and live in the core.** At 60 kPa, more than 99.9% of their material energy is in the core. The L mode's outer-face motion has a wavelength of 6.5 mm (about two elements) and is anti-phase. The flank core stress there is about 0.8 MPa. This is not the 37 mm wrinkle.
- **Consequence.** The card's endpoints are properties of meshes too coarse to see this instability. In the real honeycomb, this regime is governed by the core's shear strength, which is passed even earlier (C029-11).

**C029-11. The honeycomb core fails in shear far below the endpoints (demonstrated).** This is the core sibling of the card's face stress diagnostic; the card makes no core check.

Along the card's own static paths (graded 512 × 1f × 4c), I tracked the core's Cauchy stresses in the current tangent/normal frame. The strengths are the card's own source, Hexcel CR III Table II (5056, 3/8 in., 1.0 pcf): W shear 35 psi typical (0.241 MPa), L shear 55 psi (0.379 MPa), crush 35 psi.

| 021 W | 26.4 | 20.0 | 1.61 | 6.7 | 67.3 |
| 021 L | 41.2 | 35.1 | 1.84 | 4.8 | 76.6 |
| 021b W | 19.5 | 14.1 | 1.77 | 7.4 | 54.4 |

- The shear peaks on the dimple flank (r 330–600 mm). Crush occurs under the dimple centre.
- At operating pressure the core shear is 1.56–1.59 MPa, for the cases that reach operating.
- **The shear is mesh-converged.** For 021b W on 512, 1024 and 2048 meshes, and with 2 face × 6 core elements, the typical-shear crossing is 19.49 kPa in every case. The limit shear is 1.773–1.774 MPa and crush comes at 54.31–54.36 kPa.
- **Fix card.** Core admissibility at the specified dimples (shear and crush), together with the face admissibility of C029-06. The elastic endpoints are not capacities.
