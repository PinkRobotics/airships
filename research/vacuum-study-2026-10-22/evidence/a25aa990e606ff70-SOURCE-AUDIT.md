CHECK-049 source audit. All transferred material values remain QUESTIONABLE; laminate mixtures, biaxial strengths, installed joints, thermal boundaries and supply allocations remain PROJECTION.

K13D2U's 935 GPa, 3.70 GPa tensile strength and 2200 kg/m³ describe fibre, not a quasi-isotropic face or a compression allowable. The manufacturer confirms those fibre inputs. The 33 wt% resin mixture belongs to a separate prepreg description; it cannot also represent 60% fibre volume with the stated constituent densities. [Mitsubishi fibre data](https://www.m-chemical.co.jp/carbon-fiber/pdf/tow/Carbon%20Fiber%20Tow%20(Continuous%20Fiber)%2020230227.pdf), [prepreg research report](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1963549).

Tuttle's authored slides give E1=59.5 Msi, E2=.806 Msi, G12=.595 Msi and nu12=.39. Using the owner's rounded source-extraction inputs, 410.2/5.557/4.102 GPa, independent equal-angle laminate arithmetic gives E_QI=142.025007371 GPa and nu=.325230920223. Unrounded unit conversions give 142.038081316 GPa, an immaterial precision difference. The slides describe one measurement for each specimen type and departures from standard specimen geometry. The fibre volume fraction and statistical compression allowable are not established. The six-ply thickness is a construction assumption, not a universal minimum gauge. The 220 MPa inherited compression value remains unsourced. [Tuttle presentation](https://www.slideserve.com/ronan-huff/elastic-properties-of-k13d2u-epoxy-prepreg).

The 585.240740741 GPa projected lamina modulus is arithmetically 935×338/540. Toray does print M55J fibre modulus 540 GPa and a 338 GPa composite modulus normalized to 60% fibre volume, with composite compression 890 MPa. Its epoxy, normalization, specimen and lot averages do not validate the same ratio for K13D2U. A separate simple rule-of-mixtures sensitivity, assuming a 3 GPa resin, gives 562.2 GPa lamina and about 192.694 GPa QI stiffness. That is an explicitly assumed alternative, not a measured K13D2U property or a rigorous impossibility proof. [Toray M55J data](https://www.toraycma.com/wp-content/uploads/M55J-Data-Sheet.pdf).

Rock West explicitly identifies its M55J laminate numbers as calculated: 15.8 Msi in-plane modulus, 45.4 ksi compressive strength, nu=.308, and −.203 ppm/°F CTE. Their converted rounded values agree with the card. The listed tensile value is 102.9 ksi, about 709 MPa; it is not the fibre tensile strength. The card's 1626 kg/m³ density comes from its separate 60%-volume mixture assumption. Multiplication of uniaxial compression by 1/(1−nu) is a projected strain criterion, not a demonstrated biaxial failure envelope. [Rock West plate](https://www.rockwestcomposites.com/22z002102-3-0-044-96-48.html).

AlBeMet's rolled-sheet column supports density 2071 kg/m³, modulus 193 GPa, minimum tensile yield 276 MPa, typical tensile yield 314 MPa and 62 wt% Be. The general physical table gives nu=.17 and CTE 13.9 ppm/K. Minimum tensile ultimate strength is 379 MPa. The card correctly discloses the tensile-to-compression transfer and that this material is not Be-free. [Materion AlBeMet datasheet](https://hkvxni.files.cmp.optimizely.com/download/d896d6d2a04211ee94aa3aa4a5458726).

The HRH-10 3/8–1.5 row supports its rounded modulus inputs. Its W minimum is actually printed as 25 psi; deriving it by multiplying a rounded 35 psi typical by 25/35 is unnecessary and introduces a small rounding discrepancy. HRH-49 1/4–2.1 prints W minimum 30 psi, versus the card's projected 28.571 psi from the aluminum ratio. The latter is conservative but is not the manufacturer's minimum. In-plane CTE, cell-wall constitutive data, vacuum conductivity and installed strength transfers remain projections. Neither source makes an aramid core float in the checked model. [Hexcel HRH-10](https://hexcel.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/HexWeb_HRH10_DataSheet_us.pdf), [Hexcel HRH-49](https://hexcel.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/HRH_49_us.pdf).

The AF163 family documentation gives elevated-temperature cure schedules. Its detailed 200°F performance example is AF163-2K at .06 lb/ft² on metal. It does not qualify a 25–50°C cure of thin AF163-2U on CFRP/5056. A low effective stress-free temperature might be a future process target, but it cannot be booked as a sourced replacement bond. The co-cured FM300 scarf screen also needs a joint-specific allowable; laminate failure in a wide-area lap test does not establish the curved scarf's peel, defects or compression behavior. [3M AF163 datasheet](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/282041O/3m-scotch-weld-structural-adhesive-film-af-163-2-af-163-3.pdf).

The coating layer recipe and bulk-density proxies reproduce 7.5752 g/m² for the multilayer and 9.3256–11.1786 g/m² for classical CCAg. The added 10% and 10 g/m² structural primer remain assumptions. NASA describes a multilayer coating class with the favorable optics; this is not a matched measurement of the exact deposited recipe on either proposed face. [NASA patent](https://patents.justia.com/patent/11391493), [NASA 2018 coating presentation](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20180002012/downloads/20180002012.pdf).

The water inputs are disclosed transfers across resin systems, grades and exposure conditions. HRH-10 and HRH-36 uptake measurements do not establish HRH-49 uptake. FM300's hot/humid estimate is not FM300-2M at the intended condition. The extra 90% drying credit is an acceptance target. The inherited metal-shell service model also lacks a measured full-area gas permeance and non-water volatile inventory for the CFRP faces. Added water is counted once as vehicle mass and then released into the pressure calculation. [Hexcel honeycomb attributes](https://hexcel.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/HexWebHoneycombAttributesandProperties.pdf), [NASA adhesive moisture study](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19810008656/downloads/19810008656.pdf).

[USGS 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-beryllium.pdf).

[Nippon Steel technical report](https://www.nipponsteel.com/common/secure/en/tech/report/pdf/134-23.pdf).
