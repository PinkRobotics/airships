# MAINLINE-020 calculation and evidence notes

`calculate.py` is this seat's standard-library-only arithmetic. It does not import or execute the copied 009c5, 014b or 016 programs. Their copies and reports preserve provenance. `INPUTS.json` pins all intake copies and downloaded primary PDFs by SHA-256 and bytes. Source IDs S01–S09 resolve to URLs, exact archived files where available, selected pages, facts and limitations. The OSHA and AlBeMet datasheet bodies were read with the browser; direct archive attempts returned 403, so no original-file hash is claimed for them. The AlBeMet manufacturer landing page is archived. An attempted NIRCam-paper retrieval timed out and was not used as evidence.

The baseline uses the inherited model unchanged: radius R = 10 m, external pressure p = 95,460.8379139 Pa, available lift = 4,889.442150186961 kg, 100 kg systems, and joints = 5% of sandwich mass. One spherical surface has area A = 4πR². Two face masses are 2Aρf tf; core mass is Aρc d. Actual inside/outside radii differ slightly, but this card preserves the inherited thin-shell area convention. It does not add that small geometric correction while comparing copied results.

The face midpoint spacing is h = d + tf. Membrane compression is pR/(4tf). The inherited reference buckling stress is E h/[R√(1−ν²)], while crimp stress is h²Gc/(2tf d). Their ratio is Vc. For Vc ≤ 2, Kc = 1−Vc/4 and gamma = 0.2; above 2, Kc = 1/Vc and gamma = 1. Global SF is gamma Kc times the reference stress divided by membrane compression. On the crimp branch it equals the crimp SF. This branch discontinuity is inherited, not newly justified. In particular, direct M55J substitution and the uniformly resized AlBeMet point cross branches. Their unexpectedly low values are model consequences, not demonstrated physical discontinuities.

Honeycomb wrinkle stress is q × 0.33 × E√(Ec tf/(E d)), where q is the positive root of q³+q = 1. Face yield uses the chosen compression value divided by membrane compression. Core crush uses p/2. Intracell dimpling uses `2E/(1−ν²) [source link unavailable in this public projection]²`. Ec remains the sourced stabilised compression modulus, whose transfer into the continuum wrinkle model is unresolved. The core's W-direction shear modulus is Gc; it is not a shear strength. No unsupported thermal-stress safety factor is calculated from Gc.

The first check reproduces 009c5's masses, payload margin and least SF. Four further point evaluations reproduce the selected 014b Be, K13D2U, M55J and AlBeMet minima at their reported geometries. Those cases share a zero-payload mass ceiling but do not all share geometry or core. The exact-geometry alternatives hold the 009c5 faces/core dimensions fixed; their changed densities alter payload. A separate equal-budget comparison uniformly shrinks each published 014b geometry by the structural-budget ratio, retaining its face/core split. It is not a new optimum and is not claimed as one.

For panel width a and length b, N = ceil(A/(ab)) is only an area lower bound per skin. The shared-edge length on one surface is approximated by L = A(1/a+1/b). A coincident hull seam means the corresponding seam on both faces; individual skin-seam length is 2L. Approximately 2N edge joints per skin assumes a predominantly quadrilateral layout. Trimming, pole regions, openings, laps and defects are not designed, and can increase counts and mass. The large panel is the published maximum for thicker material transferred to thin sheet as a PROJECTION; the small panel is an owner planning assumption. Neither is represented as currently quoted thin stock.

Panel corner sag is R−√(R²−r²), where r is half the flat-panel diagonal. Through-thickness bending strain is tf/(2R); a spherical metric-strain scale is r²/(6R²). These are geometric scales, not a forming simulation, strain limit, springback result or proof of biaxial ductility. Sheet flatness specifications cannot be substituted for an as-assembled shell survey or a validated buckling knockdown.

The bonded seam has one full-gauge Be doubler on each skin, width w across the seam; one bond layer under each doubler; a core gap g filled with paste; and an assumed total sealant bead cross-section Ab. Its mass per coincident hull-seam metre is

`2 ρf tf w + 2 μfilm w + ρpaste g d + ρpaste Ab`.

The optimistic/conservative widths are 60/120 mm, gaps 1/2 mm, and total sealant areas 10/30 mm². Paste density 1,100 kg/m³ is an owner assumption carried from the 016 style of screen. There is no stiffness or buckling credit for added straps. Single-sided load eccentricity within each skin, peel, corrosion, fatigue, defects and sealing still require design.

Both face/core interfaces receive full-area adhesive film in the main scenario, mass 2Aμfilm. The 3M 0.030/0.060 lb/ft² nominal examples convert to 0.14647/0.29295 kg/m². These are representative product weights, not a finding that AF163 is qualified for this Be/HRH10 bond. The ±0.005 lb/ft² product-weight tolerance alone corresponds to ±61.35 kg over both interfaces. A 0.015 lb/ft² unsupported film would weigh 184.06 kg, but is not accepted as sufficient coverage/fillet capacity. Patterned or reticulated application could reduce mass; no validated coverage fraction is known, so that route receives no mass credit.

Joining replaces the original 5% joint allowance; it is never added on top of that allowance. The nominal optimistic case adds 751.77 kg relative to the allowance, more than the original 244.47 kg payload. This is a sensitivity at fixed geometry, not a new optimized shell. The conservative scenario is a selected stress case, not a proven worst-case bound.

Brazing is a separate joining route. Assumed added filler cross-sections per skin of 0.3/1.0 mm² and densities 9,000/10,000 kg/m³ give 0.0054/0.020 kg per hull-seam metre. This is a filler-only geometric floor, not a mechanically sized seam. It omits lands, straps, distortion repair and post-braze losses. Parent/core bond films remain necessary if an adhesively bonded organic core is retained. A low filler mass cannot be used to erase these items.

An assumed joint efficiency η scales only the net-section face-compression capacity: ησc/[pR/(4tf)]. Other mode SFs are recomputed and the minimum taken. Multiplying the entire shell SF by η would be unjustified. Historical NASA thick double-shear tensile coupons and Materion average joint data show that joining is possible, but do not measure this η.

For gauge sensitivity the supplier's inch tolerance is converted exactly to ±0.1016 mm. The low-gauge SF is a local screening value. Separate whole-surface uniform-gauge masses show the possible budget sensitivity; a thin local patch cannot claim the mass saving of an entirely thin shell. Ordering extra nominal thickness to guarantee a minimum gauge would require its own mass and tolerance specification.

Mechanical sensitivity preserves inherited 303 GPa / 283 MPa first, then checks current 290 GPa with a representative VHP ν = 0.08, and a 241 MPa compression proxy. The latter is a sourced tensile minimum used only as an assumption. Rolled-sheet compression allowables are unknown. The additional 0.8 stiffness and 0.7 strength factors are explicit owner stress cases, not measured anisotropy or lower confidence bounds. Current guide text on compression is internally inconsistent; the copy's 283 MPa cannot be upgraded to a guaranteed current minimum.

The 016 long-joint screen is λ = √[Gc/(E tf d)] and τ = |Δα ΔT|E tf λ. It is re-evaluated with the actual Be/HRH10 geometry, not the old SiC/PVC values. Effective mismatch 5/20 ppm/K and excursions 30/60 K are assumptions; this gives transfer length 0.567 m and peak shear 0.0577/0.4616 MPa. Long-joint, scalar, elastic load transfer omits finite length, honeycomb orthotropy, adhesive relaxation, edge peel and gradients. Fully restrained axial/biaxial elastic stress screens are stored separately. The upper case can exceed yield, so linear-elastic stress is a warning of model breakdown, not a valid post-yield prediction.

The assumed thirty-year daily-cycle count is 10,958. The smooth, zero-mean bulk-Be benchmark of 10⁷ cycles at 262–266 MPa cannot establish a pressure-loaded bonded-sheet life. No fatigue life, cumulative-damage fraction or thermal capacity credit is claimed. Be/aluminium bulk CTE mismatch is also not a measured effective CTE of an aluminium honeycomb sandwich; no such substitution is made.

Supply uses mine-contained Be, not rolling capacity or available inventory. Planning prices are deliberately parameterized, unquoted sensitivities anchored to a master-alloy-content indicator. The conversion multipliers and 85%/50% fabrication yields are owner assumptions. Costs exclude purchasing or contacting a vendor, which were not performed.

One scientific command ran through simjob with one CPU and 512 MiB. Its short technical watchdog did not impose a task budget. Five recorded checks passed; the launcher reported reservation release. A subsequent read-only systemd check found the named scope inactive/dead and not loaded. Run metadata, stdout/stderr and scope status are retained under `execution/`. No physical test, material certification or new optimized design was performed.
