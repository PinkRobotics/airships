# CHECK-040 evidence and limits

QUESTIONABLE material transfers; PROJECTION fabrication, loads, mission and assembly. This is an independent check of a conditional screen. No installed-hull safety factor or flight qualification is assigned.

## Independence and scope

No owner or predecessor executable was read, hashed, copied, imported or run. The check froze the named non-code artifacts and wrote its own `model.py`, `thermal.py`, `audit.py`, `search_audit.py` and `supplement.py`. Source pins are recorded in `PROVENANCE.json` and `WATER-SOURCE-PINS.json`. Work and outputs stayed in this seat's `.ship` root. No seat was contacted, restored or woken.

All current point equations are reconstructed from the documented constants and geometry: hydrostatic weather headroom, uniform wind pressure, nine shell/interface screens, cure compression, joining, pad, gas inventory, service pressure and complete mass bill. The common logarithmic axes are independently applied. Geometry searches do not start from owner optimum coordinates: they scan logarithmic core thickness, solve the minimum acceptable face thickness and refine maxima and feasibility transitions.

**The owner temperature table is an explicit input to the primary arithmetic/search verification.** This is necessary because a separately implemented, fully specified heat balance did not reproduce its absolute temperatures from the prose alone. That limitation is not hidden behind a numerical pass. `THERMAL-CLOSURE.json` gives the exact differences and the check's boundary interpretation. `THERMAL-INDEPENDENT.json` and `MODEL-DEPENDENCE.json` quantify that interpretation's consequences. This is an unresolved reproducibility issue, not proof that one physical boundary is the correct operating environment.

The superseded `comparison-037` points retain 2,640 kg/m³ in the thermal/pore calculation and the original scarf/interface treatment. Their masses, service and nonthermal equations are checked. An equal-conductance mapping into the current 2,700 kg/m³ table interpolates their thermal quantities; its exact field differences are retained separately in `ARITHMETIC-DIFFERENCES.json`. Those interpolated results are not counted as exact reproduction of the historical thermal solve.

## Coating and water sources

The [AZ-93 manufacturer](https://www.aztechnology.com/product/1/az-93) provides nominal cured thickness and optical characteristics, but does not close the installed coating-plus-primer mass on Be. Its 5 mil nominal dry thickness corresponds to 127 µm. A 0.20 kg/m² mass at that thickness implies 1,574.803 kg/m³; that conversion is arithmetic, not a measured density. The manufacturer's stated thickness range is 3.5–6.5 mil over its specified area fraction. The assumed mass cannot establish coverage, adhesion, emittance or solar absorptance on this shell.

The [NASA coating compilation](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19730017242/downloads/19730017242.pdf), PDF page 84, describes historical Z-93 and does not supply a density for this purpose. It does not qualify an AZ-93/primer/Be process. The 040 optical inputs remain α=.20 and ε=.90; they do not gain additional credit from the newer optical data.

Water traces through 031 to 019's explicitly optimistic **0.1 mm equivalent retained film**, density 1,000 kg/m³. It is not rainfall depth, a measured shedding result or an absorbed-moisture allowance. It gives 0.10 kg/m², hence 125.663706 kg at R10. The `both` weather case changes atmospheric headroom and pressure while retaining this optimistic vehicle water inventory; it does not adopt 019's separate 1 mm retained-water case.

At the R10 nominal two-factor endpoint, coating must fall from 0.20 to at most 0.112743933 kg/m² to erase the deficit by this line alone. Alternatively, retained water must fall from 0.10 to at most 0.012743933 kg/m², equivalent to 12.743933 µm at the assumed density. These are single-line mass thresholds with the other assumptions held, not qualified achievable limits.

`WATER-ONE-MM-SENSITIVITY.json` applies the independently reconstructable extra 0.9 kg/m² from the inherited 1 mm scenario. Every scored heavy-pad/vented account becomes negative even at all four full endpoints. R30 ISA actual joining, the best of these rows, is about −594.989 kg; its named γ/Q endpoint is −820.787 kg. Water retention can therefore reverse the headline ranking's practical usefulness without changing a structural formula.

## Loads, pads and service

The weather computation integrates the stated lapse-rate hydrostatic atmosphere from the 500 m anchor to the 100 m centre floor. At R10 it recovers optimistic headroom 55.340908918 kg and pressure multiplier 1.036552961984; both-weather gives 441.770105446 kg and 1.022888807761. Headroom scales with R³. Pressure uses `max(1.01, q_weather)`, then adds wind pressure. The weather loss and the old 033 climate allowance are not charged twice.

The two wind loads are the full 70 kn dynamic pressure and the full pressure of 35 kn plus an aligned 35 ft/s gust. The latter includes the baseline steady load. Uniform Cp=1 is a declared shell-load surrogate. ISA density and Cd=.47 remain assumptions for the mooring calculation.

The heavy pad is resized at every radius. The light pad's 1.4×20 m/s load is only 0.4318321612 of the 70 kn force. Its lighter mass cannot be treated as a 70 kn assembly pass. Pad area and plate thickness do not establish the actual sandwich load path, punching, peel or local attachment strength.

The gas reconstruction uses a dry initial 10 Pa inventory, humid external ingress, finite outgassing and the actual scarf length. Desorbed mass is material already aboard and is charged to pressure, not again to flying mass. Only external ingress adds material. The charged gas line is the larger of the 1%-of-lift reserve and initial-plus-ingress-plus-trapped gas. Unvented cells are a mass sensitivity with an unresolved mechanical load path.

The 172 service-audit records independently include inner-cavity volume, connected pores, high leakage, absence of conditioning and the counterfactual late vent. The 30–50 kg sealed-service estimate is nested inside the retained 100 kg systems line. Its incremental mass is zero on that accounting basis; +30/+50 kg alternatives cover a fully committed allowance. The 1.111111 kg monitoring battery is inside the 10 kg power package. None of those bookkeeping identities establishes hardware scalability or measured leakage.

## Dependence on 037b

The current primary model uses 200°F stress-free temperature, a compliant core field, a free-edge shear screen against the **minimum-average 25 psi W strength**, and balanced cure compression excluded from global buckling/crimping. At R10 the edge screen is about 1.07188 and the balanced face compression is 3.21941 MPa.

CHECK-037b's source qualifications remain open. The detailed 200°F cure tests concern AF 163-2K at .060 lb/ft² on metal; they do not qualify AF 163-2U at .015 lb/ft² on Be/5056 honeycomb. The family process guidance supplies a candidate, not an installed allowable or demonstrated stress-free temperature. The cited 50 psi cure load exceeds the core's 35 psi minimum-average stabilized compression value unless tooling changes the load path.

The free-edge transfer length in CHECK-037b was about 1.69 cells, making a homogenized mean-field argument insufficient to establish cell-scale peaks. Scarf-edge coincidence, peel, hardpoints and residual effects on global modes need grounding. The 5052-H39 foil yield proxy is transferred to 5056. None of these issues is resolved by the very small scarf mass increment.

`MODEL-DEPENDENCE.json` changes these assumptions one at a time and reoptimizes. Including balanced cure compression in global/crimp demand modestly reduces the principal endpoint payloads. Replacing the compliant shear formula by the rigid-face swing formula, while retaining the current minimum W allowable, removes every positive endpoint in the primary matrix. Using rigid-face transfer from the 200°F cure also removes them; no feasible geometry appears in the scanned range.

There is a stronger conditional mass bound for the latter sensitivity. Combining rigid shear and wrinkling eliminates E:

`σ_wr,max = β Q τ_W sqrt(Ec/Gc) / mismatch_strain`.

Even choosing the smallest cure mismatch over the full tabulated thickness range, the implied minimum **face mass alone** is 1.89945–1.94911 times gross lift across the primary rows. Core, joining and all other charges would increase that deficit. `RIGID-CURE-BOUND.json` records this bound. It is a test of the stated alternative shear constraint, not a claim that rigid transfer describes the actual honeycomb.

The 250°F cure fails the free-edge screen independently of face/core geometry in this model; the worst-edge factor is roughly 0.83. Reducing cure temperature is therefore a substantive process assumption.

The marginal four-axis cases sharpen this qualification. At **all four favorable endpoints**, including cure compression in global/crimp demand changes R15 optimistic actual joining from +15.913790 to −1.821166 kg. Adding free-edge shear to the scarf demand removes four of the thirteen primary float cases even with all four axes at their endpoints: both R12 ISA accounts, R15 optimistic actual joining and R20 optimistic retained floor. These are changes to the model assumptions, not arithmetic discrepancies in the published primary account.

The earlier rigid-037 comparator is preserved, not silently overwritten: only R20 and R30 ISA actual joining floated there, and no retained-floor row did. Its typical W strength, cold-reference shear model, solid density and scarf treatment differ from the later minimum-strength compliant account. Those changes must accompany any comparison of its payloads with the primary 040 values.

## Thermal numerical correction

A 300 K constant field, projected with the original uncorrected 37-midpoint rule into degrees 2–6, generates 242.801285 K of spurious curvature-temperature amplitude in the independent polynomial calculation. The 128-point Gauss-Legendre version gives about 7.1e−10 K. Removing the constant projection is mathematically justified.

The absolute thermal solve remains unclosed from the written boundary details. With the declared optics, conductivity, wind and air properties, the check's explicit sky-view/ground/enclosure interpretation gives, at the old R10 geometry and 2,640 kg/m³ conductivity basis:

The alternative is solved to heat-balance closure and refined through 512 Gauss points. It is a documented sensitivity; it is not fitted to the owner result or to a floating payload. A complete non-code statement of local convection, external radiative views, cavity exchange and pattern extraction is the fix needed to reproduce the owner table and audit its 1.002 guard independently.

## Search interpretation

The lexicographic objective is fewest selected axes, then least common logarithmic usage. It is not a probability of success or a cost function. The favorable E and compression tuple is coupled; compression strength alone is nonbinding because of the scarf screen. The relaxed-E audit uses favorable elastic E with baseline thermal E as an exclusion bound.

Intermediate film weights are mathematical targets rather than established stock products. The use of stock-envelope sheet dimensions at thin gauges, formed-sheet properties, full-radius assembly tolerances and the retained 100 kg systems line remains conditional. Larger radius improves the stated ledger; it does not independently qualify a 40–60 m vehicle.

Omission recoveries are differences of reoptimized endpoints. The reported least γ with Q=.82 and least Q with γ at its envelope belong to separate designs. Adding the recoveries together or combining their coefficients would create an uncomputed design.
