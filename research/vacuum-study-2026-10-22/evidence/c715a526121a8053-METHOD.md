## Cure-compression alternative

Let `σp` be the pressure face stress, `σg` the face-gradient term, `σb` the curvature term and `σc` the balanced cure compression.

| Demand | Filed 048 | Added CHECK-040 row |
|---|---|---|
| Global buckling | σp | σp + σc |
| Core shear crimping | σp + σg + σb | σp + σg + σb + σc |
| Face compression, wrinkling, dimpling, mean scarf shear | σp + σg + σb + σc | same |

Cure compression is not counted twice in local demands. Capacity expressions, global coefficient/Vc convention, free-edge shear, cell-wall yield, current 200 °F cure reference and 30.341397:1 scarf stay as filed. Including cure compression is a sensitivity to an unresolved structural premise, not a validated description of the actual residual field.

The optimizer enumerates all sixteen subsets, minimizes the number of named optimism axes, then their common logarithmic usage. The named 028b endpoint is mask 11; the all-four endpoint is mask 15. Both and every subset trial are saved.

## Retained-water alternative

Water density is 1000 kg/m³. A 0.1 mm equivalent layer means 0.1 kg/m²; 1 mm means 1 kg/m². Changing only this line adds

`ΔM = 0.9 × 4πR² kg`.

This is 1130.973355, 1628.601632 and 2544.690049 kg at R10, R12 and R15. It is constant in face/core geometry and all optimism coefficients. Each optimized endpoint geometry therefore remains optimal under this exact extra debit. The bill and pass flags are recalculated, rather than copying the old positive-payload flag.

The new 1 mm rows cover both the original cure-demand treatment and the cure-included alternative: 432 further configurations. The total is 648 new rows, alongside the immutable original 048 matrix. Zero-exterior-water operation remains the previously filed conditional case; it is not a consequence of any dry-out route and is not rerun here.

For each 1 mm configuration an additional, deliberately favorable optimization uses full elastic E/Fcy, full gamma/Q, minimum film weight and today's smaller thermal E. Its payload bounds every allowed coupled-E choice and smaller subset. Full-E elastic capacity and reduced thermal stress are intentionally combined even though they are not one permitted material tuple. Negative values exclude hidden interior-optimism floats within this scalar model. The same favorable-E construction checks lower-cardinality subsets in the new 0.1 mm rows.

Dry-gas assembly, projected 90% material-water removal at 70 °C, and onboard sorbent retain the same **in-service exterior-water scenario** before and after their material-conditioning accounting. This does not mean an oven cannot evaporate accessible water already present; it means a prelaunch dry state does not establish later rain/dew/frost retention. No source supplies a measured exterior-retention limit for these finishes after these processes.
