**Hexoloy-SA**

Flexure bars are 3×4×45 mm: gross volume 540 mm³. Compression specimen geometry, compression m, fitted scale, sample count and confidence bounds are unspecified. The [current manufacturer page](https://www.ceramicsrefractories.saint-gobain.com/materials/silicon-carbide-sic/hexoloy-silicon-carbide-material/hexoloy-sa-sic-material) instead lists m=8. Both scenarios are retained.

Treating 380 MPa as a Weibull mean gives **derived**, not reported, scales 399.432 MPa at m=10 and 403.507 MPa at m=8. Assuming unpublished 40/20 mm spans gives effective tensile volumes 11.9008/14.8148 mm³. Neither span assignment nor mean interpretation is established for this product data. No compression distribution is inferred from the flexure modulus.

**Statistical and proof-test boundaries**

[ASTM C1424-26, §§4.2–4.4](https://store.astm.org/c1424-26.html) cautions that tensile weakest-link descriptions need not describe compressive distributions, and that coupon tests may not represent a full product. This is why the compression-Weibull results here are explicitly hypotheses.

[ASTM C1683-10(2024)](https://store.astm.org/c1683-10r24.html) treats tensile size scaling under assumptions about compatible flaw populations and stress fields. Volume and surface hypotheses based on the same flexure data are alternatives; they are not independently calibrated hazards.

[NASA CARES/Life, TM-2003-106316](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20030014949) covers surface/volume flaws, multiaxial stresses, proof loading and subcritical crack growth. This card uses transparent scalar projections, not a CARES qualification analysis. Its zero tensile-normal-stress hazard excludes compression damage, contact, other principal stresses and aging. The [NASA 2005 implementation discussion, p.97](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20050237907/downloads/20050237907.pdf) explicitly separates purely compressive failure from its weakest-link model.

No retrieved source supplies a probability-qualified distribution for the selected curved, 275.083 mm OD, 1.137 mm wall, 3.184 m P3 members. No vendor was contacted.
