# CHECK-054 source and qualification audit

Every transfer below is QUESTIONABLE. Assumed installation masses, field rates, working capacities, temperature envelopes and material-life extrapolations are PROJECTION. The primary documents are frozen under `inputs/owner/sources/` or `sources/`; exact custody appears in INPUTS.json. Local copies supplied with the card remain the numerical evidence when a fresh network fetch fails.

**Baseline.** The DuPont Teijin packaging comparison supports 12 µm OD3 metallized PET below 1 g/m²/day at 38°C/90% RH, and 40 for uncoated PET. Charging an OD4 mass without claiming better WVTR is consistent. This does not measure aged seams or service temperature. [Manufacturer sheet](https://blog.outdoorherbivore.com/my_wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Barrier_Properties_of_Flex_Pack_Films.pdf)

**MET.** The retrieved F-UHB-M sheet lists 0.1 at 38°C/**50% RH** and 59.52 m²/kg for 12 µm film. Both values and the added 100 nm opacity allowance are used correctly. The manufacturer describes typical values and limits storage exposure; neither puncture strength nor metal adhesion is a cyclic WVTR qualification. [FlexFilms TDS](https://www.flexfilm.com/pdf/Products/Metallized/BOPET/F-UHB-M.pdf)

**SiOx.** The HX brochure's page 4 table visibly gives 0.08 g/m²/day at 40°C/90% RH. The patent identifies its carrier as 12 µm PET. The added 2 g/m² coating allowance is explicitly unsourced mass, not a measured areal density. The 0.08550851 seven-day budget is only 6.8856% higher than the coupon value; installed measurement remains essential. [Mitsubishi brochure](https://www.mc-india.co.in/data/userfiles/image/TECHBARRIER%20Brochure.pdf), [applicant Table 1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20250296296A1/en)

**Verreal and foil30.** The same applicant table supplies 50 µm PET/Verreal at 0.001 and 30 µm Al/25 µm PET at less than 0.001, measured at 40°C/90% RH. The upper bound is used conservatively. Coating and adhesive additions of 2 and 3 g/m² remain planning allowances. FOIL9's 0.01 is a target, not a measured transfer from this table. [Patent and measurement method](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20250296296A1/en)

**ALD and ML3.** The author article's Table 1 supports 0.0008 for 30 nm alumina. It describes three 30 nm/500 nm alumina/parylene dyads and a 0.00005 g/m²/day instrument sensitivity at 38°C/100% RH. The sub-floor headline is not accepted as a quantified measurement. The paper does not specify PET carrier gauge; its stretch specimens use PDMS and different stacks. The proposed 12 µm hull carrier and coating densities are correctly identified as projections. The frozen EuropePMC full text was inspected; fresh publisher retrieval returned 403. [Wu et al., DOI](https://doi.org/10.1039/C8RA00023A), [archived author article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9078233/)

**Flex and defects.** The card appropriately refuses to equate coating loss with a through-hole or a short coupon bend test with hull-scale durability. Xiao et al.'s bending experiment and the packaging patent motivate testing, but are not a service-interval calibration. [Xiao et al.](https://d-nb.info/1198125438/34), [multilayer packaging disclosure](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20180170017A1/en)

**PVDC and LCP.** The Saranex sheet gives 0.065 g/100 in²/day at 100°F/90% RH and 39.2 ft²/lb; the conversions to 1.007502 g/m²/day and complete-product mass are correct. Kuraray's chart normalizes VECSTAR to 1, with PET/HDPE/PP/PVDC at 137/23/19/7 under 38°C/90% RH. It does not supply an absolute WVTR/gauge pair. [Johns Manville TDS](https://www.jm.com/content/dam/jm/global/en/industrial-insulation/vapor-retarder/saranex-cx-film/JM_IND_Saranex540CXFilm_DS.pdf), [Kuraray chart](https://vecstar.kuraray.com/en/properties/barrier/)

**3A equilibrium.** Lin et al. measured UOP 3A at 298, 313, 333 and 353 K. The manuscript's equation image and Table 2 support the transcribed four-site GSTA model, including the 100 kPa reference pressure. At 333 K and the design water pressure, equilibrium is 8.62318 wt%. Subtracting one percentage point initial loading and multiplying by 0.8 gives 6.09854 wt%, sufficient for the stated 6% screen. At 353 K it is only 2.6550% under the same convention. These are model predictions for the source batch, not demonstrated cartridge working capacities. The paper is from 2015 despite the local filename ending in 2017. [Primary manuscript](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1311254), [ORNL publication record](https://www.ornl.gov/publication/isotherms-water-adsorption-molecular-sieve-3a-influence-cation-composition)

**Regeneration and heat.** Source laboratory regeneration at 503 K, eight hours and below 0.5 Pa differs from the separate planning cycle. Merck supports vacuum treatment at 300–350°C for residual moisture and lists 4.2 MJ/kg adsorption heat, 0.75 kg/L bulk density and heat capacity above 0.8 kJ/kg (its printed unit omits /K). The card's 4.4 MJ/kg, 600 kg/m³ and 1 kJ/kg/K are allowances. They are identified accordingly. A fresh local download returned 403; the primary PDF was readable through the browser. [Merck drying-agent guide, page 8](https://www.merckmillipore.com/deepweb/assets/sigmaaldrich/product/documents/106/819/br3537en-mk.pdf)

**Aramid.** DuPont's Table I-1 supports 3 GPa conditioned-yarn strength and 1440 kg/m³ density. It also gives Kevlar-49 moisture of 3.5%, both as shipped and at equilibrium after bone drying at 24°C/55% RH. The card's 1% water allowance is an explicit assumption, not this manufacturer's equilibrium value. Our 84-case 3.5% sensitivity changes no retained-reserve payloads and produces no pressure failure through 30 days; the largest pressure is 776.2574 Pa against 954.6084 Pa. Actual sorption/release, laminate efficiency, inherited thermal stress and 95% life retention remain unqualified. [DuPont guide](https://www.r-g.de/wiki/images/e/ec/Td_en_Kevlar_guide.pdf)

**UHMWPE.** The manufacturer patent's paragraphs 0099 and 0106–0107 support 42.5/32 cN/dtex strength and \(7\times10^{-6}/1.3\times10^{-6}\) **percent/s** minimum creep rates at 900 MPa/30°C. The thirty-day screen gives 18.144%/3.3696%; neither supports a 1% budget. These rates cannot validate a higher-stress, warmer strength-sized film or a rupture life. [DSM patent](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/6e/89/bd/f4165a0c295be7/US20180112336A1.pdf)

**M5 and PBO humidity.** The cited NASA section records handling-related cracking of the M5 specimens and no meaningful tensile assessment; it does not establish a humid-service allowable. The inherited 5%-of-cold-saturation PBO criterion is an engineering inference from protected/dry yarn observations, not a demonstrated loaded hull moisture-dose limit. These gaps remain open in the owner's register. [NASA tether study](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20050215563/downloads/20050215563.pdf), [NISTIR 7373](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=860679)

**Method documentation.** Two inputs need clearer publication: the full cold-target precision and the effective hardware-volume convention. Both are recoverable from non-code evidence, with all exact differences preserved. Neither supplies missing heat-transfer, dust-filter, cartridge-flow or structural installation qualification.
