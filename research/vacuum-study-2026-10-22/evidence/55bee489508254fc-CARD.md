# MAINLINE-018b — gamma measured on the Sim hemispheres

## Absolute pressures

Flat model, inner sheet 0.42 mm. Figure 9: linear perfect buckling 27.53 MPa. The nonlinear analysis of that same perfect shell is 12.45 MPa. The paper assigns the drop to foam plasticity, not to the dimple. Figure 10: imperfect collapse converges to 6.03 MPa once mu exceeds 0.50.

Dimple model, inner sheet 0.40 mm. Figure 12: linear perfect 27.56 MPa, nonlinear perfect 12.44 MPa. Figure 13: imperfect collapse 5.63 MPa once mu exceeds 0.50. Figure 15 repeats both pairs. Figure 16 KDFs are 0.22 and 0.20. Those KDFs divide by the linear perfect load. They are not gamma.

## Crimp pressure

p_crimp = 2 h^2 Gc / (R_middle tc). Gc is 20 MPa, from the stated foam modulus 0.04 GPa and Poisson 0, because the paper does not state Gc. Middle-surface radius, h = core plus the average face thickness.

Flat: h 0.00352 m, R 0.04083 m, Vc 201.532463528, p_crimp 3.9156534174 MPa. Dimple: h 0.00351 m, R 0.04084 m, Vc 197.24625832, p_crimp 3.89248364981 MPa. Both are shear crimping. Classical no-shear pressure is 789.131279531 MPa flat and 767.777835497 MPa dimple. The robotics checks, about 3.92 MPa, 3.89 MPa and 789 MPa, match this recomputation.

gamma is 1.53997286205 flat and 1.44637730213 dimple.

## Shear-modulus sensitivity

Divinycell H datasheet H.ds-u/10_2012.rev7, opened this wake. ASTM C273. H80 shear modulus 27 MPa, an average, not a guaranteed minimum. At 27 MPa, p_crimp rises to 5.28613211348 MPa flat and 5.25485292724 MPa dimple. gamma falls to 1.14072063856 and 1.07139059417. Both stay above 1.

The same sheet lists H35 compressive modulus 40 MPa, which matches the paper's 0.04 GPa, and H35 shear modulus 12 MPa. H80 compressive modulus on that sheet is 90 MPa, so H80 is the ordered example and not the coupon. At 12 MPa, gamma is 2.56662143676 flat and 2.41062883689 dimple.

## Face bending

Own-face bending is 0.00472319506474 of the sandwich flexural rigidity on the flat shell and 0.00453826240047 on the dimple. That is the term CR-1457 section 4.7 says can be neglected for ordinary buckling. Section 2.3.2 says the crimp formula also neglects facing bending about each face's own middle surface, and that this influence can be considerable. The papers do not separate that term from foam elasticity. Linear pressure over p_crimp is 7.03075504019 flat and 7.08031233513 dimple. That ratio mixes face bending, foam modulus and R/tc near 13. It is not a pure face-bending share.

## Which curve

Both measured gammas exceed 1 at 20 MPa and at the H80 modulus. The numerical collapses sit above the handbook crimp pressure, so they support the optimistic curve and the handbook unity on Vc above 2. They do not support the conservative curve at 0.20.

The 009c-5 least factor is 0.650411843681 at gamma 1. Times the measured gamma it is 1.00161658843 flat and 0.940740927738 dimple. At the H80 modulus the products are 0.74193821365 and 0.696845131658. Design credit on the lead stops at gamma 1, because a collapse above the crimp formula does not raise the design gamma.
