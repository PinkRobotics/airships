# MAINLINE054 source audit — QUESTIONABLE / PROJECTION throughout

No source located measures a complete P3 barrier, its seams and penetrations, at the cyclic service temperatures, strains, UV dose and humidity. Reported coupon WVTR is therefore a **conditional numerical envelope**, never an installed-service specification. Film temperature reaches 321.940 K (48.790 C); the cited principal coupons are at 38–40 C. The cold-film screen is 274.007 K; the h=2 sensitivity is 268.630 K. The coldest allowable water pressure is a separate constraint from the warmest adsorbent temperature.

## Humidity-tolerant reinforcement

The inherited046 guide basis is Kevlar49 conditioned-yarn strength3GPa and density1440kg/m3. Neutral-pH humidity evidence is materially better than the PBO record, but the imposed95% retention, continuous-film efficiency, thermal mismatch and seam life remain unqualified. We compute100%yarn and60%laminate-efficiency bounds, retain light protection and price the resized film. [DuPont Kevlar technical guide, distributor mirror](https://www.r-g.de/wiki/images/e/ec/Td_en_Kevlar_guide.pdf)

The046 SK99 andDM20 creep figures are7e-6 and1.3e-6 **percent/s**, respectively, at900MPa/30C. They are not fractional strain/s and are not validated at the higher stresses of the thin yarn-strength designs. Neither meets a1% creep budget over30days at that test condition. No ten-year creep credit is inferred from chemical-aging estimates. [Manufacturer patent, paragraphs0099,0106–0107](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/6e/89/bd/f4165a0c295be7/US20180112336A1.pdf)

M5/PIPD is not assigned a humidity-proof installed-film strength. NASA's tether investigation records handling-related cracking/splitting and insufficient material for meaningful tensile testing; it is an atomic-oxygen study, not a humidity qualification. Aramid supplies the quantitative alternative requested here. [NASA20050215563, section6](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20050215563/downloads/20050215563.pdf)

The5%RH PBO criterion remains a screening inference from the protected/dry NIST yarn evidence inherited through046. It is not a demonstrated ten-year hull durability boundary. [NISTIR7373](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=860679)
