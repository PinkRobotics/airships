The advisory crimp and wrinkling formulas are replaced by equations read from NASA CR-1457, Manual for Structural Stability Analysis of Sandwich Plates and Shells, R. T. Sullins, G. F. Smith, and E. E. Spier, December 1969, https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19700004831. Section 6.2 restates J. C. Yao, Journal of the Aerospace Sciences, March 1962, as F. J. Plantema rewrote it in Sandwich Construction, Wiley, 1966. The numbers below use the handbook equations. Yao and Plantema were not opened separately.

Equal elastic faces. eta is 1. Face separation h is core thickness plus face thickness, the same h as MAINLINE-007. sigma_0 = E*h / (R*sqrt(1-nu^2)). Crimp stress = h^2*Gc / (2*tf*tc). Vc = sigma_0 / crimp stress. Kc = 1 - Vc/4 when Vc is at most 2, and 1/Vc when Vc is greater than 2. The hemisphere knockdown gamma_d is 0.20 when Vc is below 2, and 1 when crimping governs. Critical face stress = gamma_d*Kc*sigma_0. Critical pressure = 4*that stress*tf/R. Equations 6.2-2 through 6.2-6, 6.2-13, and 6.2-25 through 6.2-29.

gamma_d = 0.20 is the same number as kd. On the moderate-core branch the safety-factor ratio against the MAINLINE-007 buckling factor is exactly Kc. A complete sphere has no edge. The handbook says the 0.20 rests on meager hemisphere tests. R in the ratios below is the external 10 m. The handbook's radius is the middle surface. Using that radius in both the classical pressure and the handbook pressure leaves the ratio unchanged.

Infinite core shear restores Kc = 1 and the kd = 0.2 pressure. That identity passed. The sealed P2-E buckling safety factor 0.233885578993 was recomputed and passed.

Geometries are held. The splits are not searched again. Yield safety factors do not move. No sourced core reached Vc = 2, so none entered the crimping branch, and gamma_d stayed 0.20. At Vc = 2 the handbook changes gamma_d from 0.20 to 1 while Kc is 1/2, so the recommended stress is not continuous there. No reported row is near that line.

## Wrinkling

Solid and foam cores use equation 2.2-5 with Q = 0.50. Uniaxial stress = 0.50*(E*Ec*Gc/(1-nu^2))^(1/3). The sphere is equibiaxial. Equation 2.2-6 is the flat-plate interaction R_x^3 + R_y = 1. With the two stresses equal, the factor is 0.682327803828. The sourced equibiaxial stress safety factor is 0.682327803828 / (1-nu^2)^(1/3) times the advisory uniaxial stress factor, which is 0.709062069568. MAINLINE-007's wrinkling margins were ratios of the modulus product. The stress factor is the cube root of that product ratio.
