The two unretained author full texts were read from their public author copies: [Hong et al.](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/250012990_Evaluation_methods_for_the_hoop_strength_of_small-sized_tubular_ceramic_components) and [Yang et al.](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/386586734_Multi-scale_analysis_of_the_progressive_failure_behaviour_of_25D_woven_SiCSiC_composites_under_compression). The owner's geometry assumptions and lack-of-calibration statements are preserved. This review confirms the cited values but does not establish procurement capability, a failure-law transfer, matched compression tails, inspection detection probability, manufacturing yield or a qualified life model.
