# MAINLINE-040: P2 with its common bill

QUESTIONABLE / PROJECTION throughout. Installed-hull SF remains unknown.

**The retained-floor account does not float at any tested radius or weather choice at the stated favorable endpoints.** That conclusion uses 0.20 kg/m² coating+primer, resized 70 kn pad, vented cells, 100 kg systems, the 24 h sealed-service scenario, and the as-designed cold-reference thermal screen. It is conditional on that explicit coating inventory and cure model.

**At actual joining, ISA only, R30 needs the least optimism: γ and Q.** R20 also needs lighter film. The optimistic and both-weather accounts do not close at any tested radius. Releasing the 5% floor is itself a change of account; the two successful actual-joining rows do not satisfy the retained-floor requirement.

## Sensitivities that can change the answer

At R30 ISA with the retained floor, the maximum coating+primer inventory at the full endpoints is 0.132091 kg/m². The assumed 0.10 kg/m² coating sensitivity gives +362.942 kg there; 0.40 gives −3,029.978 kg. This is why the nominal 0.20 kg/m² conclusion cannot be presented as a measured coating verdict.

The hypothetical half-swing cure sensitivity gives retained-floor ISA endpoint payloads −155.067, +909.506 and +4,783.592 kg at R10, R20 and R30. It is an explicit sensitivity, not a credited cure fix or evidence that an adhesive process can establish that stress-free state. 037b filing status and the final intake time are recorded in CLOSEOUT.json. If no report is filed, the primary account retains the as-designed cold-reference factor.

The 031 choices specify pressure/buoyancy envelopes and a 100 m centre floor. 037's separate solar/thermal environment is retained. Their simultaneous real-weather thermal envelope remains open. There is no certified whole-vehicle SF=1 claim.
