## Equivalent shell: derive both stiffnesses

Let `f = tf`, `h = d + f`, with d the clear core depth. Neglect the core's
in-plane stiffness and transverse shear for this mapping. For equal isotropic
faces the extensional and bending stiffnesses per unit width are

```
A11 = 2 E f / (1 - nu²)
D11 = E (f h²/2 + f³/6) / (1 - nu²)
t_eq = sqrt(12 D11/A11) = sqrt(3 h² + f²)
E_eq = 2 E f/t_eq
thin-face limit: t_eq ≈ sqrt(3) h
```

The card's thin-face thicknesses agree: P2-E 197.052211716 mm and the frozen
Be/CR-III point 73.166912953 mm. Matching the thickness alone while retaining
the face modulus does **not** match membrane stiffness. In the thin-face
approximation, including E_eq makes the Zoelly pressure equal to the COMMON
sandwich pressure to numerical precision. The reported factor 293.149599 is reproduced when the original
face modulus is retained instead.

Hutchinson–Thompson's final discussion gives this effective-modulus/effective-
thickness equivalence under Kirchhoff kinematics. Its existence strengthens the
mapping's theoretical basis; it does not establish a shear-flexible, bonded,
anisotropic honeycomb shell's measured tolerance. [Hutchinson–Thompson 2018,
p. 168](https://groups.seas.harvard.edu/hutchinson/papers/2018-1-Imperfections%26EnergyBarriers.pdf).

The bending-only comparison is a convention difference: using the clear
depth gives `(6 f d²)^(1/3) = 29.604764789 mm`, as reported; using face
separation gives `(6 f h²)^(1/3) = 29.663214496 mm`. Keep the chosen
approximation explicit. They also account for the minor cap-parameter difference:
66.470066095 using clear depth versus the sealed 66.404546027 using face
separation. Neither bending-only thickness is the simultaneous A/D equivalent
thickness.

uncentred RMS at small β0 is `δ β0/sqrt(8)`. For the same dimple,

```
β0 = lambda_I sqrt(t_eq/R) / [12(1-nu²)]^(1/4)

All supplied material properties are QUESTIONABLE. At area S=4πR²:

```

f(x) = (1-x) M/(2 S 1850)
d(x) = x M/(S 25.629541398336222)
sigma_membrane = p R/(4 f)
sigma0 = E h/(R sqrt(1-nu²))
sigma_crimp = Gc h²/(2 f d)
Vc = sigma0/sigma_crimp

global SF = gamma Kc sigma0/sigma_membrane
gamma = kd on the stiff branch; 1 on the crimp branch
