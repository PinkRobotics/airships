# MAINLINE062 manufacturing precedents

**QUESTIONABLE transfer / PROJECTION application throughout.** These records
demonstrate useful fabrication or measurement methods. None establishes a
qualified, unsupported Be sandwich sphere at the required local-defect limit.
The proposed hull diameters are **20, 40 and 60 m** for R10, R20 and R30.

RMS, maximum inward deviation, core-to-face fit and relative segment alignment
are different observables. A small RMS is compatible with an isolated large
dent. We do not multiply RMS by three to infer a maximum. A missing value below
means **not established in the inspected record**, not zero or a claim that no
record exists anywhere. Machine-readable values, source locations and scope
limits are in `PRECEDENTS.json`.

The ALMA measurements and structures come from the
[prototype evaluation, PDF pp.6, 8, 12 and 14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/0609329).
Its AEC panels use aluminum honeycomb and nickel skins with five adjusters;
Vertex uses machined aluminum panels with eight. The cost normalization uses the
[NSF 2006 management review, PDF pp.15 and 20](https://www.nrao.edu/archives/files/original/b60d725dc7b817249207f6b1fa146173.pdf):
$137 million in year-2000 dollars divided by 25 × π × 6² m².
It is not inflation-adjusted or equated to a spherical surface area.

GBT's accuracy is from the
[NRAO performance record](https://www.gb.nrao.edu/GBT/Performance/PlaningObservations.htm);
its 2,004 panels and 7.7-million-kg structure are from the
[NRAO telescope brochure](https://www.nrao.edu/greenbank/2010brochure.pdf).
These are strong precedents for measurement and active correction on a support
structure. They do not demonstrate passive shape retention after that structure
is removed.

S-II's fit figure is from
[the NASA lessons report, PDF p.296, printed p.4-45](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19720008130/downloads/19720008130.pdf).
Matched core profiling transfers as a method. Its materials, thermal history,
bond and loading do not establish the proposed Be process. The separate
hemisphere gamma 0.21–0.22 evidence inherited by 042 must not be assigned
automatically to this flight bulkhead.

The 5.5 m spinning capability is from
[NASA's 2012 fabrication presentation, PDF p.23](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20120014531/downloads/20120014531.pdf).
The inspection descriptions belong to
[the separate 2016 Orion dome report, PDF pp.32 and 36](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20160003616/downloads/20160003616.pdf).
The stretch-forming scale and die gaps are from
[NASA's 1974 study, PDF pp.11 and 22](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19740023230/downloads/19740023230.pdf).
Aluminum forming does not establish Be springback, ductility, thickness loss or
retained sandwich shape. These records do not substantiate a blanket
“5–100 m domes hold 1–5 mm” claim.

JWST's segment figure and collecting area are documented in
[the NASA mission paper, PDF p.4](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20200001556/downloads/20200001556.pdf).
The [NASA media kit](https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/webbmediakit.pdf)
gives the approximately 20 kg mirror-body mass; the
[mirror overview](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/webb/webbs-mirrors/)
describes the segmented Be construction. Precision Be manufacture is real,
but a lightweighted mirror body is not an SR-200 thin face bonded to CR III.
Keck's segmentation and mass come from
[the operator's mirror description](https://keckobservatory.org/a_mirrors_perfect_reflection/);
the [2012 annual report, PDF p.14](https://www.keckobservatory.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/annual-report-2012.pdf)
identifies the gifts whose scope prevents treating them as a mirror quotation.

The transferable candidate is **panel-level contour control, metrology and
temporary adjustment followed by passive closure**. The unsupported retention
step remains the missing demonstration. The 042 estimate of 9.3–16.1 mm expected
dimples remains a projection, and exceeds every conditional SF 1.05 limit
calculated here; telescope RMS does not by itself close that gap.
