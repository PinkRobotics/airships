## Geometry, forces and section resizing

Checker member IDs differ from owner IDs. Member arc lengths are therefore rebuilt from the checker’s own endpoint coordinates:

    L_i = 2 R0 asin(chord_i/(2 R0)).

Each checker subelement points to that same checker member. A first sweep incorrectly paired owner lengths with checker IDs; witness comparisons caught it. The superseded sweep and its failing checks remain in history/. The final model passes the checked witnesses and all 750-member stress and hazard comparisons after sorting physically equivalent members.

Let k=R/10 and let a be tube area expressed at the 10 m reference scale. Keep g/R fixed. At radius R:

    A=a k²; I=a g0² k⁴; L=L0 k;
    ro²=2g²+A/(2π); ri²=2g²−A/(2π).

At fixed g, stiffness matrices scale with a. Pressure forces are independent of a in the inherited linear model. A geometric enlargement at the same pressure gives N~k² and M,T~k³. The checked eigenvalue scales as a/k² in actual-area variables, equivalently as a in reference-area variables. This is an exact scaling identity for that linear model; it is not a nonlinear or manufacturing similarity proof.

With q=applied pressure/P0, use the correlated subelement N and M and its actual member length:

    λE=π² E I/(L² N)
    σc=qN/A + q(M+NL/1000)ro/[I(1−q/λE)]
    σt=max(0, bending−qN/A).

E=410 GPa and density=3100 kg/m³ apply to every tube. Ti/CFRP in earlier cards identify fittings. This adopts CHECK037's corrected material and arc-length interpretation.

The minimum area satisfies all of: 0.2 times the checked perfect-frame eigenvalue ≥1; Euler ≥1; full-member compression target ≥peak compression; tensile projection 15.586 MPa ≥positive tension; and local tube-shell capacity ≥peak amplified compression. The latter is the conservative local-peak substitution already exposed by CHECK041, now enforced. Its uniform-axial alternative is a separate sensitivity. Local shell capacity is

    γ=.099+.901 exp(−sqrt(ro/t)/16),  σlocal=γ*.6E*t/ro.

This local interaction formula and the 0.2 global knockdown remain unvalidated. A thin tube meeting these algebraic checks does not establish joint rotational stiffness, imperfection tolerance or damaged-frame capacity. The final intact cases have no positive longitudinal tensile demand under this pressure-only force envelope.

## Four declared optimism axes

There is no qualified baseline from which a unique physical “least optimism” can be measured. To make the requested comparison reproducible, the optimization uses explicit engineering targets:

| Axis | Lower numerical target | Favorable endpoint |
|---|---:|---:|
| Full-member SiC compression capacity | 1.0 GPa | 3.9 GPa |
| Effective strength of each PBO ply | .6×5.8=3.48 GPa | 5.8 GPa |
| Ten-year strength retention | .96^3.65=.861568874 | .995^10=.951110130 |
| Sleeve allowance | max(actual sleeves, .05×tube mass) | actual sized sleeves |

The 1 GPa value is a chosen qualification target, not a transferred RBSC allowable or a claim that this complete SA frame is available at that strength. The 3.9 GPa catalog compression number is not a fitted Weibull scale. Effective ply strength includes construction efficiency; reaching 5.8 GPa assumes perfect transfer of the yarn number. The lower lifetime value extrapolates the warehouse observation; the favorable value is the 046 required protection target. Neither qualifies a ten-year external crossply.

Enumerate all 16 subsets. A disabled axis stays at its lower target; enabled axes move by a common log fraction u in [0,1]. The sleeve allowance decreases logarithmically from its floor to actual mass. Choose the fewest moving axes whose favorable endpoint closes, then the smallest common u among that count. A 21-point grid checks monotonic payload for every subset and scenario, including discrete pump changes. Find the final zero-payload boundary by bracketing.

The common-u SiC upper target can exceed what the final section needs. Tables therefore show the **actual minimum compression requirement**, alongside the searched upper target in machine data; that excess strength is not necessary. In a root row the payload is zero by definition. The fully favorable endpoint gives available payload if all endpoint assumptions are granted. This is a declared mathematical ranking, not an economic ranking, calibrated confidence score or cross-material qualification.

## Film, sleeves, protection and other debits

The two orthogonal plies share total gauge t. The spherical membrane resultant in each direction is p rc/2, and each directional ply has gauge t/2, so ply stress is p rc/t. Use the checked f5 maximum-chord curvature rc=2.6159809746514977 k m, full sphere area and PBO density 1560 kg/m³.

The 046 hot, fully restrained screen supplies φT=.964012200518 and σthermal=90.501688129 MPa. Thus

    t = p rc/[Fply R10 φT − σthermal].

This keeps the 046 hot thermal envelope in every weather case; no thermal-relaxation benefit is assumed. Film self-weight, cell deformation, seams, biaxial strength and cyclic life are not a qualified installed membrane model.

Each of the 1,500 sleeve ends uses its correlated N,M,T. Sleeve outer radius is sqrt(ro²+Aj/π), section inertia Aj(rj²+ro²)/4, and the longitudinal sleeve screen is N/Aj+M rj/Ij≤1470 MPa. Bond overlap is sqrt[(N+2M/ro)²+(T/ro)²]/(2πro×28.9 MPa). Sleeve density is 1550 kg/m³. All of these are inherited projections. Film-to-frame attachment is in the protection bill; sleeve adhesive is separate: the larger of 5 k² kg installation allowance and the 034 bond-area estimate with 0.2 mm gap and 1400 kg/m³ density. Actual bond mass receives its own outgassing term.

The 046 OD4 white package replaces the 034 OD3 barrier:

    aluminium 100 nm ×2700 kg/m³;
    attachment .025 kg/m²;
    white layer .025 kg/m²;
    seams 5 kg ×actual arc length/774.4408874322422 m.

It costs 95.751050695 kg at R=10. It is charged once. It is not a qualified ten-year coating recipe.

The account also charges geometrically scaled 5.04 k³ kg node allowance; 100 kg general systems; vacuum equipment separately; max(1% of ISA lift, initial gas plus mission external uptake); .1 kg/m² exterior water; 5 k² kg contamination; sag volume at the applicable buoyancy density; and weather headroom. The 040 heavy70 pad mass formula supplies a visible additional load-introduction allowance, 33.137529 kg at R=10. It does not qualify a P3 attachment. Missing attachment details cannot be declared covered merely because a mass line exists.

No gas from outgassing is added a second time to material mass. It changes cavity pressure and pump demand, while its material inventory is already aboard. External ingress is real added mass. The gas reserve is retained even with a pump. All tube bores are vented and evacuated; unvented bore-air penalties are reported separately.

After an assumed demonstrated 90% conditioning removal, the remainder releases with a one-day time constant and water molecular weight. Report first crossings of the 1%-ambient hot total-pressure limit and the 046 dry-cavity criterion: water partial pressure ≤5% of saturation at the h=5 cold-film temperature 274.0071 K, or 32.5165 Pa. Also report the h=2 cold sensitivity. The initial 10 Pa fill is taken as dry. Any residual water fill consumes the humidity budget.

## Reliability and P2 comparison

For each resized member, take the maximum correlated amplified compressive stress over its 24 subelements and apply it uniformly to its entire SiC volume, matching 043's envelope. With m=10 and hypothetical scale σ0=3.9 GPa:

    Hwhole=Σ(σi/σ0)^10;
    Hcoupon=Σ(A Li/1 cm³)(σi/σ0)^10;
    Pf=−expm1(−H).

The first hypothesis reassigns the same scale to every whole member at every radius; the second interprets it as a 1 cm³ material scale. Their enormous difference is the question being exposed. Neither is measured full-member compression reliability. No tension, joint, film, instability, common-cause, lifetime or damage probability is combined into them.

P2 comparison selects the filed heavy70, vented, retained-floor 040 endpoint in all five radii and the 048 MLCCAg, bake70, retained-water, retained-floor endpoint at R=10/12/15. All published subset endpoints are considered and the largest payload is displayed. Subtract 50 kg from each to put the same additional vacuum-equipment allocation outside systems100. P2 geometry is not reoptimized and its life/reliability confidence is not equated with P3. 048 did not calculate R=20/30; these cells remain absent.
