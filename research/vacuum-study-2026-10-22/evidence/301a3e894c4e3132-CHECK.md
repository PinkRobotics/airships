## Objection (NFI.json)

optimistic gamma of 1 and aluminium (Vc 0.5-0.6) the conservative 0.2. Applied to both families, either of 018's
curves puts aluminium ahead:
- Optimistic curve (PROJECTION), gamma = 0.20 + 0.40*min(Vc, 2): 0.847 against 0.609 on the optimistic line, 0.924
  against 0.650 at 5%, and 0.988 against 0.685 at 0%, where aluminium is capped by Be face compression.
- Conservative curve, gamma 0.20 everywhere: 0.557 against 0.253, 0.617 against 0.270, and 0.669 against 0.284.

The "aramid leads" order on every margin line therefore rests on the mixed pairing. The budgets were not re-solved
under the curves.
