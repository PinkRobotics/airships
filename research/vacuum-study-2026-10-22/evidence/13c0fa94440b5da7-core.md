MAINLINE-037b core sourcing summary. Every sourced value is QUESTIONABLE; values converted from US units are worker arithmetic. Full rows: core.json (116 rows).

Honeycomb in-plane mechanics (honeycomb_inplane, 7 rows)
- Hexcel AGU 075b (2000), p.20: "the in-plane stiffness of the honeycomb is negligible compared to that of the facing skins"; Ex and Ey set to about 0.
- Gibson 1981 thesis Table 4.4 and MIT 3.054 lecture 4: regular hexagon bending-dominated E = 1.5 Es (rho*/rho_s)^3 = (4/sqrt3) Es (t/l)^3, nu = 1, G = 0.375 Es (rho*/rho_s)^3. For the 16 kg/m3 core that is about 3e-7 Es, roughly 0.02 MPa.
- GAP: the equal-biaxial stretch stiffness (Es x relative density / 2) is not printed in any page I opened. Allen, Zenkert, NASA CR-1457 and "antiplane core" are unsourced.
