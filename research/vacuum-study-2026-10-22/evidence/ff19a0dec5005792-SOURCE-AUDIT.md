# Source audit and limits

QUESTIONABLE applies to source-property transfer; PROJECTION applies to installed Be performance, manufacturing and assumed operations. A retained primary document is distinguished from the owner's interpretation. This is a partial source audit, not blanket acceptance of every inherited value.

| Claim | Evidence checked | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Selected deposited mass | Patent layer recipe; bulk-density records; arithmetic in CHECK | Recipe arithmetic agrees. Density, application allowance, actual film coverage and direct-Be process remain projections. The recipe/claimed-total-thickness ambiguity is retained in NFI. |
| Multilayer optical pair | [NASA 2018, printed slide 39](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20180002012/downloads/20180002012.pdf) | Class-level hemispherical ε>.80 and α<.09 are described, with dependence on coating requirements. This does not pair an exact patent stack with Be. Owner's PROJECTION label is appropriate. |
| Classical optical citation | [NASA 2015, printed slide 19](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20150018109/downloads/20150018109.pdf) | Source gives α=.08, normal ε=.72 for CCAg/ITO on 3 mil Kapton. The modeled .09/.65 pair needs the corrected source and an explicit emittance-statistic assumption. Adjacent discussion reports storage-related cracking/delamination and possible moisture involvement. |
| Alumina density | Retained manufacturer text [source link unavailable in this public projection] | 3.97 g/cm³ is a bulk/theoretical-density value. Applying it to a deposited stack is a model assumption. |
| Silica density | Retained manufacturer text [source link unavailable in this public projection] | 2.648 g/cm³ is listed. It is not a measured density of the proposed deposited optical film. |
| ITO density | Retained manufacturer sheet [source link unavailable in this public projection] | 7.2 g/cm³ is listed. NiCr's 8.4 g/cm³ remains an assumed mixture density. |
| Silver/FEP masses and optical bounds | [Sheldahl Red Book](https://sheldahl.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/RedBook.pdf), printed 65/67 | Bare masses 28/54 g/m² and adhesive-backed masses 88/115 g/m² refer to distinct constructions. Bare silver/FEP α≤.09, hemispherical ε≥.40/.48 support the selected catalog bounds. The 5% overlap is assumed. |
| Tape adhesion and temperature | Same manufacturer catalog | Adhesion specification is to stainless steel. A broad service-temperature range does not eliminate the adhesive footnote concerning loss of peel strength around −45°C. No Be qualification follows. |
| FEP moisture/weathering | [Chemours film bulletin](https://www.chemours.com/en/-/media/files/teflon/teflon-fep-film-properties-bulletin.pdf) | Base-film moisture absorption <.01%; long outdoor-exposure stability is reported, with a Type-C exclusion. Whole tape, metal edges, PSA uptake and overlaps require separate evidence. |
| AZ93 optics/thickness | [Manufacturer page](https://www.aztechnology.com/product/1/az-93), retained HTML | α=.15±.02 is tied to at least 5 mil; ε=.91±.02. Nominal thickness tolerance cannot be used to claim the optical specification at every thinner point. No dry areal mass is established here. |
| MLP300 optical primer | [Manufacturer page](https://www.aztechnology.com/product/29/mlp-300-az), retained HTML | Nominal .75±.25 mil and 1.0 g/cm³ are specified. This is distinct from the model's assumed additional structural-primer charge. Be compatibility and actual finish system still need evidence. |
| AF163-2U moisture proxy | Retained NASA query response [source link unavailable in this public projection] | Four named 1994 rows: GSFC22348, 22351, 22354, 22357; WVR .12/.09/.20/.15%, TML 2.59/3.17/4.85/4.42%, with different cures. The selected worst WVR is not an installed equilibrium isotherm. |
| Meaning of WVR/TML | [NASA guide](https://etd.gsfc.nasa.gov/capabilities/outgassing-database/user-guide) | Distinct measurements. WVR follows vacuum outgassing and 24 h re-exposure at 25°C/50% RH. Neither directly specifies the proposed 200°F cure's installed water inventory. The nominal 90% conditioning credit remains assumed. |
| Coated-core water | Retained 5056 query [source link unavailable in this public projection] | This query returned no matching row. It supplies no measured basis for .001 core water fraction. Absence of a query hit is not proof that no applicable evidence exists. |
| 70°C drying and high uptake | [Yasuoka et al.](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/transjsme/88/916/88_22-00284/_article/-char/en), retained full paper | AF163-2M coupons, 121°C cure, average adhesive thickness 1.21 mm and 70°C vacuum preconditioning are described. Fitted hot-immersion uptake 8.72–9.16% is not ambient 2U uptake or evidence for 90% removal from a hull. |
| Sorbent capacity | Retained BASF brochure [source link unavailable in this public projection] | 4A equilibrium water capacity 21 wt% is at 25°C/80% RH. The account's 10% usable capacity and 95% capture at hull vacuum are assumptions, not those test conditions. |
| Exterior water | 016 pinned input [source link unavailable in this public projection], 016 card [source link unavailable in this public projection] | The .1–1 mm retained-film band is explicitly an owner assumption. The model selects its .1 mm lower endpoint; it is separate from adhesive and coating uptake. |
| Dew-point coefficients | [NIST water page](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C7732185&Mask=4) | The Antoine relation uses pressure in bar and temperature in kelvin. The owner has a numerical result, but its planned independent arithmetic was not completed before refusal. |
| SolarWhite, Z93, bare Be and other secondary finishes | Owner's retained sources and notes | Not independently completed as a full claim-by-claim audit. The SolarWhite reflectance/absorptance label ambiguity is retained. Do not treat the source bundle's presence as independent verification. |
| Inherited mechanical/cure properties | Pinned 028b/033/034/037/037b/040 inputs | Used as declared QUESTIONABLE/PROJECTION inputs. The check reconstructs equations but does not establish certified compression, bond, scarf, cell-wall or whole-hull allowables. |

Browser-only patent and Sheldahl observations are recorded as dated source notes, not as immutable raw-document pins. Other retained copies and extracted text are in the input manifest. No owner script was accessed during source review.
