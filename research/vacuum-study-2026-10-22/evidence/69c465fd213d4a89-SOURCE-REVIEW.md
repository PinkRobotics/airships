Independent review of MAINLINE-014b and MAINLINE-009c-5

The two owner programs were neither read nor executed. `independent_check.py` uses data files and independently downloaded primary PDFs. All owner and inherited input digests matched their pins. `sources/URL-CUSTODY.json` and `sources/EXTRA-URL-CUSTODY.json` identify the downloads. Local PNGs preserve the visually inspected equations and table headings.

The frozen case is external radius 10 m, pressure 95460.83791390515 Pa, lift 4889.442150186961 kg, face Poisson ratio 0.33, joint mass 5% of structural mass, and systems mass 100 kg. These are inherited case inputs, not new atmospheric measurements.

With area A=4πR², reserve fraction m, and core mass fraction x, our reconstruction uses M=((1−m)L−100)/1.05, tf=M(1−x)/(2Aρf), tc=Mx/(Aρc), and h=tc+tf. Applied face stress is pR/(4tf). Classical reference stress is Ef h/[R√(1−ν²)], crimp stress is Gc h²/(2tf tc), and Vc is their ratio. Kc=1−Vc/4 through 2, then 1/Vc. Multiply the reference stress by gamma*Kc before dividing by applied stress. The handbook's weak-core convention includes equality at 2; 009c-5's stated convention assigns gamma=kd at equality. None of the 1,530 optimized core rows lands there; the closest distance is 9.363671427609077e-5. Hemisphere gamma=.20 is an acknowledged transfer to this complete-sphere estimate. See [NASA CR-1457, printed pp. 6-5, 6-7, 6-10, 6-15](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19700004831/downloads/19700004831.pdf).

Our equibiaxial factor solves b³+b=1. Honeycomb allowable is b*q*sqrt(Ef Ec tf/tc); foam allowable is b*q*(Ef Ec Gc/(1−ν²))^(1/3). Design q is .33 or .50 respectively. Face strength, crush, and dimpling divide the stated allowables by their applied stresses. The dimpling formula and p/2 crush target are brief assumptions. The .82 column changes q at fixed geometry. Honeycomb wrinkling in this selected expression contains no Gc. [CR-1457, printed pp. 2-26 and 2-33](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19700004831/downloads/19700004831.pdf).

The independent search used x=j/8000, j=1…7999, for every face/core/kd combination. It reproduces all filed optima. This is a finite-grid result; it is not a continuous optimality certificate. An independent root calculation also refines the 009c-5 crimp/wrinkle balance.

Vendor tables were checked against their URLs, not merely the owners' transcriptions. Twenty-five [Hexcel CR-III rows, PDF pp. 4–5](https://www.hexcel.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/HexWeb_CRIII_DataSheet.pdf), six [HFT/HRH-49 rows, PDF pp. 31–32](https://www.hexcel.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/HexWebHoneycombAttributesandProperties-1.pdf), and twelve [Plascore rows, PDF p. 2](https://plascore.com/wp-content/uploads/dlm_uploads/2025/08/PLA_PAMG-XR1-5052_2023-06-02.pdf) match. Modulus, shear, density, cell and strength columns and their unit conversions agree. Plascore Ec is explicitly transferred from matching CR-III rows; it is not a Plascore measurement. The [HRH-10-4.8-32 row, PDF p. 4](https://www.hexcel.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/HexWeb_HRH10_DataSheet_eu1.pdf) confirms Ec=76 MPa, GW=14 MPa and bare compression=1 MPa. The [ROHACELL sheet, PDF p. 1](https://cdn.sanity.io/files/dorbplvp/production/ed045b3e26f3de6b4faf1533d03ca5c1b66f8ae0.pdf) confirms the foam inputs. Six density projections reproduce their stated scaling and retain PROJECTION tags. All 15 face rows match their pinned MAINLINE-008 numbers, sources and tags; this does not independently validate every inherited material assumption.

The cited [Materion comparison, PDF p. 2](https://hkvxni.files.cmp.optimizely.com/download/c3141b9ea04211ee9a7b2a5d2cb825d4) assigns 290 GPa to S200F and 303 GPa to S200FH. That differs from both cards' inherited S200F input. The independently obtained [Materion design guide, PDF pp. 4–5](https://hkvxni.files.cmp.optimizely.com/download/dafce45ca04211ee97ee126db737ac74) gives generic beryllium modulus 303 GPa and approximately 255 MPa for both S200F yields. An indexed older MB-001 excerpt distinguishes 255/283 MPa; its full PDF now requires login. Therefore the grade-specific modulus mismatch is concrete, while the strength requires edition reconciliation. No forced substitution of the strength is made.

The .82 honeycomb expression is also present in [NASA/CR-1999-208994, equations 10–11](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19990017863/downloads/19990017863.pdf). Its discussion after equation 17 calls for laminate bending stiffness rather than an equivalent membrane modulus for composite faces. The cards' scalar QI moduli remain estimate assumptions. A layup-dependent correction cannot be calculated from the supplied inputs.

All observations are NFIs or fix proposals. They neither stop the owner nor grant certification, a physical validation, or programme acceptance. The only unobservable numerical leaf is the owner's historical elapsed-time measurement; no independent timing verification is claimed.
