# be-face: beryllium sheet face properties, history (card MAINLINE-017b)

All sourced values are tagged QUESTIONABLE; calculations are tagged PROJECTION or DERIVED. Details, quotes and URLs are in be-face.json (61 rows, 5 limits).

**Tensile yield minimum (SR-200 sheet).** 345 MPa (50 ksi) in 1966 (NASA TM X-53453), 1972, 1983, the 1992 Brush Wellman specification and the 2024 Materion datasheet. No change in 58 years. Minimum elongation rose from 5 percent (1966) to 10 percent (by 1972).

**Compressive yield.** Today's datasheet prints none. Best sheet values: 379 MPa (one 1.96 mm lot, 1967), 379 MPa design (Lockheed 1971), and 303 MPa A-basis / 338 MPa B-basis on 3 mm sheet (Lockheed 1972). A 1990s brochure says compressive yield is "at least equal" to tensile yield. No values for about 2006 or 2016. No gain in 50 years.

**Modulus.** 276-303 GPa (1966), 293-303 GPa (1967-1991), 303 GPa (1990s, 2020 guide), 290 GPa (2024). The drop is what is printed, not a measured change. Derived from sourced single-crystal constants, the ideal random polycrystal is about 307 GPa, so sheet is within about 1-6 percent of its limit.

**Largest sheet.** 36 x 96 in listed 1966; 30 x 80 in (3 mm) delivered 1972; 24 x 72 in at 0.021-0.030 in in a 1990s table; 24 x 72 in at 0.059-0.250 in today, thinner gauges smaller. Capability has shrunk. At 0.7-0.85 mm no maximum is stated today.

**Thickness tolerance.** +/-0.004 in (0.102 mm) at 0.025-0.034 in, identical in the 1992 specification and 2024 (special grade +/-0.002 in in 1992). Flatness: only 2024 found (Type 1 2 percent, Type 3 0.030 in per foot). The 1992 scan lacks its flatness clause. 1960s-70s: no numbers.

**Specific stiffness.** Beryllium 157-164 MPa per kg/m3 in every era (E 290-303 GPa over 1850 kg/m3). Carbon fibre axial values are 387 (P-120, 1992) to 423 (K13D2U, K1100, 2007), but these are fibre-direction only. A quasi-isotropic K13D2U or K1100 laminate is estimated (PROJECTION, assumed matrix constants) at about 98-107, about 35 percent below beryllium. No sourced quasi-isotropic test value found. Only one-direction laminates (patent, 1994: 164) match beryllium.

**Limits.** Theoretical density about 1845 kg/m3 (derived) against 1850 printed. Brush Wellman called beryllium "unsurpassed" in specific modulus (1990s). Beryllium is near its physical limit.

**Main gaps.** DTIC and MIL-HDBK-5 data unreadable; no ~2006 or ~2016 SR-200 datasheets; fibre introduction years; flatness before 2024.
