# MAINLINE-068 face projections

Unit vacuum-mainline068-face-projections-1020. Literature arithmetic only. No hull model was run. QUESTIONABLE. PROJECTION. No qualified hull safety factor.

The index is density over modulus to the power 0.514. Higher is better. The baseline is programme SR-200 at 290 GPa and 1850 kg/m3, the tuple REVIEW-D used. Improvement is that baseline index divided by the candidate index. The concept line is 1.862737885 and the TRL 7 line is 1.614325129, the joint factors 066 measured, set by R 20 optimistic, at 993.16 and 1145.99 kg/m3. Those two lines are the local slope at concept R 30 ISA. They are not a re-optimised hull clear. 066's payloads sit at 293.6 GPa and 379.2 MPa, not at 290 GPa and 345 MPa. P2 face gauges from that run are 2.093 mm at R 20 ISA and 3.147 mm at R 30 ISA. Two-face areas are 10053.1 m2 and 22619.5 m2.

## SR-200 and a lighter beryllium sheet

Today's measured sheet is SR-200. Improvement 1. The concept multiplier still needed is 1.863 and the TRL 7 multiplier is 1.614. The form is the programme face. No published beryllium sheet at 993 or 1146 kg/m3 was opened, so the lighter sheet is the target itself and not a product. At constant 290 GPa the concept density is 993.16 kg/m3. A stretch-dominated porous beryllium that hits the same index would sit near 514 kg/m3 and 81 GPa. That architecture is not built. Bending-dominated porosity, modulus falling with the square of density, does not improve this index.

## Aluminium-beryllium sheet

AlBeMet AM162 rolled sheet is a real isotropic product: 2.071 g/cm3, 193 GPa, yield 276 MPa, ultimate 379 MPa on the rolled-sheet minimum. Improvement 0.725. It still needs 2.571 for the concept and 2.228 for TRL 7. AM140 is a lower beryllium fraction and is softer. The opened sheet CTE is 13.91 ppm per degree at 25 C. No bond to CR-III and no match to the core were calculated. This route moves away from the target.

## CVD diamond

The mechanical page checked in REVIEW-D gives 1050 GPa, 3.515 g/cm3 and 750 MPa at 500 µm on the nucleation side. Improvement 1.020. It still needs 1.827 and 1.583. Disks are up to 120 mm, so R 20 still needs 888,889 pieces. That is not a hull face. Holding 1050 GPa and hitting the concept index would require 1924 kg/m3, which is not solid diamond. A stretch-dominated porous diamond would need about 1017 kg/m3 and 304 GPa. That is not built. A 5-inch wafer's hardness is not a modulus and is not used here.

## Graphite and graphene films

The opened in-plane film is the mirror-like graphite of DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-62227-6. Mean Young's modulus 969 GPa, give or take 69, highest specimen 1020 GPa, tensile strength 1.29 GPa, density 2.25 g/cm3. Improvement 1.529. It still needs 1.219 for the concept and 1.056 for TRL 7. At constant 969 GPa the concept density would be 1846 kg/m3 and the TRL 7 density 2130 kg/m3. The basal plane of graphite is isotropic, so the uniaxial modulus is the right Young's modulus for this index. The test is still tension, not compression. Process thicknesses opened are about 2 µm, 25 µm and about 145 µm. Those are intermediates. The dogbone thickness was not extracted separately. A 2.093 mm face is many layers of any of those films, and a stack was not tested. Foil planform was not stated. It is not 10053 m2.

The same paper's supplementary table lists graphene and reduced-graphene-oxide films. The highest modulus row there is 0.0987 TPa at 0.6 µm. Pairing that modulus with 2.25 g/cm3 is QUESTIONABLE, because the density belongs to the mirror film. That pairing scores 0.472 and still needs 3.94. It is not the route.

A stretch-dominated porous version of the mirror film would hit the concept index near 1498 kg/m3 and 645 GPa. That architecture is not built. The published trend does not by itself reach 1.86 at the measured density.

## CNT yarn laminates

Unidirectional table means from DOI 10.1021/acsanm.3c01266, as indexed: AroCy XU 371, 329 GPa at 1.38 g/cm3, improvement 1.430, still needs 1.302 and 1.129. Araldite MT 35610, 353 GPa at 1.42 g/cm3, improvement 1.441, still needs 1.292 and 1.120. Both are tensile coupons about 0.19 mm and 0.20 mm thick, not a membrane. The abstract's 256 GPa per g/cm3, placed on the 1.38 density, is 353 GPa and scores 1.484, still short of the graphite film. M60J unidirectional, carried from REVIEW-D at 1.262, still needs 1.476. The Toray sheet was not re-opened.

No cross-ply or quasi-isotropic modulus was read. DOI 10.1016/j.compositesb.2024.111465 reports those layups, and an index extract gives laminate thicknesses 205 µm, 390 µm and 680 µm. Figure 4 and Appendix D were not opened. With transverse modulus neglected, a cross-ply is half the unidirectional modulus and scores 1.002 at the AroCy density and 1.009 at the benzoxazine density, so it still needs about 1.85, the same as staying on beryllium. A fiber-only quasi-isotropic modulus, one third of the ply, scores 0.813 and 0.819 and still needs about 2.3. The 3/8 shortcut scores 0.864. To clear the concept as that one-third quasi-isotropic laminate at 1380 kg/m3, the ply would need about 1650 GPa, about five times the opened 329 GPa.

A cross-ply cannot outrank the graphite film unless its modulus exceeds the opened unidirectional modulus. The best opened unidirectional improvement is 1.441, below 1.529. The unread figure does not change the leader on that bound, unless the multidirectional density is far below 1.38 g/cm3. That density was not re-opened.

## Architected faces

Carbon plate-nanolattices reach 21.6 GPa at a density at or below 0.792 g/cm3. Improvement 0.615. They still need 3.030 and 2.626. The constituent cited for the bound is about 62 GPa at about 1.4 g/cm3, which scores 0.598. Micrometre cells made by two-photon lithography are not a face over 10053 m2. Because the constituent is soft, this architecture cannot reach 1.86. An architected diamond or beryllium helps only when modulus falls no faster than density, and neither has been built at the densities above.

## What every route still must show

Compressive strength, because every CNT and graphite number opened is tensile, and 066's strength slope is zero only at 345 MPa. A makeable gauge over 10053.1 m2 and 22619.5 m2 at about 2.1 mm and 3.1 mm. A bond to the CR-III core. A thermal expansion the core and bond can carry. The thermal interface near 1.07 is the beryllium case. It was not recomputed for these faces.

## Ranking

By the concept multiplier still needed, measured membrane-capable forms only: graphite film 1.219, diamond 1.827, SR-200 1.863, AlBeMet 2.571, plate-nanolattice 3.030. Unidirectional CNT is a ceiling, not a row in that list, and its ceiling still sits behind the film. Classical CNT laminates sit with SR-200 or behind it. None is under 1.

The demonstration that changes the ranking is in-plane compression of the graphite film built up to about 2.1 mm, with modulus, density and compressive strength reported. If the compressive modulus stays near 969 GPa at 2.25 g/cm3, the film remains first and still short of both lines. If that modulus falls below the diamond improvement of 1.020, diamond or SR-200 becomes the best measured membrane. A CNT cross-ply modulus cannot take first place on the numbers opened here.

## Answer

T3 and T7 stay on SR-200 bonded to CR-III. The materials research direction is the mirror-like graphite film, because it is the closest opened membrane and it does not yet clear. Its first coupon-scale test is that compressed stack near 2.1 mm. No purchase. A purchase would be his decision. The lighter beryllium sheet at 993 kg/m3 remains the incumbent's target and has no published product.
