# MAINLINE-072b graphite faces on a core they can carry

Unit vacuum-mainline072-graphite-matched-core-1020. QUESTIONABLE. PROJECTION. No qualified hull safety factor. One simjob. No finite-element run.

## The cores

Published through-thickness expansions below or near 12.2e-6 per K exist in four families: Kevlar (Hexcel family table 2.7e-6/F = 4.86e-6/K; HRH-49 1/4-2.1, 33.6 kg/m3, full data, flagged preliminary), carbon (Ultracor UCF-51's measured 4.5e-6/K across thickness; UCF-355 3/8-3.5, 56 kg/m3, full data, CTE a cross-grade family value), quartz (UQF-95, no CTE, no W modulus), and the non-catalog metals (titanium 8.6, beryllium 11.3, Invar 1.2: alloy values, braze-formed panels only). Glass publishes 8.2e-6/F = 14.76e-6/K (HRH-327, HRP). Nomex at 19.4e-6/F fails the screen. Carbon foam at 5.0e-6/K is 30x the core density. Every value is datasheet-typical, QUESTIONABLE; the admissibility limits are in NFI.json. UCF-355's ±45 wall modulus is not published and its whole verdict turns on it.

## The hull

071's model copy with the core expansion and wall material parametrized; reproductions 0.00099 kg (beryllium baseline) and 0.0 kg (graphite rows). Gamma is the CR-1457 step; Q 0.33; the film is 070's spec at -1.0e-6/K signed.

CR-III on the published family CTE (23.76e-6) instead of the installed alloy 23.6e-6: interface 0.5325 against 0.5359. The aluminium verdict survives the convention.

**UCF-355 carbon: no feasible geometry in any case.** The low CTE is real, but the core's own bond-shear stiffness (kb 632 MPa, G 161 MPa; 3.3x CR-III's sqrt(2·kb·G)) holds the interface under the 25 psi screen. Release needs alpha_core at or below 2.99e-6, or a wall modulus under about 18 GPa: at half the assumed 35 GPa the margin reaches 1.025. At the released expansion the density factor is 4.69-5.40.

**HRH-327 glass: no feasible geometry.** Release needs alpha_core at or below 8.76e-6, under every published glass value. Even half kb only reaches 12.8e-6. Factor 2.79-3.23 at its released expansion.

**HRH-49 Kevlar: feasible in all eight cases — the first core whose published expansion the film can carry** (interface 3.86 at stated density, 2.73-5.45 across kb x[0.5,2]). The problem becomes mechanical. W shear modulus 9.0 MPa, 0.19 of CR-III's, drives the core to 108-166 mm and 18.2-63.2 t; scarf shear and shear crimping co-bind at 1; Vc 17.5-18.0 keeps gamma at the step's 1. Payloads at concept are -27250, -29373, -90223 and -97399 kg (R20 ISA, R20 opt, R30 ISA, R30 opt); at TRL 7, -21402, -23377, -70681 and -77357. The face-density factor to float is 2.673, 3.038, 2.673 and 2.927 at concept, to 842, 741, 842 and 769 kg/m3; and 2.219, 2.408, 2.160 and 2.340 at TRL 7, to 1014, 934, 1042 and 961 kg/m3. R20 optimistic sets both maturities.

## The answer

No real core makes the graphite route feasible. Aluminium fails on expansion, glass fails on expansion at its own stiffness, carbon carries the expansion but fails on its own interface stiffness, and the one core that releases the interface pays for it in shear: the factor the route still needs is face density, 741-842 kg/m3 at concept and 934-1042 at TRL 7 at constant 969 GPa - factors 2.16-3.04, against CR-III's gamma-step 1.067-1.248. The named improvements, in order of leverage: a measured ±45 wall-laminate modulus and in-plane CTE for the Ultracor carbon grades (the carbon interface releases below ~18 GPa or ~3e-6); a Kevlar-family core with higher W shear modulus at 2.7e-6/F-class expansion (HRH-49's 9 MPa sets the 108-166 mm core); and, unchanged from 071, the face itself.
