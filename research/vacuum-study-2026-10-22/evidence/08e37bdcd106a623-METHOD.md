# CHECK055 method

All inherited material values are QUESTIONABLE. Stand-ins, test articles, costs and transfers are PROJECTION. Nothing is a certification.

**Independence.** The owner supplied CARD, RESULTS, INPUTS, NFI and 23 JSON run records. Only data, written methods, published sources and this seat's previously authored launcher were used. No foreign scientific executable was opened, hashed, copied, imported or run. The numerical implementation in `independent_fe.py` was written from the weak form below. Earlier authorship of 028b and CHECK035 is disclosed. The owner's result arrays were used as comparison targets and to select common prescribed pole displacements, not as a numerical solver.

**Scalar equations and units.** Section calculations use mm, MPa and N; pressure is converted from MPa to kPa. Set `h=tf+tc`, `t_eq=sqrt(3)*h`, `p_cl=4 E tf h/(R² sqrt(1-nu²))`, `p_crimp=2 h² G/(R tc)`, `Vc=p_cl/p_crimp`, and `Kc=1−Vc/4` for `Vc≤2`, otherwise `1/Vc`. Gamma uses `p_H=Kc*p_cl`. The inherited reference equations are traced through 029 and [NASA CR-1457](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19700004831/downloads/19700004831.pdf); this check verifies their arithmetic, not a new validation of their sandwich applicability.

For each surrogate, `tf*=tf E/E* sqrt[(1−nu*²)/(1−nu²)]` and `tc*=h−tf*`. Operating face compression is `pR/(4tf)`. Biaxial stiffness is proportional to `E tf/(1−nu)`. The unenhanced 0.82 wrinkle expression in this card is `0.82 sqrt(E Ec tf/tc)`, with wavelength `2 pi (E tf³ tc/(24 Ec))^(1/4)`. Its cell-dimpling expression is `2 E/(1−nu²)*(tf/cell)²`. These conventions reproduce the owner's groups; they do not replace the biaxial and joint rules in the full hull account.

The core moduli are converted from the pinned Hexcel values: normal and L shear 15ksi, W shear 6.8ksi. Cell size is 9.525mm. Stress crossings use 35/25psi for typical/minimum W shear, 55/45psi for L shear, and 35psi crush. No normal-strength allowable is inferred from the crush plateau.

**Geometry and miniature.** A spherical cap of base radius `a` on radius `R` has rise `R−sqrt(R²−a²)` and area `2 pi R rise`. The panel-area divisor is the exact 24-by-72-inch envelope, 0.6096 by 1.8288m, not the rounded heading. The manufacturer's reduced-gauge stock restriction remains open. The shallow-cap parameter is `[12(1−0.33²)]^(1/4) sqrt(2 rise/t_eq)`. Arc distances use `R asin(a/R)` separately.

The inherited dimple width is `sqrt(R*t_eq)/[12(1−0.33²)]^(1/4)`, retaining the 015b Poisson convention. The actual Be Poisson ratio is not silently substituted. The 038 miniature calculation uses E*=69GPa, nu*=0.33 and stiffness factor `(E*/E)*sqrt(1−nu²)/sqrt(1−nu*²)`, applied to pressure and core shear modulus. Homothetic face/core/defect lengths scale together; retaining stock cells breaks cell-scale similarity.

**Independent FE.** The strain energy is `∫(1/2) E:C:E dV0`, with Green strain `E=(FᵀF−I)/2`. Eight-node serendipity quadrilaterals use 3×3 Gauss integration. Axisymmetry adds hoop stretch `(r+u_r)/r` and reference volume weight `2 pi r det(J)`. Faces use three-dimensional isotropic elasticity. The core has zero Poisson couplings, in-plane modulus 0.01MPa and independent tangent-normal shear, rotated with the reference meridian tangent.

Every layer receives the same stress-free radial offset `−amplitude*exp[−(R theta/width)²]`. The exterior nominal radius is the supplied R. Full-sphere meshes span theta=0 to pi with one axial south-inner-pole constraint; radial pole motions are constrained. Cap meshes end at `asin(a/R)` and constrain both displacements of all edge nodes. Meridional nodes follow `(j/(2n))^1.5`. Baselines use one element per face and four through the core, n=512 for spheres and n=256 for caps.

The follower pressure acts on the current outer quadratic edge. Its nodal force per unit pressure is `2 pi ∫ N_i r (z',−r') ds`, differentiated for load stiffness. The tangent is the material plus geometric stiffness minus pressure times load stiffness. A bordered Newton solve controls outer north-pole axial displacement and solves pressure as the additional unknown; failed increments are subdivided. Reported baseline maxima use the same pole coordinates as the filed run data. Declining pressures beyond every reported maximum are retained.

**Stress diagnostics.** Cauchy stress is `F S Fᵀ/det(F)`. Face diagnostics take the maximum full von Mises stress over all 3×3 Gauss points in the appropriate layer. Core shear and normal stress are resolved into the current meridian tangent and its perpendicular normal. First crossings are linearly interpolated between consecutive equilibrium points. No extrapolation to a material interface or a real reinforced edge is performed. Exact differences from the owner's unspecified diagnostic details remain in the comparison tables.

**Checks and refinement.** Partition of unity, internal tangent finite differences, pressure tangent finite differences, symmetry on free equations and exact thick-sphere linear elasticity are verified in FE-VALIDATION.json. The two membrane solutions agree with the exact continuum result to approximately 3e−9 relative. The initial tiny-load nonlinear validation stalled at floating-point force noise; the exact small-strain benchmark is instead solved as linear elasticity. An early high-Poisson run exposed cancellation in Green strain; evaluating the algebraically identical `sym(H)+HᵀH/2` form fixed it. Completed earlier runs and all failed attempts remain recorded.

Refinement doubles meridional resolution and uses two elements per face and six through the core. The baseline relative maximum-force residual tolerance is 1e−7. Refined meshes use 1e−6 after observed force roundoff near 1e−7; every actual residual is retained. Sampled maxima and a separately sampled finer peak bracket are distinguished. The cap-edge stress drift means no mesh-converged edge allowable is claimed.

**Direct dimple roots.** For face and core limits, solve load-controlled equilibrium from zero to operating pressure in twelve steps, then vary amplitude with Brent's method until the named stress equals its proxy. For the elastic limit, follow each amplitude's displacement-controlled path through the pressure maximum, optimize pressure over a bracket in pole displacement, then solve amplitude for peak pressure equal to operating. TOLERANCE-ROOTS.json and both trial logs preserve the results. These are roots of the chosen coarse elastic branch; material instability and unmodeled modes prevent physical qualification.

**Core ellipticity.** Independently construct the orthotropic StVK fourth-order tensor in the meridional tangent/normal plane. Under simple shear `F=I+g e_t⊗e_n`, the acoustic tensor for a reference unit normal n is `Q=F (C:nn) Fᵀ + (nᵀ S n) I`. Minimize its least eigenvalue over directions in that plane, then find the first shear where it reaches zero. The computed Cauchy shear is 0.087740979MPa in W for in-plane E=0.01MPa. This simple-shear result is not a full-state FE eigenvalue test.

**Statistics.** The one-sided normal 90%-coverage/95%-confidence factor is `k=t_nct(0.95,n−1,z_0.90 sqrt(n))/sqrt(n)`. If sample standard deviation is represented by `CV*mean`, the planning mean is `target/(1−k CV)` only when the denominator is positive. Gamma≤1 is a separate assumption. Distinct seeded amplitudes are not iid replicates of one amplitude population. Formula basis: [NIST tolerance intervals](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc263.htm).

**Scope.** No seams, bond failure, cell-resolved behavior, plasticity, actual edge reinforcement, water-gradient response, thermal residuals or nonaxisymmetric bifurcations are modeled. Every pressure maximum is conditional. All heavy calculations were admitted through simjob, with fixed one-core allocations. Start/end measurements are records, not work limits.
