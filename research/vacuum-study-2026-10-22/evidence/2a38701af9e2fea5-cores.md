## 5. Through-thickness conductivity

Nomex 410 paper 0.103 to 0.123 W/(m K) at 150 C [DuPont]. Kevlar fibre 0.04 W/(m K).
## 6. Bonding to carbon faces

Their peel and flatwise data are on aluminium core.
