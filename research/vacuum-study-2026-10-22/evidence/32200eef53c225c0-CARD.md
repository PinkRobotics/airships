## Retention supported by the sources

016 calls 0.1–1 mm an assumed equivalent retained surface-film band. 019 selects 0.1 mm for its optimistic planning scenario and 1 mm for a separate conservative scenario. **Neither source measures either retention for these finishes or establishes an upper bound.** The atmospheric `both` weather set does not automatically change the selected vehicle-water charge.

Before processing, actual exterior liquid is unmeasured. After each listed dry-out route, no measured exterior-retention limit is established. A bake can evaporate accessible liquid already present, and a controlled dry environment can support a dry release; neither proves subsequent rain/dew/frost retention. Internal sorbent changes internal vapor distribution and keeps captured mass aboard. No route earns an in-service exterior-water reduction.

## Exact bond-qualification gap

The detailed 200 °F source test uses **AF163-2K .060 lb/ft², EC-3924B primer, metal-to-metal joints and 50 psi cure pressure**. It does not qualify **AF163-2U .015 lb/ft² on Be/5056 honeycomb**, its primer or its tooling load path. The 200 °F model reference does not establish actual stress-free temperature. This explicit finding is `048A-200F-2U015-BOND` in [NFI.json](8df81646550a6f8e-NFI.json), with the primary sheet and page recorded in [WATER-AND-BOND-SOURCES.md](f700179baa6c65a9-WATER-AND-BOND-SOURCES.md).

All original 048 and CHECK-040 findings are retained with attribution, including cell-scale edge transfer, cure tooling, wet optics and physical thermal-boundary questions. The complete existing solver boundary is supplied in [THERMAL-BOUNDARY.md](ebbb59a4bf12cd7c-THERMAL-BOUNDARY.md); independent reproduction and selection of the real mission boundary remain open. This fold does not claim that the earlier internal numerical checks closed CHECK-040's separate thermal result.
