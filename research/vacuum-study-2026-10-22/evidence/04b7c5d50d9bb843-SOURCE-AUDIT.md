# Source and qualification audit

Every numerical material transfer remains QUESTIONABLE; assembly, lifetime, ranking targets and service extensions are PROJECTION. The owner applies global labels and supplies the upstream source registers. This check supplies the following explicit mapping so those labels and source boundaries travel with the reproduced numbers.

SiC density 3100 kg/m³ and typical compression 3.9 GPa agree with the [Saint-Gobain Hexoloy SA technical sheet](https://www.ceramicsrefractories.saint-gobain.com/sites/hps-mac3-cma-pcr/files/assetbank_media/hexoloy-sa-sic-en-1013-tds-215398.pdf), page 4. That edition gives E=430 GPa and a listed Weibull modulus 10 without a matched compression distribution. The inherited 410 GPa is deliberate, as 041/SOURCES.md already states. A separate [manufacturer mining brochure](https://www.ceramicsrefractories.saint-gobain.com/de/files/4761/view) lists 410 GPa and modulus 8. These source versions are not merged into a fictitious calibrated compression population. The 1 GPa search baseline is an engineering target, not a sourced allowable.

The [Toyobo technical information](https://www.toyobo-global.com/seihin/kc/pbo/zylon-p/bussei-p/technical.pdf) supports nominal HM density 1.56 g/cm³, strength 5.8 GPa and moisture regain .6%. Its pinned 2005 PDF was hash-verified and page 9 visually inspected. The warehouse curve is approximately .96 at 1000 days for AS yarn. Transferring that reading to HM crossply and exponentiating it to ten years is a projection. The .995/year protected endpoint is a requirement. The manufacturer's current direct URL could not be fetched during this check; the matching frozen PDF and indexed manufacturer copy were used, with that access distinction retained.

The [NIST report](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=860679), pinned as chin2007.pdf but identified upstream as NISTIR 7373, records low-humidity and protected-tube experiments. Its text was read from the hash-verified frozen PDF. Those experiments do not validate the present 12 µm barrier, outdoor lifetime, service temperature distribution or 32.5 Pa target. The 5% target is a design transfer.

The sleeve scalar 1470 MPa is the inherited [Toray T700S sheet](https://www.rockwestcomposites.com/downloads/T700SDataSheet.pdf) value; 1550 kg/m³ is a composite density assumption. Adhesive shear 28.9 MPa is an aluminium-lap transfer from the [Henkel EA 9394 bulletin](https://dm.henkel-dam.com/is/content/henkel/EA-9394-Aero). The exact provenance and boundaries are in the frozen 041/SOURCES.md. Curved SiC adhesion, stiffness, peel, torsion, fatigue, gap and density are not qualified by those scalars.

Gas transmission, outgassing proxies and hardware allowances trace to frozen 034/INPUTS.json, METHOD.md and SOURCE_CATALOG.json. They distinguish coupon conditions, NASA record IDs, material release inventories, assumed permeability ratios and installation allowances. The OD3 water and oxygen coupon conditions differ, and no improvement in permeability follows from increasing optical density to OD4. The global QUESTIONABLE/PROJECTION labels remain on all such transfers.

The [Edwards E2M28 manual A37310880_X](https://global.my.edwardsvacuum.com/medias/A37310880-Instructions.pdf?attachment=true&context=bWFzdGVyfHJvb3R8MTI5MDI3NHxhcHBsaWNhdGlvbi9wZGZ8YURZd0wyaGhaaTg0TWpnNU16Y3dNVGt6T1RJek1DOUJNemN6TVRBNE9EQWdMU0JKYm5OMGNuVmpkR2x2Ym5NdWNHUm18ZDUyY2ZlZmRjMGQxNWY1NjBhNjlkNThjZTA5ZjY3MDM1NjNlN2NjY2I1YTc3NGI3MDQ1YzAwZGI5MTFmM2UwMw) was read through the browser. It supports 27.5 m³/h maximum pumping speed at 50 Hz, 44 kg approximate mass, 3 Pa fully ballasted ultimate pressure, .7 kg/h maximum water rate and 750 W mechanical motor power. The check preserves 1000 W electrical and 75% speed as planning assumptions. Installed wet-speed curves, operating temperature, oil, plumbing, heat removal and power conversion remain unqualified. No local copy of this manual is claimed.

P2 material/thermal/service sources remain in frozen 040/INPUTS.json and 048/SOURCE-AUDIT.md. A positive compared payload is not evidence of common life, probability or qualification between P2 and P3. The P2 datum is explicitly a filed endpoint selection.

All 15 owner findings and 11 inherited registers remain intact. No source qualification is closed by this numerical agreement.
