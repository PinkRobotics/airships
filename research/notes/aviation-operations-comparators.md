# Aviation operations comparators

Read on 2026-10-05. These are primary-source reading notes, not operational validation.
Only links are retained; no source document or image is redistributed.

- [NWCG Standards for Water Scooping Operations](https://fs-prod-nwcg.s3.us-gov-west-1.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/publication/pms518.pdf),
  PMS 518, February 2026, chapter 3, printed pages 1–2. Read the aircraft groups and
  circuit definition: amphibious single-engine and multi-engine scoopers already repeat
  water-source-to-target circuits. This US standard is comparator context, not BC law.
- [De Havilland's Canadair programme announcement](https://dehavilland.com/aircraft-sales/de-havilland-aircraft-of-canada-limited-launches-dhc-515-firefighter/),
  March 31, 2022. The page loaded and its programme description and backgrounder were
  read. It identifies the established CL-215 and CL-415 family and describes nearby-water
  refill for the proposed successor. Its marketing performance figures are not used.

The reviewed Be-200 maker page did not load in this reading (gateway error and timeouts).
No specification is taken from the failed opens or substituted search extracts. The opening
comparison therefore uses the readable standard and manufacturer page, without conventional
load, sortie-count or daily-output figures.
