## Target and deliverable

An evacuated hull must float without crushing at 500 m ISA. Use the ordered reference air
density 1.167 kg/m³ and survive sea-level external pressure 101325 Pa. State residual
internal pressure, geometry, scale, safety factor, payload/system allowances and the full
mass basis for every candidate; do not combine configurations or mistake a local bound for
a complete hull. No lifting gas.

Build a requirements map across sandwich monocoque, isogrid/geodesic frame plus membrane,
cellular lattice, hierarchical lattice, and pressure-graded nested cells. For each concept,
report required E, compressive strength and density (including specific properties), film
strength/allowable strain, joint efficiency and node mass fraction; today's best sourced
material; and the material gap in percent. Include HM/IM/pitch carbon, boron, beryllium,
ceramics/CMCs, metallic glasses/microlattices, CNT yarn, graphene/polymer films and aerogel
cores as appropriate. Do not substitute tensile strength for compressive capability.

Each map line says: floats at 500 m if the material reaches X; today's best is Y; gap N%.
Unknown inputs stay UNKNOWN, with their basis and sensitivity. Keep the list of what the
future investment-stage bench cell must demonstrate: crush pressure, knockdown, node mass
and leak rate. No coupon or cell construction now.

## Progress and scientific acceptance

A validated point comprises (a) a closed-form candidate or explicit extension of
[research/analysis/vacuum-cell.py](../analysis/vacuum-cell.py), (b) FEA validation of the
relevant knockdowns with mesh convergence, linear eigenvalue and nonlinear seeded
imperfections, and (c) agreement within a stated tolerance. Record the tolerance and
comparator before the run; do not select them after seeing an answer. Una verifies entry
to the validated map/scoreboard. A demonstrated FAIL can be a point and must name the next
hypothesis ranked by expected margin change.

U1 benchmarks PASS only within 5% of the sourced published comparator with demonstrated
mesh convergence. Report FAIL, UNKNOWN, NOT RUN and control stops distinctly. A converged
run is never relabelled a control stop. A genuine infrastructure failure before science
may be fixed and retried once within the existing rules; preserve both events. Exhausted
old scientific attempts do not gain retries from the redirect.

A landing is science only when it adds a validated point or changes a validated input.
Landings-per-day and message volume are not metrics.
