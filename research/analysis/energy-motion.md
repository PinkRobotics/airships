Lamb coefficient as tabulated by Munk, NACA Report 184, printed page 20 (PDF page 21): https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19930091249. The primary table gives 0.702 at length/diameter 2; 0.70 is its rounded surrogate and 1.0 remains a sensitivity assumption. No local primary-source copy is shipped. The potential-flow ellipsoid approximation does not validate the hull or its hanging-bag dynamics. Background: research/papers/hochstetler-2016-heavy-lift-airships.pdf, pages 3 and 13.
