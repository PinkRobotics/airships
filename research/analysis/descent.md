# What the prescribed descent costs

Generated by `node research/analysis/energy-descent.mjs` after the browser model regenerates `descent.json`. No aircraft has flown.

These supplied-effort comparisons do not establish delivery or savings. Every listed prescribed cycle is unsupported; the force verdict decides what the model can claim.

Rotor energy and power figures are conditional on a constant hover merit times drive efficiency at every thrust and speed, with no separate blade profile power and no specified blade, rotor-speed or pitch policy. A separated model can move these figures in either direction.
