## What changed the earlier sensitivity

The shoulder diagonals are measured at their drawn endpoints. The reserve is repriced by each purchased increment, not by a blended mass fraction.
Longeron arcs cover the complete interval between the drawn endpoints. The earlier numerical integration omitted its last subinterval.

Record: shoulder diagonals 610.940 m billed, 1307.772 m drawn; reserve 81.340464 t becomes 68.587986 t.
Favourable: shoulder diagonals 610.940 m billed, 1307.772 m drawn; reserve 0.000000 t becomes 0.000000 t.

These accounting changes do not prove the capacity of shorter rings or longer braces. An outside structures assessment must settle their load paths and sections.
