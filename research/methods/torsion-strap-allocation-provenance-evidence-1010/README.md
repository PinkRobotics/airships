The published accounting description and [open torsion question](../../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md#fittings-torsion-straps-and-retired-skin) distinguish helical straps from the outfit’s circumferential straps. This paper retains those qualitative identities without copying historical counts, areas, masses or chord values, merging the records or selecting strap geometry.

The published whole-hull accounting domain does not identify a complete installed allocation. The census places outfit outside the bare-hull mass comparison and leaves its mass unknown. The outfit symbol and undrawn helical allowance remain separate from an actual matched article, configuration, allocation, interface and installation record. The [README](../../../README.md) states that no aircraft has been built or flown; repository checks and publication signatures are not installation observations.
