# Cap grid accounting and member direction provenance

The [published cap question](../../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md#cap-members-directions-and-connections) asks what physical members, sections and connections make up the caps, whether membrane directions coincide with drawn hoops or bars, and which sections and connections carry each load. It distinguishes area accounting for the grid, drawn hoop/bar families and a separate bending-bar allowance. This paper preserves those qualitative identities without copying source numerical lengths, areas, counts or masses, calculating a comparison or selecting an answer.

The [member census](../../../docs/MEMBER-CENSUS.md) records `capGrid` with no explicit grid topology and `capGrid-allocation` with no complete drawn allocation. The separate `cap-hoops` and `cap-bars` drawn families lack station-mapped bill lines. Its smeared-area checks do not resolve station lengths, sections or connections and approve no cap design. These accounting/drawing records are distinct from actual installed physical members, directions, sections, connections and demonstrated loadpaths. The [README](../../../README.md) states that no aircraft has been built or flown; publication/checks are not physical observations. [PROVENANCE.json](PROVENANCE.json) pins unchanged assigned sources.

[EVIDENCE-MAP.json](EVIDENCE-MAP.json) retains the separate grid, hoop, bar and bending allowance records, published domain/revision and the missing matched actual member/article/configuration/inclusion, direction/section/connection/loadpath/raw-record identity, custody, calibration, uncertainty, applicability, assigned owner and adoption provenance. The published direction-coincidence question remains unanswered.
