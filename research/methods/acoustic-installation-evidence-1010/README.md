# Published noise gap and acoustic installation evidence applicability

The [verification plan](../../../docs/VERIFICATION-PLAN.md#what-is-still-missing-entirely) explicitly lists noise among missing topics. This paper maps prospective evidence for that gap. The [member census](../../../docs/MEMBER-CENSUS.md) and [prescribed power model](../../../sim/power.js) supply conceptual geometry and model diagnostics; they supply no acoustic recording, receiver exposure or qualified installation verdict. Noise remains open.

| Evidence boundary | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| Published gap | A named topic and a prospective map are not measured evidence. |
| Installed configuration | Conceptual geometry, member counts and class declarations are not a complete built installation. |
| Operating state | Prescribed rotor/bus power and conditional force closure are not acoustic emissions. |
| Receiver geometry | Camera and model frames are not microphone or exposure records. |
| Recording and calibration | Raw recordings, instrument/settings, clocks and calibration need their own provenance. |
| Background and applicability | Other sources, environment and recording conditions need matched records. |
| Exposure and criterion | Actual receiver time history and a responsible applicable criterion remain separate requirements. |
| Uncertainty and comparison | Unknown inputs and incompatible quantities cannot be silently resolved into a verdict. |

The census records drawing/bill disagreements and unknown counterparts. Rendering segments are not physical member counts; model sections are not resized or checked by their quantities. The film is a schematic drape, outfit mass is unknown, and scaled nominal models do not establish the fleet renderer's member design or payload capacity. CROSSWALK.json retains the entire existing census verbatim, including its disagreements, signs and unknowns. It must not become an acoustic installation inventory or a hardware selection.

The power source evaluates fixed choreography quasi-statically and refuses unsupported instants. Its installed-thrust surrogate is explicitly unverified and is not a blade rating. Electrical input, a rendered rotor or a conditional power/force predicate cannot be translated into acoustic emission from these records. Actual blade/drive/machinery state, mounts/enclosures and the complete installation boundary would need matched records before any acoustic comparison. Existing prescribed-cycle failure records and source limits remain unchanged. This paper does not repeat rotor sizing or load-control/failure-button tables.

A prospective acoustic record needs the exact installed article and operating state, raw acquisition record, receiver/source geometry and reference frame, instrument identity/settings, calibration and acquisition-clock provenance, background/interference/environment context and uncertainty. Quantity units, filtering, weighting, bandwidth and averaging conventions must identify what was actually recorded; this map selects none of them. A screenshot, audio clip without calibration or a model power trace cannot fill missing evidence.

Exposure provenance would separately identify the receiver or occupancy context, actual event/duty time history, recording/integration convention and gaps. Model phase time is not an observed exposure. An acceptance criterion requires a responsible source/version, intended domain and demonstrated applicability to the matched records before a decision. That criterion is currently NULL/UNKNOWN; no numerical threshold, standard, operating permission, mitigation or safety/compliance conclusion is adopted.

[CROSSWALK.json](CROSSWALK.json) records prospective custodian roles, units/reference conventions and insufficient or disconfirming evidence. Actual observations, evidence identifiers, matched configuration, receiver record, recording, calibration, exposure, uncertainty and acceptance criteria are NULL/UNKNOWN. Roles remain UNASSIGNED. [PROVENANCE.json](PROVENANCE.json) pins every local input at the baseline revision. Unknown mass, geometry, acoustic output or exposure is not zero.

This is a source-only requirements paper. It makes no acoustic level, frequency, emission, propagation or exposure calculation, chooses no hardware/isolation/mitigation, operates no machinery or model, conducts no physical observation and updates no source or remote issue. It establishes neither physical qualification nor safety/compliance.
