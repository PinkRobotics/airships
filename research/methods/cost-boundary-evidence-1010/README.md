# Published cost gap and prospective cost-record applicability

The [verification plan](../../../docs/VERIFICATION-PLAN.md) explicitly lists the cost of anything among topics not analysed. Its use of budget and priced effort also describes physical model quantities and a historical architecture boundary; it supplies no complete monetary cost. The [member census](../../../docs/MEMBER-CENSUS.md) preserves drawing/bill disagreements, unknown counterparts and excluded outfit. A conceptual bill or a rendering is not a complete, applicable priced inventory. The [root README](../../../README.md) says no aircraft has been built or flown and that a repository badge does not establish feasibility.

| Record domain | Applicability boundary |
|---|---|
| Matched scope | Original configuration, architecture, inclusions, exclusions and unknown items. |
| Acquisition and installation | Original applicable records, distinct from geometry or physical allowances. |
| Supporting infrastructure | Supplying-system scope, distinct from aircraft energy diagnostics. |
| Consumables and operation | Original consumption/cost records, distinct from prescribed physical quantities. |
| Maintenance and replacement | Original records and documented life basis, without an assumed schedule. |
| Labour and services | Applicable work records and original rate definitions, when supplied. |
| Disposal and end of life | Original scope and records, without an assumed method or residual value. |
| Accounting period | Original categories and period/currency/date consistency, without a chosen rule. |
| Provenance and criterion | Source records, uncertainty and responsible comparison criteria remain unknown. |

The census uses physical lengths, counts and tonnes, including unchanged section allowances. Its wording about tonnes pricing sections does not identify a currency or a monetary price. Rendering segments are not physical member counts, unknown mass is not zero, and the record does not approve a design. The physical bill has unmatched or unknown counterparts and its outfit is outside the bare-hull comparison. These facts remain limitations; this paper does not choose, complete or reprice a bill, and it reproduces no mass, cell or rotor tables.

The plan separately says that aircraft energy diagnostics do not establish a system shown to supply it, and that the tender fleet is deliberately unmodelled. It says tenders are named in README, although the pinned baseline README has no tender mention. This source discrepancy is retained. No supplying system or infrastructure cost can be inferred from power or daily energy. Detailed tender, energy and timebase mappings remain separate work.

Prospective cost-record evidence would need original records applicable to the exact configuration and declared inclusions/exclusions for acquisition, installation, supporting infrastructure, consumables, maintenance/replacement, labour/services and disposal. Currency, currency basis, price date, original quantity units, accounting category/period, rate and service-life definitions must come from supplied records. Unknown or incomparable records and uncertainty must remain visible before any interpretation. This is a record-provenance requirement; it supplies no monetary value, selected bill or supplier and adopts no period, rate or life.

Missing prices, rates, lives, observations, currency/date/unit/period values, assigned owners, uncertainty and adopted comparison criteria are NULL/UNKNOWN in [CROSSWALK.json](CROSSWALK.json). Prospective custodian roles remain UNASSIGNED. Historical excerpts retain the source limitations and conditional quantities as context, without a conversion or cost inference. [PROVENANCE.json](PROVENANCE.json) pins the authoritative inputs to the baseline revision.

This paper performs no pricing lookup, conversion, calculation, forecast or cost-benefit analysis, gives no procurement or financial advice, and establishes no affordability, viability, benefit or safety outcome. It selects no bill, price, rate, lifetime, supplier, accounting method or threshold. It runs no model or physical action and changes no source or remote issue. A repository check cannot qualify a complete cost record.
