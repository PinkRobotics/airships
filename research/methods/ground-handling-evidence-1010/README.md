# Ground handling and parked-state prospective evidence

Date: 2026-10-10. Status: prospective method, OPEN. The [verification plan](../../../docs/VERIFICATION-PLAN.md#what-is-still-missing-entirely) lists ground handling, mooring and the parked state as missing. Its historical evacuated-article buoyancy, wet-snow, broadside-wind and off-season quantities remain dated source context. They are not recomputed, normalized, transferred to a current article or treated as observations here. The source has no demonstrated state in which the aircraft sits still.

The [evidence contract](EVIDENCE.json) maps matched article/state, restraint and ground load path, inventories, environment, stationary response and complete ground-equipment provenance to prospective records. Every observation, evidence-record reference, measured uncertainty and physical acceptance criterion is null. GROUND identifiers reserve future records; they do not report acquired data.

This is a stationary ground boundary. Aerial dynamics, pickup/release and empty-hull strategy questions remain separate, as do material/strength and installation crosswalks. No ground anchor, support, site, operating procedure, design or criterion is selected.
