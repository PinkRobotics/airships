# Published pre-treatment-effect question and prospective outcome evidence

The [verification plan](../../../docs/VERIFICATION-PLAN.md#needs-an-expert) leaves pre-treatment usefulness and value unanswered. Its [external-evaluator questions](../../../docs/VERIFICATION-PLAN.md#what-we-would-ask-external-evaluators) separately ask whether the concept is useful, how long a water line persists and what effectiveness metric applies to a stipulated unlimited-water concept. The [root README](../../../README.md) says no aircraft has been built or flown and that an infeasible model row establishes no delivery or endurance. This paper identifies prospective evidence provenance; it establishes no effect, persistence, value or operational benefit.

| Evidence boundary | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| Published question | A conditional concept and expert prompt are not an observed effect. |
| Matched comparator | Original treatment/untreated records and their comparability must be identified. |
| Vegetation and fuel | Actual condition/history differs from water totals or geometric line labels. |
| Weather, exposure and history | Matched environmental records and other influences remain necessary provenance. |
| Timing and persistence | Observed histories differ from model phases and unlimited-supply stipulations. |
| Response and benefit | Fire-behaviour response and operational usefulness have separate evidence boundaries. |
| Measurement and criterion | Calibration, uncertainty and a responsible adopted metric/criterion remain unknown. |

The operations prompt contains historical delivery and geometric-line quantities under suitable conditions; the fire-behaviour prompt stipulates unlimited water and no crews. CROSSWALK.json retains both questions verbatim as source context. Those quantities and stipulations are not observed treatment, actual ground exposure, achieved delivery, a demonstrated fire response or a selected tactic. They supply no efficacy or value inference. Existing model failures, conditional quantities and source history remain unchanged.

A future comparison would need original matched treatment and untreated/comparator records, exact configuration and condition, vegetation/fuel state and history, weather/exposure and other influences, event timing and persistence observations, and original response records with their quantity definitions and acquisition/calibration/uncertainty provenance. Comparability and missing/confounded/censored observations must remain visible. This paper prescribes no allocation, ignition, exposure, treatment or physical procedure and chooses no experimental or causal-estimation method.

Persistence is an unanswered question about an observed history, not a number supplied by a delivery phase or assumed reapplication. Fire-behaviour response likewise cannot be inferred from model water mass, apparent wetness or geometric line length. Operational benefit is a further question with its own domain and evidence; no efficacy, coverage, economics, value or benefit calculation is made here. Detailed deposition, lake availability, supply-chain, timebase and acoustic provenance remain separate work.

An effectiveness metric and acceptance criterion need a responsible source/version, intended domain and applicability to the matched observations before any interpretation. Both are NULL/UNKNOWN. No tactic, material, duration threshold, metric, reapplication schedule or safety/benefit verdict is adopted. Unknown is not zero, and a repository check does not supply an observed fire outcome.

[CROSSWALK.json](CROSSWALK.json) records source locators, prospective custodian roles, comparison conventions and insufficient evidence. Actual observations, treatment/comparator/configuration/history/timing/response records, calibration, uncertainty and adopted metrics/criteria are NULL/UNKNOWN. Roles remain UNASSIGNED. [PROVENANCE.json](PROVENANCE.json) pins every input to the baseline revision.

This is a source-only requirements paper. It runs no simulation or physical action, makes no fire/ignition/field-deployment procedure, selects no tactic/material/metric/threshold, calculates no efficacy/value/cost/coverage and updates no source or remote issue. It establishes no physical effect or safety claim.
