# Icing, lightning and hail evidence gaps

The [verification plan's missing-evidence list](../../../docs/VERIFICATION-PLAN.md#what-is-still-missing-entirely) explicitly names icing, lightning and hail among topics absent or represented only by catch-all wording. This prospective paper preserves that published gap. The same list names other topics; gust loading, mooring, ground handling, thermal, noise and cost remain outside this paper.

[EVIDENCE-MAP.json](EVIDENCE-MAP.json) records the exact source statement, the hazard-name trace across the three assigned documents and separate missing exposure, response and criterion fields for each hazard. No applicable hazard-specific exposure, calibrated response or adopted criterion is located in that assigned source trace. Those records are explicitly absent here, with actual exposure/response and adoption still null/UNKNOWN. This scoped absence makes no claim about records or physical activity elsewhere.

Existing atmosphere and other model assumptions describe a reference or calculation context. They supply no matched icing, lightning or hail exposure. Exposure needs its own article-bound episode, time/location and state custody; observed response needs separate raw records matched to the same configuration and surface/material state. No atmosphere implementation, comparison table or calculation is repeated.

The [member census](../../../docs/MEMBER-CENSUS.md) preserves drawing/bill disagreements and unknown counterparts. Its film mesh is schematic rather than a measured membrane, and its outfit mass remains unknown. Those representation records cannot identify weather-tested hardware or establish response. The [README](../../../README.md) states that no aircraft has been built or flown and distinguishes conceptual illustrations and repository checks from physical feasibility.
