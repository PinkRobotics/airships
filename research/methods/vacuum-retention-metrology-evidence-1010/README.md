The published demonstrator/build-sequence description remains a source hypothesis and proposed evidence context; this paper does not repeat or prescribe an article design, print schedule or conditioning procedure. Pressure changes alone do not identify gas ingress, outgassing or permeation. Humidity/dry-state, temperature, sensor drift, synchronization and missing intervals must remain explicit in any later matched record.

The [member census](../../../docs/MEMBER-CENSUS.md#film-close-up-fittings-and-outfit) distinguishes schematic film and unweighed outfit from complete installed definitions. Coupon/component observations cannot silently become assembled-cell retention. Keep article identities, calibration, raw records, processing versions, uncertainty components/correlations, failed cases and environmental/configuration changes together before any duration transfer.

Adopted degradation allowance, acceptable uncertainty and physical acceptance remain UNKNOWN pending a separately recorded decision. This paper calculates no leak rate, pressure rise or lifetime, selects no seal/material/conditioning/criterion, prescribes no physical procedure or experiment and grants no scientific acceptance, source mutation or remote action.
