# Print yield and tolerance record provenance

The [verification plan's proposed printed demonstrator](../../../docs/VERIFICATION-PLAN.md#e1--what-a-sealed-vacuum-cell-actually-weighs) names print yield and tolerance among the intended observations. This paper retains that qualitative intent without copying historical nominal dimensions, counts or numeric outcomes. A proposed article does not identify an actually made complete article or supply documented dimensional inspection.

The [member census](../../../docs/MEMBER-CENSUS.md) preserves drawing/bill disagreements and limits its checks to nominal smeared-area scope. Those generated geometry records are not dimensional observations of a made article. The [README](../../../README.md) states that no aircraft has been built or flown and distinguishes conceptual illustration and repository checks from physical feasibility. [PROVENANCE.json](PROVENANCE.json) pins these unchanged assigned sources.

[EVIDENCE-MAP.json](EVIDENCE-MAP.json) records qualitative source fragments and the missing actual source/article/configuration/revision/lot/process-record identity, supplied drawing, raw dimension/defect record custody, metrology/calibration/uncertainty and applicability/adoption provenance. The assigned print-yield question supplies no source-defined numerator, denominator or included/excluded record set. Those definitions and actual yield remain null/UNKNOWN; none is invented or selected here.

Actual made-article, lot/process and dimensional/defect records, yield, tolerance, calibration/uncertainty/applicability, assigned custodian/owner and adopted criterion remain missing. Missing defect records are never zero defects, and an unknown denominator is not an accepted record set. Nominal drawing values, repository checks and publication signatures are not physical inspection observations or adopted tolerance.

This is prospective record provenance only. It gives no printing, manufacture, conditioning, inspection or test procedure, recommends no tool, material, design or process setting, and selects no tolerance, yield or defect threshold. It invents no defects, accesses no sealed source, copies no private outcome and performs no physical/native action or numeric calculation. Original cell/joint science, installation geometry, dry-mass/census comparisons, material properties, outfit, seam, barrier and vacuum-retention work remain separate.
