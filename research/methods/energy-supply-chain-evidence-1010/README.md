# Published tender-chain omission and matched replenishment evidence

The [verification plan](../../../docs/VERIFICATION-PLAN.md#what-is-still-missing-entirely) explicitly separates aircraft-only daily effort and assumed solar from an unmodelled tender fleet. The [root README](../../../README.md) establishes that no aircraft has been built or flown and that infeasible model rows establish no delivery or endurance. The [open questions](../../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md#what-would-change-our-minds) retain the intended battery-tender mechanism and its missing system evidence. This paper maps those boundaries; it demonstrates no production, transport, transfer, replenishment or sustained aircraft rate.

The verification plan says the tender fleet is named in README, but the pinned current root README contains no tender reference. OPEN-QUESTIONS still names the intended battery tender mechanism and says it is deliberately unmodelled. This source-reference discrepancy is retained, not repaired or treated as a system demonstration.

| Evidence boundary | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| Published aircraft scope | Diagnostic effort and assumed solar are not a demonstrated supplying system. |
| Production | A source/rating or lossless quotient is not usable observed upstream output. |
| Storage and custody | Capacity and charged labels do not establish usable retained inventory. |
| Transport and handoff | A named tender and mechanical-speed prose do not establish an actual compatible transfer. |
| Aircraft receipt | Nominal delivered supply is not accepted usable replenishment. |
| Complete-chain matching | Successful pieces and unmatched records do not establish the whole chain. |
| Calibration and criterion | Missing observation, uncertainty and responsible acceptance provenance remain unknown. |

The existing aircraft effort and solar comparison retains its original model basis and boundary. It must not be used as an upstream production requirement or a complete supply-chain demonstration without an explicitly matched question and inputs. This paper makes no daily or cycle calculation and repeats no storage/model performance tables. Historical failures and conditional results remain in their source files, unchanged.

The open questions preserve a legacy days-to-hours tender assertion alongside explicit lossless-recovery caveats and a deliberately unmodelled energy-import chain. That assertion is retained verbatim in CROSSWALK.json as source history, not adopted as achieved replenishment or rescue time. Plant rating and tank capacity do not supply production/retention trajectories; nominal or assumed energy is not an observed usable carrier receipt. No charging method, transport fleet, storage capacity, duty schedule or supply source is chosen.

A future chain record would identify actual configuration and operating state at production, storage, transport, transfer and receiving boundaries. It would retain article/batch/carrier identity and responsibility through each handoff, original input/output/condition records and units, losses/auxiliaries and inclusion/exclusion boundaries, rejected or partial transfers, and calibration/uncertainty/applicability evidence. Original records must match across interfaces before a comparison can be justified; this paper does not combine them or compute a balance. Supplied aircraft model values, nominal delivered inventory and accepted usable stores remain distinct quantities.

System acceptance requires a responsible criterion with source/version, intended domain and applicability to the matched chain records. Neither a single favourable model row nor successful isolated pieces can establish the complete chain or repeated availability. Unknown is not zero, and no reserve threshold, operational permission, economics conclusion, safety assertion or system-capability verdict is supplied.

[CROSSWALK.json](CROSSWALK.json) records source locators, original gap/history excerpts, prospective custodian roles, interface conventions and insufficient evidence. Actual observations, evidence IDs, matched configurations, operating state, interface records, calibration, uncertainty and adopted criteria are NULL/UNKNOWN. Roles remain UNASSIGNED. [PROVENANCE.json](PROVENANCE.json) pins each public local source at the baseline revision.

This is a source-only provenance paper. It does not implement a tender model, calculate energy/capacity/fleet/duty/cost, choose a supply or charging/transport design, run a mission, conduct a physical action or update a source or remote issue. Detailed storage sizing and mission timebase remain separate questions.
