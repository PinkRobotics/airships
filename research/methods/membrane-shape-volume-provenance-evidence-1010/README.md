# Membrane shape and enclosed-volume comparison provenance

The [Sag debit and enclosed volume](../../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md#sag-debit-and-enclosed-volume) question asks how the drawn mesh's smaller enclosure should affect the billed lift basis, and what measured membrane shape would settle its sign-change question on the same declared material and safety-factor basis. This paper retains that question. Its numerical ratios, altitude, volume and safety-factor values remain only in the unchanged sources; there is no corrected lift bill, sign-change verdict or feasibility claim here.

The [member census](../../../docs/MEMBER-CENSUS.md#film-close-up-fittings-and-outfit) calls the film mesh a schematic drape, not a measured membrane. Film is billed on projected capsule area; the executed mesh has a different area and volume. Separate representations are not additional physical copies. Source-generated geometry and bill disagreements identify a comparison boundary but supply no observed membrane shape or qualified enclosure.

[CROSSWALK.json](CROSSWALK.json) maps the exact film/ShipFilm mesh identity and the film census fields for vertices, triangles, whole/barrel/cap area, volume and billed area. Those identifiers and source locations carry no numerical field values. Vertex and triangle census identities establish no actual observed coordinates, topology, complete enclosure or physical member count. Area partitions, projected bill area and enclosed volume must not be treated as interchangeable records.

That context cannot supply an observed whole membrane shape, the actual comparison material/state, or a same-basis enclosed-volume record. This paper copies no test protocol, physical procedure or material selection from that plan.

Comparable records would need the original membrane/configuration/state and source-defined material/safety-factor basis, observed shape custody, reference conventions and correspondence, and enclosure/mesh provenance. Partial or open coverage and incomparable frames or states must remain visible. Source identity or rendering cannot silently fill a missing cap, opening, boundary or physical observation. This paper chooses no geometry, shape, material, frame, acceptance criterion or registration operation and performs no volume integration or sag/lift/mass computation.
