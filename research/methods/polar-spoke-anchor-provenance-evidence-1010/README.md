# Odd-column spoke anchors and polar station provenance

The [published source question](../../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md#odd-column-spoke-anchors-and-polar-stations) asks how diametral spokes attach in an odd-column configuration, contrasts bill/formula/drawn records and asks which polar stations are physical members and what sections, anchors and terminations belong there. This paper preserves that qualitative question without copying numerical counts, dimensions, lengths or masses, recomputing the comparisons or selecting an answer.

The [member census](../../../docs/MEMBER-CENSUS.md) describes nominal spoke diameter as inferred from a smeared bill and gives a barrel-and-cap domain above a cutoff. It preserves drawing/bill disagreements, leaves odd-column anchor and formula-length questions open, and does not resolve station lengths, sections or connections. Bill, formula and drawn representations remain distinct from actual matched station, member, article and installed connection evidence. The [README](../../../README.md) states that no aircraft has been built or flown; checks and publication signatures are not physical observations. [PROVENANCE.json](PROVENANCE.json) pins unchanged assigned sources.
