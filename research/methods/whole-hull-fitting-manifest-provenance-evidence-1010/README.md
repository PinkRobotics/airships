# Area-derived fittings and whole-hull physical-joint manifest provenance

The fitting-manifest and physical-joint questions in [Fittings, torsion straps and retired skin](../../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md#fittings-torsion-straps-and-retired-skin) ask what complete whole-hull manifest replaces area-derived clamp/pad accounting, and which physical joints support the joint allowance. This paper preserves those questions and the incomplete source records. Numerical counts, areas, masses and allowances remain solely in the unchanged sources.

The [member census](../../../docs/MEMBER-CENSUS.md) identifies clamps with a GridClamps patch-only counterpart, pads as not drawn, and tiJoints with a GridJoints patch-only counterpart that is unallocated across the whole hull. It also says clamp/joint symbols are a close-up, not a whole-hull count, and records no complete drawn allocation for pads and tiJoints. These are separate kinds of incomplete representation/accounting evidence, not a completed physical manifest.

[CROSSWALK.json](CROSSWALK.json) maps the specific declared boundaries:

| Source identity | Custody and completeness gap |
|---|---|
| clamps / GridClamps patch | Area-derived bill versus local patch, without whole-hull population. |
| pads / not drawn | Bill label without drawing; no physical absence inferred. |
| tiJoints / GridJoints patch | Source joint allowance versus unallocated partial depiction. |
| fittings | Explicit close-up limitation; no scale-up to physical joint counts. |
| pads-allocation | Missing complete drawn allocation retained separately. |
| tiJoints-allocation | Missing allowance-to-actual-joint correspondence. |
| GridClamps | Local symbol identity only; no copied or selected symbol count. |

Rendering segments are not physical member counts. Partial fitting symbols do not establish whole-hull populations. Unknown mass is never zero. A bill quantity, area proxy, joint allowance, missing drawing or local patch cannot supply actual fitting identities, installation/configuration, complete coverage or measured physical absence. No fitting census is counted, scaled, deduplicated or generated here, and no physical joint/fastener/pad/clamp/count/section/load path is selected.

Prospective evidence would require original source/domain/revision and count-definition/inclusion/exclusion conventions, explicit bill/representation/manifest correspondence and completeness boundaries, responsible complete actual manifest/configuration/installation records, unmodified raw custody, calibration, uncertainty, applicability and adoption source/version/domain. Missing or incomparable records remain visible. Record/favourable allowance labels are source accounting worlds, not adopted physical joint records.

Complete actual manifest, configuration/revision, installation, count definition, inclusion/exclusion, correspondence, raw/custody, calibration, uncertainty, applicability, matched comparison, assigned owners, adopted manifest/count and criterion remain NULL/UNKNOWN. Role names are proposed custodians only, UNASSIGNED. [PROVENANCE.json](PROVENANCE.json) pins the authoritative task inputs and baseline revision and declares fragment indexing.

StageB cell/joint installation geometry, generic outfit symbols, Q11 mass, cap/polar directions/topology, P45 cord stiffness, torsion straps, retired skin and seams remain separate. This paper concerns only area-derived fitting accounting versus complete physical-joint manifest custody. It changes no source/model/code/catalogue/schema/data/test and runs no code, generator, model, fixture or solver. No numeric copy/counting/calculation, geometry/material/design/criterion/procedure, physical action, external lookup, new measurement or scientific result is supplied.
