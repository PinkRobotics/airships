# Conceptual outfit versus complete installed interface evidence

The [member census](../../../docs/MEMBER-CENSUS.md#film-close-up-fittings-and-outfit) separates outfit from the bare-hull mass comparison and leaves its mass unknown. Film and close-up fitting representations are separate from a complete physical installation. The [attachment question](../../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md#fittings-torsion-straps-and-retired-skin) and [remaining-outfit question](../../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md#outfit-named-but-not-weighed) remain open. The [root README](../../../README.md) identifies the drawing as conceptual and says no aircraft has been built or flown. This paper maps prospective record applicability; it establishes no installed inventory, interface or completeness.

| Evidence boundary | Required record distinction |
|---|---|
| Drawing and inclusions | Original representation/configuration revision, exclusions and contextual objects. |
| Physical article identity | Applicable article, configuration, serial and revision records. |
| Attachment and interface | Original interface identities and linkage, distinct from connection symbols. |
| Support linkage | Applicable support-record identities, distinct from a schematic or support design. |
| As-installed completeness | Original record scope and missing/unmatched/excluded items kept visible. |
| Observation provenance | Original observations and applicable units, calibration and uncertainty. |
| Custody and criterion | Original record custody and responsible completeness criteria remain unknown. |

Rendering segments are not physical member counts. Fitting symbols are a close-up, not a whole-hull population, and unknown mass is never zero. The drawing/bill disagreements and missing counterparts remain source limitations; a passing census check does not resolve them or approve a design. The conceptual outfit table and remaining-equipment question are cited without copying or recomputing their counts, dimensions, masses or other figures. No conceptual family list is chosen as a complete inventory, and this paper completes no bill.

Prospective evidence would need original article/configuration/serial/revision identities, drawing and inclusion/exclusion provenance, applicable interface and support-record identities, and linkage to original as-installed completeness records. Missing, unmatched, excluded or differently revised records must remain visible. An absent record establishes neither an absent physical item nor a qualified installation. Original observation records would need their source-declared quantity definitions and applicable acquisition/calibration/uncertainty provenance; no observation or calibration is supplied here.

The equipment-attachment question also names other unresolved bill topics. Only attachment identity, record applicability and the remaining-outfit boundary are mapped here. Q11 mass, Q8 rotor/storage performance, thermal/cryo support detail, ground inventory, cost accounting and membrane seam work remain separate. This paper selects no hardware, material, support, interface design, installation, fabrication or test procedure.
