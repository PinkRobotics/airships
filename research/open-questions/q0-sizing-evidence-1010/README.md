# Question 0: sizing requirements and missing evidence

Date: 2026-10-10. Status: prospective evidence thread, PARTLY SPECIFIED. The original [Question 0](../../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md#0-the-sizing-requirement-that-ties-1-4-and-6-together) requires recovery after total rotor failure and a flat battery: rise loaded under the reference-atmosphere assumption, recharge on solar alone, produce sufficient nitrogen and land empty using ballast alone. Taking days is permitted; production and retention are unresolved. The existing [issue 1](https://github.com/PinkRobotics/airships/issues/1) tracks this question; no issue is created, changed or remotely checked here.

The original corrected sizing puts the binding ballast reference at the bottom of descent, rather than just enough to start sinking at the ceiling. The old DONE and peak-solar/no-loss sizing tables remain historical results. The current numbered register distinguishes specified capacity and lossless accounting from unproven production and retention. This thread leaves the original headings, operands, generated regions and conclusions intact. No size or recovery prediction is calculated.

The [evidence contract](EVIDENCE.json) traces each requirement to existing model fields or source anchors, prospective observations, units and reasons an observation may be inapplicable. All observations, evidence-record links and physical acceptance criteria remain null. Names beginning Q0 identify future records, not evidence already acquired.
