The [published limitations and signed screen](../../../docs/ENERGY-CLOSURE-2026-10.md) distinguish sampled prescribed acceleration and hull-only sensitivity coefficients from capsule evidence. Upward is the declared positive direction. Inertial demand must be compared with both signed authorities on the same history; buoyancy is already in the ledger and shedding downward thrust does not create upward authority. The withdrawn absolute diagnostic remains dated history because it missed that distinction. No diagnostic or force result is recomputed here.

A smooth altitude join alone does not establish a realizable trajectory. These are source applicability requirements; no controller or numerical model is constructed.
