# Question 2: force-owner observation contract

Date: 2026-10-10. Status: prospective method, OPEN. [Question 2](../../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md#2-which-measurements-would-validate-the-force-owners) asks for attainable hull downforce and drag across the stated airspeeds and a rotor thrust rating replacing the unverified hover surrogate. The existing [issue 2](https://github.com/PinkRobotics/airships/issues/2) is retained; no issue or label action occurs here.

The existing instantaneous ledger has four vertical hold-down owners: cable-carried water, downward rotors, permitted aerodynamic downforce and signed vertical drag. The local buoyancy-minus-weight surplus is their reference, not a fifth support credit. Horizontal drag is separately priced to propulsion. The [observation contract](EVIDENCE.json) names each owner and those two comparison dependencies, quantities, units, source functions and missing evidence. Every prospective observation, evidence-record link and physical acceptance threshold is null.

| Requirement or owner | Existing model reference | Observation units | Missing applicability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cable-carried water downward owner | `sim/power.js drawAt/anchorAt, owners.bagT` | N; kg water; m; s | Configured reachable bag inventory is credited quasi-statically; cable/load/controller dynamics and sample-specific measured support remain unvalidated. |
| Downward rotor owner | `sim/power.js drawAt/rotorThrustLimitT/inducedMW, owners.rotorT` | N; W; m2; m/s; kg/m3 | Installed cap is an unverified hover surrogate; downward-only authority and constant merit-times-drive-efficiency assumptions do not establish buildable thrust or flight performance. |
| Permitted aerodynamic downforce owner | `sim/power.js drawAt/splitForce, owners.aeroT` | N; m/s; kg/m3; Pa; m2; angle rad | Record basis credits no aerodynamic hold-down; favourable basis uses a capped coefficient. Neither basis supplies an attainable measured force envelope. |
| Signed vertical drag owner | `sim/power.js drawAt, owners.verticalDragT` | N; m/s signed; kg/m3; m2 | Broadside coefficient is unverified; upward-positive velocity fixes sign convention, and applying a positive-only magnitude loses the required signed owner. |
| Buoyancy minus onboard weight: ledger foundation, not another hold-down owner | `sim/physics.js ledger; sim/power.js drawAt surplusT/massT` | N; kg; m3; kg/m3; m MSL | Local-atmosphere and dry-allowance assumptions do not establish a physical hull. Buoyancy is already in surplus and must not be counted twice. |
| Horizontal force and propulsion price: outside the vertical owner sum | `sim/power.js drawAt zeroLiftDragN/inducedDragN/draw.prop` | N; W; m/s; kg/m3 | Hull drag and propulsion efficiency remain assumed; drag electrical load consumes bus headroom but is not an additional vertical support credit. |

Every record must identify the article and hardware, local atmosphere, coordinate/sign conventions, geometry, phase/time, calibration, uncertainty, synchronized electrical loads and comparison scope. A name beginning Q2 reserves an evidence record; it does not assert that measurement happened. Simultaneous observations must preserve the same surplus and bus basis. Bag support must not also be counted as onboard weight relief; buoyancy must not be added again when rotor hold-down is shed.

The model's existing computational residual and bus tolerances are copied exactly into EVIDENCE.json with their source expressions. They are numerical closure rules, not physical measurement tolerances. No published coefficient, operating margin, thrust cap or threshold is retuned, and absent physical comparison criteria remain UNKNOWN. The [closure record](../../../docs/ENERGY-CLOSURE-2026-10.md#what-this-model-leaves-out) retains conditional quasi-static results and its omitted dynamics/rotor limitations. Its [signed authority screen](../../../docs/ENERGY-CLOSURE-2026-10.md#signed-vertical-authority-screen-of-the-served-candidates) retains the withdrawn earlier diagnostic and corrected sign convention. Neither is rerun here, and no measured force or new acceptance is claimed.

The [verification plan](../../../docs/VERIFICATION-PLAN.md#the-register) identifies missing expert and experiment evidence. This method paper grants no physical experiment or simulation. Comparisons remain UNKNOWN until actual matching evidence and independently approved criteria exist.
