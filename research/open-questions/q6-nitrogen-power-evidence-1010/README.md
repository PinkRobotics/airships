The local source function `regenMW` separately limits requested round-trip work, the declared state-specific per-tonne ceiling and generator power. Increasing the assumed production energy does not raise that declared recoverable-work ceiling. This describes the existing code, not a new energy calculation. The source comment cites a pure-nitrogen process comparator and explicitly withholds measured airborne or universal applicability. This unit reads that public comment and preserves its provenance; it does not independently validate the cited paper or substitute a new reference state.

Actual evidence must match the same component and installation, feed/product states, flow history, electrical/thermal boundaries and phase timing. Record calibration, uncertainty and failures as well as successful observations. Gross recovery and auxiliary consumption must remain separate before any future net-output disposition. Actual thermal supply cannot be silently free. Mass inclusions must cover the same machinery and support boundary as the power comparison.

The [verification plan](../../../docs/VERIFICATION-PLAN.md#what-we-would-ask-external-evaluators) asks for complete cryogenic equipment and cooling records; its ground hardware comparators do not establish an airborne expander. The [closure record](../../../docs/ENERGY-CLOSURE-2026-10.md#what-this-model-leaves-out) retains the conditional quasi-static basis and missing dynamics. Existing generated phase tables remain as published model output; this thread asserts no achievable power or recovery fraction. Missing physical criteria, installed mass and bus/thermal evidence stay UNKNOWN.
