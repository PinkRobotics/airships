The current [signed-unheld phase table](../../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md#where-the-prescribed-hull-is-unheld) is retained unchanged: positive residual denotes unsupported surplus lift; negative residual requires unavailable upward authority. These are conditional model requirements on named controls, phases and atmosphere, rather than observed acceleration, trajectory or outcome. No table, trajectory, mass or power result is regenerated.

Records for different strategies must not exchange configuration or actuator assumptions. State initial water/nitrogen/battery inventories, local atmosphere, mass/displacement basis, matched failure identity, time and phase, control/actuator response, cable/bag state and synchronized bus constraints. A commanded response is not an observed response. Keep uncertainty, calibration, raw records and missing intervals, including failures and adverse conditions.

The variable-displacement route's sealed-cell incompatibility and the [air-ballast retraction](../../analysis/air-ballast.md) stand. Future evidence cannot silently reopen that design decision. A two-way-rotor sketch likewise cannot become a property of classes declared non-reversible. Installed component and support inclusions must match the strategy and the power/force boundary; no new mass estimate is supplied.
