Date: 2026-10-10. Status: prospective method, OPEN. [Question 12](../../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md#12-the-drop-may-not-reach-the-ground-and-tonnes-may-be-the-wrong-metric) distinguishes released tank tonnage from water arriving at the target and measured fire effect. Tank mass change and outlet discharge require distinct boundaries: leaks, residual inventory and other transfers must not silently become target delivery. Target deposition requires a georeferenced pattern with the same release/time, collector calibration, missing-data flags and uncertainty. A model line inside a perimeter is a geometry predicate; it supplies no evidence of where water lands. The existing fixed-drop and ideal-disc computations are not rerun here.

Any future effectiveness disposition must state its metric, evaluation interval and fire/ground-engagement context separately from mass or coverage. A literature association cannot identify a causal effect for this vehicle. Outcome definitions, acceptable pattern, uncertainty limits and physical criteria remain UNKNOWN; this paper selects none. Prospective records must preserve failed releases, missing samples and unfavourable conditions rather than only favourable observations.
