# Energy closure — 2026-10-01

Generated by `node research/analysis/energy-close.mjs` from the one model. Balanced mode, still air. No vehicle flew.

**INFEASIBLE means the drawn cycle cannot be flown under these assumptions. Its energy and battery-hours quotient price only supplied effort along that unsupported profile; they do not establish delivery or endurance.**

Record basis permits the bag and rotors, with signed vertical drag. Favourable basis also permits minimum-power aerodynamic hold-down, using unverified C_L,max = 1, capsule planform area, span equal to hull diameter and e = 1. The same momentum law, bus, geometry and inventory apply to both.

The existing class record has no independent blade thrust rating. The model uses hover thrust at its existing battery-plus-generator rating as an **unverified installed-thrust surrogate**, separate from instantaneous bus availability. Broadside vertical C_D = 1 is also unverified. Force closure here is quasi-static along the prescribed trajectory; acceleration and the opposite-flow rotor regime are not validated.
