# Float ledger

**Nothing floats today as drawn.** These are computed masses and load cases, not flight or physical test results. Negative margin means a mass deficit before adding unpriced payload and equipment. A positive formula or allowance is not a floating vehicle.

Headline comparison: sea level (1.2250 kg/m³) and the working altitude 2,500 m (0.9569 kg/m³). **Every model structure is sized for full vacuum against sea-level pressure, 101,325 Pa. Altitude changes lift only.** The pressure-schedule question below is explicitly not computed.
